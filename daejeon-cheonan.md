# 🚉 Daejeon / Cheonan — The Flex Cities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Both cities are 35–60 minutes from Seoul by KTX, and both sit right on the Seoul–Busan rail line — a perfect 1–2 night detour. This file works whether you pick **Daejeon**, **Cheonan**, or a day trip to each.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Sojaedong hanok cafes, Sungsimdang bakery, Expo Science Park, and Cheonan Independence Hall.

- Seoul → Daejeon: KTX ≈ **50 min**
- Seoul → Cheonan: KTX ≈ **35–45 min**
- Daejeon → Cheonan: ≈ 30–40 min by KTX/ITX or bus
- Book trains: [letskorail.com](https://www.letskorail.com) (official KORAIL)

## 11) 🔬 National Science Museum (국립중앙과학관, Daejeon) — 🔁 always on
- **What:** Korea's national science museum — huge hands-on exhibits, planetarium, and special exhibitions; Daejeon's science-city icon (built around Expo 1993)
- **Hours:** ~09:30–17:30 (closed Mondays; seasonal hours — confirm on official site)
- **Price:** Main exhibits free/low-cost; planetarium and special shows paid
- **Official source:** [science.go.kr](https://www.science.go.kr)

- **Yuseong Hot Springs district** (온천) — Korea's classic hot-spring town, 20 min from downtown Daejeon; a soak + a Volleyball/Sale-FESTA day is the classic local combo ([daejeontour.co.kr](https://daejeontour.co.kr)).

## 12) 🗼 Hanbit Tower & Expo Science Park (한빛탑, Daejeon) — 🔁 always on
- **What:** The 93-meter "Tower of Great Light" built for the 1993 Daejeon World Expo. Houses a circular 2nd-floor 360-degree observatory overlooking Expo Science Park, Gapcheon river, and the Daedeok Innopolis research cluster
- **Hours:** Observatory **09:30–17:40** (exhibitions/stores open until ~21:30); **Closed Mondays**
- **Price:** Park grounds free; Hanbit Tower Observatory **₩2,500 adult** (~$1.80) / ₩2,000 youth / ₩1,500 child
- **Official sources:** [djto.kr](https://www.djto.kr) (Daejeon Tourism Org) · [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr) · 480 Daedeok-daero, Yuseong-gu
- **Beginner notes:** Hanbit Tower Plaza is the anchor site for both the **Wine EXPO (#5)** and **Noodle Festival (#3)**! In the evening, the plaza hosts a free **Media Facade light show** projected on the tower and a **Musical Fountain** show.

## 13) 🎨 The Art Space 193 (Shinsegae Daejeon Expo Tower Observatory) — 🔁 always on
- **What:** 193-meter observatory located on floors 38–39 of the Daejeon Shinsegae Art & Science Complex tower (Expo Tower). Integrates panoramic city views of Daejeon with high-altitude contemporary art installations, featuring Icelandic-Danish artist Ólafur Elíasson's *"The Living Observatory"* media art project
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu **10:30–20:00** · Fri–Sun & holidays **10:30–20:30**
- **Price:** Adults (13+) **₩15,000** (~$11) / Children (3–12) ₩12,000
- **Official source:** [shinsegae.com](https://www.shinsegae.com) · 1 Expo-ro, Yuseong-gu (connected to Shinsegae Dept Store & Lotte City Hotel)
- **Beginner notes:** The highest vantage point in Daejeon. The surrounding Shinsegae complex also houses the Shinsegae Nexperium robotics science museum and Daejeon Expo Aquarium (media art aquarium).

## 14) 🌌 Daejeon Astronomical Observatory & KAIST Campus — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **Daejeon Astronomical Observatory (대전시민천문대):** Korea's first public astronomical observatory (213-48 Gwahak-ro, Yuseong-gu). Features a 10-inch refracting telescope for night stargazing, planetarium dome, and constellation lectures. Hours: **14:00–22:00** (last entry 21:50; closed Mondays and days after public holidays). **Admission: FREE** · Official site: [djstar.kr](https://djstar.kr)
- **KAIST Campus Tour (카이스트):** Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology, Korea's top science university campus in Yuseong. Walk freely through the campus grounds, visit Ounji Pond (famous for its campus ducks and autumn maple trees), and see the Academic Cultural Complex. Free entry.
- **Beginner notes:** Stargazing is weather-permitting; call or check djstar.kr before going. Pair a sunset KAIST campus stroll with evening telescope viewing at the observatory.

## 15) ☕ Sojaedong Cafe Street & Sungsimdang Culture Space — 🔁 daily (Daejeon cafe culture)
- **Sojaedong Cafe Street (소제동 카페거리):** Located directly behind Daejeon Station (East Exit / Daedongcheon stream, Dong-gu). Historic 1920s Japanese railway worker housing district transformed into Daejeon's premier hanok cafe district — features bamboo garden teahouses (*Pungryu* 풍류), retro dessert houses, and art galleries. Operating hours: typically **11:30–22:00 daily**.
- **Sungsimdang Cake Boutique & Culture Space (성심당 케익부띠끄 / 성심당 문화원):** Located on Eunhang-dong main street, 5 minutes from Daejeon Station West Exit. While the main bakery handles the iconic fried soboro, *Cake Boutique* specializes in European-style pastries and rolls, while *Sungsimdang Culture Space* next door offers a multi-story cafe lounge, vintage bakery artifact museum, and local merchandise.
- **Beginner notes:** Sojaedong is just 3 minutes walk from Daejeon KTX East Exit — ideal for an immediate coffee/tea stop upon landing in Daejeon before taking the subway to Yuseong hot springs or Expo Park.

## 16) 🌳 Hanbat Arboretum (한밭수목원, Daejeon) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's largest urban artificial arboretum, connecting Daejeon Government Complex and Expo Science Park across 387,000 m². Features the West Garden (forest, oak trail), East Garden (rose garden, magnolia grove, lotus pond), and a glass Tropical Greenhouse
- **Hours:** West Garden **06:00–21:00** (closed Tue); East Garden **06:00–21:00** (closed Mon); Tropical Greenhouse **09:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr/huw](https://www.daejeon.go.kr/huw) · Dunsan-dong, Seo-gu (near Hanbit Tower & DCC)
- **Beginner notes:** Autumn foliage peaks in late Oct – early Nov with vibrant red maples and golden ginkgos. Directly adjacent to Daejeon Expo Plaza and Hanbit Tower (#12).

## 17) 🍁 Gyeryongsan National Park & Donghaksa Temple (동학사) — 🔁 always on
- **What:** Sacred mountain national park on the outskirts of Daejeon, legendary across Korea for its autumnal maple tree valleys, mountain ridge hikes, and 1,300-year-old Donghaksa Temple (est. 713 AD, one of Korea's premier Buddhist nunneries)
- **Hours:** Park grounds **open 24/7**; Temple grounds daytime
- **Price:** Park & Temple entry **FREE**
- **Official sources:** [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr) · [donghaksa.or.kr](http://www.donghaksa.or.kr) · Bus 107 from Daejeon Subway Line 1 Yuseong Spa Station (~30 min)
- **Beginner notes:** The gentle 2 km shaded valley stream walk from the bus terminal up to Donghaksa Temple is suitable for all fitness levels. Lined with traditional mountain restaurants serving wild vegetable bibimbap and pajeon.

## 20) 🛕 Gakwonsa Temple & Giant Bronze Buddha (각원사, Cheonan) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Major Buddhist temple set on the slopes of Mt. Taejosan in Cheonan (est. 1977 for Korean peninsula reunification). Houses one of Asia's largest outdoor seated bronze Buddha statues (*Amita-bul*, 15m tall, 60 tons) and Korea's largest wooden prayer hall (*Daeungbojeon*)
- **Hours:** Open **24/7** daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gakwonsa.or.kr](http://www.gakwonsa.or.kr) · [tour.chungnam.go.kr](https://tour.chungnam.go.kr) · Bus 24 from Cheonan Station
- **Beginner notes:** Autumn foliage frames the giant bronze Buddha on the mountain slope. Tranquil, scenic, and deeply impressive scale.

## 21) 🖼️ Arario Gallery Cheonan & Small City Sculpture Park (아라리오갤러리) — 🔁 always on
- **What:** World-renowned private contemporary art gallery and public urban sculpture plaza at Cheonan Bus Terminal. Features original outdoor sculptures by international icons including Keith Haring (*Julia*), Damien Hirst (*Hymn*), Subodh Gupta, and Arman

- **Beginner notes:** A surprising world-class art hub right outside Cheonan station. You can view Damien Hirst's 6-meter *Hymn* sculpture outdoors for free any time of day.

## 88) ⚛️ Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute Public Hall — 🔁 weekdays (FREE)
- **What:** The public exhibition hall of Korea's nuclear research institute, covering reactor technology and radiation science
- **Hours:** Weekdays by reservation
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [kaeri.re.kr](https://www.kaeri.re.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Reservation required. Of interest if you want to understand how Korea became a nuclear-reactor exporter.

## 89) 🌐 Expo Science Park & Hanbit Tower (엑스포과학공원·한빛탑) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The site of the **1993 Daejeon Expo**, still the city's landmark — the 93 m Hanbit Tower observatory, the Expo Bridge and the riverside plaza

- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)

## 90) 🎡 Daejeon Sky Road & Expo Bridge Night Lighting — 🔁 nightly (FREE)
- **What:** The Expo Bridge and Hanbit Tower are illuminated after dark, and the Gapcheon riverside path connects them into an easy night walk
- **Hours:** Lighting roughly **sunset–23:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)

## 91) 🖼️ Daejeon Museum of Art (대전시립미술관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The city's main contemporary art museum, next to the Expo park — strong on media art, reflecting the science-city identity
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩1,000**; often free for special city programmes
- **Official source:** [dma.daejeon.go.kr](https://dma.daejeon.go.kr)

## 92) 🖌️ Lee Ungno Museum (이응노미술관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** A museum dedicated to **Lee Ungno (1904–89)**, the Korean ink painter who reinvented calligraphy as abstract art in Paris — in a building by Laurent Beaudouin
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩500**
- **Official source:** [leeungnomuseum.or.kr](https://www.leeungnomuseum.or.kr)

## 93) 🏛️ Daejeon Museum of History (대전시립박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The city history museum — Daejeon from Baekje-era settlement to railway town to planned science capital
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [museum.daejeon.go.kr](https://museum.daejeon.go.kr)

## 94) 🏺 Daejeon Sunsa Archaeological Museum & Dunsan Prehistoric Site — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)

- **What:** One of Korea's leading regional orchestras, with monthly subscription concerts at the Daejeon Arts Center

## 97) 🎪 Daejeon Yesulga's House & Daejeon Culture & Arts Complex — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Price:** Mostly **FREE**
- **Official source:** [dcac.or.kr](https://www.dcac.or.kr) · [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)

## 98) 📚 Daejeon Hanbat Library & Book Street — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Hours:** Library Tue–Sun **09:00–20:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**

## 99) 🌳 Hanbat Arboretum (한밭수목원) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** **Korea's largest inner-city artificial arboretum** — east and west gardens either side of the Expo park, with 1,700+ species and a tropical greenhouse
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (Nov; closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daejeon Line 1 Government Complex Station. Free, huge, and **peak foliage is the first half of November**.

## 100) 🍁 Gyejoksan Red Clay Barefoot Trail (계족산 황톳길) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 14.5 km mountain ridge trail surfaced with **soft red clay for barefoot walking** — Daejeon's most distinctive attraction, and free
- **Hours:** Always open; best in daylight
- **Price:** **FREE**; foot-wash stations provided
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr) · [Daedeok-gu](https://www.daedeok.go.kr)

## 101) ⛰️ Bomunsan Mountain & Observatory (보문산) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The wooded 457 m mountain on Daejeon's southern edge with an observation tower, a sculpture park and easy family trails
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daejeon](https://www.djjunggu.go.kr)

## 102) 🏞️ Daecheong Lake & Daecheongho Osolgil Trail (대청호 오솔길) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's third-largest reservoir, on Daejeon's eastern edge — a network of lakeside walking courses, plus the Daecheong Dam observation deck
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr) · [K-water](https://www.kwater.or.kr)

## 103) 🌲 Jangtaesan Recreational Forest (장태산자연휴양림) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Korea's best **metasequoia** forest — a canopy skywalk 20 m up through the redwoods, spectacular when the needles turn rust-orange in November
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov)
- **Price:** Adult **₩2,000**; parking ₩3,000
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 22 from Daejeon Station (~50 min). **Metasequoia colour peaks in early-to-mid November** — this is the single most photogenic thing near Daejeon in your window.

## 104) 🦌 Daejeon O-World & Zooland (대전오월드) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The city's combined zoo, amusement park and flower garden on the slopes of Bomunsan
- **Hours:** Daily roughly **09:30–18:00** (Nov)
- **Price:** Combined ticket adult **₩17,000**; zoo only ₩12,000
- **Official source:** [oworld.kr](https://www.oworld.kr)

## 105) 🌸 Ppuri Park & Korean Clan Origins (뿌리공원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A park of 240+ sculptures, one for each Korean family clan and its origin story — a genuinely unique idea, plus the Family Name Museum
- **Hours:** Park always open; museum Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daejeon](https://www.djjunggu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daejeon Station. If you know any Korean's surname and clan seat, you can find their stone here.

## 106) 🌊 Gapcheon Riverside Path & Autumn Reeds (갑천) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The river running through central Daejeon — a flat cycling and walking corridor with reed beds, and the Expo Bridge at its centre

- **Price:** **FREE**; Tashu bike share from ₩1,000

## 107) ♨️ Yuseong Hot Springs & Foot Bath Park (유성온천) — 🔁 daily (FREE foot bath)
- **What:** Korea's best-known alkaline hot-spring district, in use for over 600 years — with a **free public outdoor foot bath** in the town centre
- **Hours:** Foot bath daily roughly **09:00–18:00**; bathhouses vary
- **Price:** Foot bath **FREE**; bathhouse entry ₩8,000–₩15,000
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr) · [Yuseong-gu](https://www.yuseong.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daejeon Line 1 Yuseong Spa Station. **A free hot foot bath on a cold November afternoon** is one of the best things in this file.

## 108) ⛰️ Gyeryongsan National Park — Donghaksa & Gapsa (계룡산국립공원) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** One of Korea's most sacred mountains, straddling Daejeon and Gongju — with the historic nun temple **Donghaksa** and the ancient **Gapsa**
- **Hours:** Park always open; temples roughly **08:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Park **FREE**; temple entry ~₩3,000
- **Official source:** [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr) · [Korea National Park Service](https://english.knps.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 107 from Yuseong to Donghaksa. **The Donghaksa valley is one of the top autumn-foliage walks in central Korea**, peaking late October to early November.

## 109) 🧘 Gapsa Temple & Templestay, Gyeryongsan (갑사) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A 5th-century temple on the western slope of Gyeryongsan, reached through a famous maple-lined approach path — with an English templestay programme
- **Hours:** Daily **08:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Entry **₩3,000**; templestay from ₩70,000
- **Official source:** [templestay.com](https://www.templestay.com) · [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr)

## 110) 🥐 Sungsimdang Main Bakery & Twigim Soboro (성심당) — 🔁 daily

## 112) 🍖 Daejeon Dujeong-dong & Jungang Market Food Alleys — 🔁 daily

## 113) 🍢 Daejeon Eunhaeng-dong & Sky Road Downtown — 🔁 daily (FREE)

## 122) 🥜 Cheonan Hodu-gwaja (Walnut Cake) Origin Shops (천안 호두과자) — 🔁 daily

## 123) 🍲 Byeongcheon Sundae Street (병천순대거리) — 🔁 daily

## 124) 🛕 Gakwonsa Temple & Great Bronze Buddha (각원사) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [cheonan.go.kr](https://www.cheonan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Cheonan Station. Built in 1977 as a prayer for reunification. The view back over Cheonan from the top is excellent.

## 125) 🖼️ Arario Museum & Arario Gallery Cheonan (아라리오) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Cheonan's internationally known contemporary art complex at the bus terminal — with major outdoor sculptures by Damien Hirst, Keith Haring and Arman on the public plaza
- **Hours:** Gallery Tue–Sun **11:00–19:00** (closed Mondays); plaza always open

## 126) 🏛️ Cheonan Museum & Cheonan City Hall Sky Lounge — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The city museum, plus the free 47th-floor observatory in the Cheonan city hall tower with a panorama over the Chungcheong plain
- **Hours:** Both Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [cheonan.go.kr](https://www.cheonan.go.kr)

## 127) ✉️ Postal Museum of Korea (우정박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

## 128) 🌳 Taejosan Country Park & Cheonan Foliage (태조산공원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

## 130) 🏯 Oeam Folk Village, Asan (외암민속마을) — 🔁 daily

## 131) 🗡️ Hyeonchungsa Shrine, Asan (현충사) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

## 132) ♨️ Onyang Hot Springs, Asan (온양온천) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** **Korea's oldest recorded hot spring**, used by Joseon kings for over 600 years — public bathhouses and a free foot bath in the town centre
- **Hours:** Bathhouses roughly **05:00–20:00**
- **Price:** Bathhouse **₩8,000–₩12,000**; foot bath FREE
- **Official source:** [asan.go.kr](https://www.asan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Line 1 / Janghang Line Onyang Oncheon Station. King Sejong came here for his eye condition — the tradition is documented back to 1441.

## 133) 💦 Asan Spavis & Paradise Spa Dogo — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Large hot-spring water parks in the Asan spa belt — indoor and outdoor thermal pools, open through winter
- **Hours:** Roughly **09:00–19:00**
- **Price:** Day ticket **₩25,000–₩40,000**
- **Official source:** [spavis.co.kr](https://www.spavis.co.kr) · [asan.go.kr](https://www.asan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Outdoor hot pools in November air are the reason to go. Bring your own towel or pay for rental.

## 134) 🌊 Sapgyoho Tourist Area & Asan Bay Sunset — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The estuary lake and sea-wall park on Asan Bay — a sculpture park, a security museum with a decommissioned aircraft, and a wide west-coast sunset
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [asan.go.kr](https://www.asan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Onyang. **Sunset around 17:20 in November** over the bay — cold, wide and empty.

## 135) 🌾 Gongju — Gongsanseong Fortress, UNESCO Baekje (공산성) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The hilltop Baekje royal fortress at Gongju, part of the **UNESCO Baekje Historic Areas** — a 2.6 km rampart walk above the Geum River
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩1,200**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1477) · [gongju.go.kr](https://www.gongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daejeon (~50 min). The rampart circuit takes about an hour and the river view is excellent in autumn.

## 136) 👑 Gongju — Tomb of King Muryeong & Songsan-ri Tombs — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The **only Baekje royal tomb ever found intact** (discovered 1971), with its 4,600 grave goods — part of the UNESCO Baekje listing, next to the national museum
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩1,500**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1477) · [gongju.museum.go.kr](https://gongju.museum.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The tomb itself is sealed; you view a full-scale replica, and the real gold crown ornaments are in the free national museum next door.

## 137) 🏛️ Gongju National Museum (국립공주박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Home of the **King Muryeong tomb treasures** — the gold crown ornaments, the stone guardian animal and the epitaph tablets that dated the tomb exactly
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gongju.museum.go.kr](https://gongju.museum.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Next to the tombs. National-treasure-grade Baekje gold, free, an hour from Daejeon.

## 138) 🏯 Buyeo — Busosanseong & Baekje Historic Areas — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The last Baekje capital — the Busosanseong fortress, the Nakhwaam cliff, Goransa temple and a boat on the Baengma River, all UNESCO-listed
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩2,000**; river boat ₩7,000
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1477) · [buyeo.go.kr](https://www.buyeo.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daejeon (~1 h 20). The legend of the 3,000 court ladies leaping from Nakhwaam is Korea's most famous tragic story.

## 139) 🏛️ Buyeo National Museum & Jeongnimsa Pagoda — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The Baekje Gilt-bronze Incense Burner — **National Treasure No. 287 and arguably the finest object in Korean archaeology** — plus the 7th-century Jeongnimsa stone pagoda
- **Hours:** Museum Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays); pagoda site daily
- **Price:** Museum **FREE**; Jeongnimsa site ₩1,500
- **Official source:** [buyeo.museum.go.kr](https://buyeo.museum.go.kr) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1477)
- **Beginner notes:** The incense burner alone justifies the trip from Daejeon. Free entry.

## 140) 🏛️ Sejong City — Government Complex Rooftop Park (정부세종청사 옥상정원) — 🔁 weekdays
- **What:** The world's **longest rooftop garden** (3.6 km) linking the buildings of Korea's administrative capital, open to the public by reservation

- **Price:** **FREE** with advance reservation
- **Official source:** [sejong.go.kr](https://www.sejong.go.kr) · [mois.go.kr](https://www.mois.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 40 min by bus from Daejeon. **Reservation required** — apply online a few days ahead. A Guinness-record garden, free.

## 141) 📖 Sejong National Library & Presidential Archives — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The dramatic wave-roofed national library branch in Sejong, plus the Presidential Archives museum on Korea's post-1948 presidencies
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [sejong.nl.go.kr](https://sejong.nl.go.kr) · [pa.go.kr](https://www.pa.go.kr)

## 142) 🌾 Sejong Lake Park & Geumgang Reed Fields — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's largest artificial lake park, with a floating stage, a 4.8 km loop and extensive Geum River reed beds that turn silver in November
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [sejong.go.kr](https://www.sejong.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daejeon (~40 min). Flat, wide and good for cycling; bike hire on site.

## 143) 🌉 Sejong Geumgang Bridges & Ihaseung Night Lighting — 🔁 nightly (FREE)
- **What:** The Geum River bridges in Sejong — including the double-deck pedestrian **Ipo Bridge** — are lit after dark over the water
- **Hours:** Lighting roughly **sunset–23:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [sejong.go.kr](https://www.sejong.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The pedestrian deck below the road deck is a genuinely good night walk.

## 144) 🌿 National Sejong Arboretum (국립세종수목원) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Korea's first urban national arboretum — with a large glass conservatory containing Mediterranean and tropical zones, plus a Korean traditional garden
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00; closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩5,000**
- **Official source:** [sjna.or.kr](https://www.sjna.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daejeon. The glasshouse makes this a good bad-weather option; the traditional garden peaks in November colour.

## 145) 🚄 Osong KTX Junction & Cheongju Access — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Osong is where Korea's KTX network splits toward Busan and Gwangju — the gateway station for Cheongju and the Sejong area
- **Hours:** Trains roughly **05:30–23:00**
- **Price:** Seoul→Osong KTX from **₩18,500**
- **Official source:** [letskorail.com](https://www.letskorail.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Useful if you want to reach Sejong or Cheongju fast from Seoul without going via Daejeon.

## 146) 🏛️ Cheongju Early Printing Museum & Jikji (청주고인쇄박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The site of Heungdeoksa temple, where **Jikji — the world's oldest surviving book printed with movable metal type (1377)** — was made, 78 years before Gutenberg
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [jikjiworld.cheongju.go.kr](https://jikjiworld.cheongju.go.kr) · [UNESCO Memory of the World](https://www.unesco.org)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Cheongju or Osong. The original Jikji is in Paris, but this is where it was printed — a UNESCO Memory of the World item.

## 147) 🎨 Cheongju Museum of Art & Culture Factory (청주시립미술관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Cheongju's art museum plus the **Cultural Factory**, a former tobacco plant converted into a huge contemporary art and craft complex
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–19:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [cmoa.cheongju.go.kr](https://cmoa.cheongju.go.kr) · [cheongju.go.kr](https://www.cheongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Cheongju is 40 min from Daejeon. The Culture Factory is one of Korea's better industrial-conversion art spaces.

## 148) 🏯 Sangdangsanseong Fortress, Cheongju (상당산성) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 4.2 km Joseon mountain fortress wall above Cheongju — a complete circuit walk with gates, a reservoir and city views
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [cheongju.go.kr](https://www.cheongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Cheongju. About two hours for the full wall circuit; the restaurants at the main gate do good country food.

## 149) 🌳 Cheongnamdae Presidential Villa (청남대) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The former **presidential summer retreat** on Daecheong Lake, secret until 2003 and now a public park — the residence, a presidential path and lake gardens
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00; closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩5,000**
- **Official source:** [chnam.chungbuk.go.kr](https://chnam.chungbuk.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Shuttle bus from Cheongju — **private cars are not allowed in**, so you must use the official shuttle. Autumn colour on the lake is superb.

## 150) ⛰️ Songnisan National Park & Beopjusa (속리산·법주사) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** One of Korea's great mountain temples — **Beopjusa** holds a 33 m gilt-bronze Maitreya and Korea's only surviving five-storey wooden pagoda, a UNESCO Sansa site
- **Hours:** Park always open; temple **08:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Temple entry **₩4,000**
- **Official source:** [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr) · [UNESCO Sansa listing](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1562)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daejeon (~1 h 40). The Palsangjeon wooden pagoda (1626) is a National Treasure and the only one of its kind left in Korea.

## 151) 🥾 Daejeon Dulle-gil Trail Network (대전둘레산길) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 133 km waymarked circuit of the mountains ringing Daejeon, in 12 numbered sections — the city's answer to the Seoul Dulle-gil
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Section maps on the city site. Sections 2 and 3 (Bomunsan, Gyejoksan) are the most scenic in autumn.

## 152) 🦇 Donggwangdong & Daejeon Traditional Herbal Medicine Market — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The herbal medicine street near Daejeon Station — dried roots, ginseng, deer antler and traditional pharmacies, with the smell to match
- **Hours:** Roughly **09:00–19:00** (many closed Sundays)

## 153) 🌌 Daejeon Sunset & Night View from Sikjangsan (식장산) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
