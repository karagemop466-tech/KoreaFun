# 🚉 Daejeon / Cheonan — verified events and activities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Daejeon and Cheonan sit on the Seoul–Busan KTX line (about 50 and 35–45 minutes from Seoul). This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** under the [manual verification protocol](audit/VERIFICATION-PROTOCOL.md). The former 210-entry version repeated the same bakeries, temples, and parks, then padded the count with half of Chungcheong Province.

> **How to read this file**
> - ✅ **Confirmed event** — organizer page has 2026 dates in the trip window.
> - 🔎 **Verified place/activity** — official source supports the named place; hours and prices can change.
> - ⏳ **Re-check** — real program whose November timetable is unpublished.
> - Asan is next door to Cheonan and is labeled. Gongju / Buyeo are **day trips**, not Daejeon neighborhoods.

**Official planning links:** [Daejeon Tourism](https://daejeontour.co.kr) · [djwinefair.com](https://www.djwinefair.com/eng) · [Cheonan / Chungnam tourism](https://tour.chungnam.go.kr) · [KORAIL](https://www.letskorail.com)

## Daejeon

### 9) 🥐 **Sungsimdang** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Daejeon’s landmark bakery (est. 1956). The fried soboro is the signature; queues are real. Daejeon-only on purpose.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [sungsimdang.co.kr](http://www.sungsimdang.co.kr)

### 10) ☕ **Sojaedong café streets** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Former railway-worker housing east of Daejeon Station, now a hanok/café district. Public streets are free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [daejeontour.co.kr](https://daejeontour.co.kr) · [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)

### 11) 🗼 **Hanbit Tower and Expo Science Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** 1993 Expo grounds, tower observatory, Expo Bridge, and the plaza used by the wine expo. Park grounds are free; tower hours and Monday closure should be checked.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [djto.kr](https://www.djto.kr) · [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)

### 12) 🔬 **National Science Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL MUSEUM
- **What:** National hands-on science museum next to the Expo grounds. Closed Monday; planetarium is separate.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [science.go.kr](https://www.science.go.kr)

### 13) 🌳 **Hanbat Arboretum** — 🔎 VERIFIED PARK
- **What:** Large urban arboretum beside the government complex. West and East gardens have different closed days.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [daejeon.go.kr/huw](https://www.daejeon.go.kr/huw)

### 14) 🎨 **Daejeon Museum of Art and Lee Ungno Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUMS
- **What:** Municipal contemporary museum plus the dedicated Lee Ungno building in the arboretum. Closed Monday.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [daejeon.go.kr/dma](https://www.daejeon.go.kr/dma) · [leeungnomuseum.daejeon.go.kr](https://leeungnomuseum.daejeon.go.kr)

### 15) 🏙️ **Skyroad LED canopy (Eunhaeng-dong)** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Media-art street canopy on the old downtown shopping street near Sungsimdang. Show hours are evening and not daily.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [skyroad.or.kr](https://www.skyroad.or.kr)

### 16) ♨️ **Yuseong Hot Springs and public foot bath** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Historic alkaline spring district. The outdoor foot-bath park is the free, easy stop; indoor bathhouses are separate businesses.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [yuseong.go.kr](https://www.yuseong.go.kr)

### 17) 🌌 **Daejeon Astronomical Observatory** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Public observatory in Yuseong. City listing has described free admission and evening telescope time; weather and Monday closures apply.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [djstar.kr](https://djstar.kr)

### 18) 🎓 **KAIST campus walk** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Open science-university campus (Ounji pond, autumn trees). Cafeterias and buildings have their own rules.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [kaist.ac.kr](https://www.kaist.ac.kr)

### 19) 🍁 **Gyeryongsan National Park — Donghaksa** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL PARK
- **What:** Maple valley and historic nunnery on Daejeon’s western edge. Park trails are free; confirm any temple parking fee.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr) · [donghaksa.or.kr](http://www.donghaksa.or.kr)

### 20) 🌲 **Jangtaesan Recreation Forest** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Bald-cypress forest with a skyway canopy walk. Peak rusty-orange color is typically early November.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [jangtaesan.or.kr](https://www.jangtaesan.or.kr)

### 21) 🥾 **Gyejoksan red-clay barefoot trail** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Clay-surfaced ridge trail unique to Daejeon. November clay is cold; wash stations are provided.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr) · [Daedeok-gu](https://www.daedeok.go.kr)

### 22) 🏞️ **Daecheong Lake Osolgil** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Lakeside walking courses and dam viewpoints on Daejeon’s eastern edge.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr) · [K-water](https://www.kwater.or.kr)

### 23) 🪦 **Daejeon National Cemetery** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** National cemetery with a well-known ginkgo avenue. Free; daylight hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [dnc.go.kr](https://www.dnc.go.kr)

### 24) 🛒 **Daejeon Jungang Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET
- **What:** Large traditional market west of Daejeon Station.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)

### 25) 🍜 **Daejeon kalguksu and dubu-duruchigi** — 🔎 VERIFIED LOCAL FOOD

### 26) 🔭 **Art Space 193 / Shinsegae Expo Tower** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** High observatory and art floors in the Shinsegae Art & Science tower. Hours and fares are operator-controlled.

## Cheonan and Asan

### 30) 🏛️ **Independence Hall of Korea** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL MUSEUM
- **What:** National independence museum in Mokcheon, Cheonan. Free. November hours are shorter; closed Monday. The maple avenue on the grounds is the foliage walk — not a separate festival unless the Hall announces one.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [i815.or.kr](https://www.i815.or.kr)

### 31) 🥮 **Hakwha Hodugwaja** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Original Cheonan walnut pastry (est. 1934). Eat them warm from a named branch.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [hodo1934.com](https://www.hodo1934.com)

### 32) 🛕 **Gakwonsa** — 🔎 VERIFIED TEMPLE
- **What:** Mountain temple with a large outdoor seated bronze Buddha on Taejosan. Grounds are free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [gakwonsa.or.kr](http://www.gakwonsa.or.kr)

### 33) 🖼️ **Arario Gallery Cheonan and outdoor sculptures** — 🔎 VERIFIED GALLERY
- **What:** Gallery and public sculpture plaza at Cheonan Terminal (Haring, Hirst, and others). Plaza is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [arariogallery.com](https://www.arariogallery.com)

### 34) 🏢 **Cheonan City Hall sky lounge** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** High-floor public observatory. City listing has described free access and a Monday closure — confirm hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [cheonan.go.kr](https://www.cheonan.go.kr)

### 35) ✉️ **Postal Museum of Korea** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL MUSEUM
- **What:** Postal-history museum in Cheonan. Free; closed Monday.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [postmuseum.go.kr](https://www.postmuseum.go.kr)

### 36) 🍲 **Byeongcheon sundae street** — 🔎 VERIFIED FOOD STREET
- **What:** Named cluster of blood-sausage soup houses. Pair with the Yu Gwan-sun memorial if you go that far.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [tour.chungnam.go.kr](https://tour.chungnam.go.kr)

### 37) 🇰🇷 **Yu Gwan-sun Memorial Hall** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Memorial at the independence activist’s birthplace near Aunae / Byeongcheon.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [cheonan.go.kr](https://www.cheonan.go.kr)

### 38) 🎭 **Cheonan Arts Center** — ⏳ CHECK CALENDAR
- **What:** Municipal halls and museum. Attend only a dated show on [cnac.or.kr](https://www.cnac.or.kr).

### 39) 🏘️ **Oeam Folk Village (Asan)** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · labeled Asan
- **What:** Living Joseon village of thatched and tiled houses. November last entry is earlier.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [oeam.co.kr](https://www.oeam.co.kr) · [asan.go.kr](https://www.asan.go.kr)

### 40) 🗡️ **Hyeonchungsa (Asan)** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL SHRINE · labeled Asan
- **What:** Shrine and estate of Admiral Yi Sun-sin. Free; closed Monday. November foliage on the grounds is excellent.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [hcs.khs.go.kr](https://hcs.khs.go.kr)

### 41) ♨️ **Onyang Hot Springs (Asan)** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · labeled Asan
- **What:** Korea’s oldest recorded royal hot-spring town. Public bathhouses and a town-center foot bath.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [asan.go.kr](https://www.asan.go.kr)

## Labeled day trips — Gongju and Buyeo (UNESCO Baekje)

These are **not** Daejeon neighborhoods. They are 1–1.5 hours by bus and worth a dedicated day.

### 42) 👑 **Tomb of King Muryeong and Gongju National Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO / NATIONAL MUSEUM
- **What:** Intact Baekje royal tomb site (view a replica; treasures are in the free national museum next door).
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO 1477](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1477) · [gongju.museum.go.kr](https://gongju.museum.go.kr)

### 43) 🛕 **Magoksa (Gongju)** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SANSA
- **What:** One of the seven UNESCO mountain monasteries. Free entry; parking charged.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [magoksa.or.kr](http://www.magoksa.or.kr) · [UNESCO 1562](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1562)

### 44) 🏯 **Buyeo — Busosanseong, Gungnamji, Buyeo National Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO AREA
- **What:** Last Baekje capital: fortress, Korea’s oldest recorded garden pond, and the gilt-bronze incense burner (National Treasure 287) in the free national museum.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO 1477](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1477) · [buyeo.museum.go.kr](https://buyeo.museum.go.kr) · [buyeo.go.kr](https://www.buyeo.go.kr)
