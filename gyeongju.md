# 🏛️ Gyeongju — Ancient Silla Capital (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's "Museum Without Walls"** — 2,000 years of Silla Dynasty (57 BCE – 935 CE) heritage packed into a single city between Busan and Daegu on the KTX line. Home to **15+ UNESCO World Heritage sites** — more than any other Korean city. Autumn foliage here is unparalleled.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Bulguksa/Seokguram, Daereungwon Royal Tombs, Hwangridan Street, and Donggung Palace/Wolji Pond.

**Official Gyeongju sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Gyeongju (official tourism): [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- Gyeongju National Museum: [gyeongju.museum.go.kr](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr)
- Bulguksa Temple: [bulguksa.kr](http://www.bulguksa.kr)
- Gyeongju World Culture Expo (occasional): [gcexpo.or.kr](https://www.gcexpo.or.kr)

**Getting there:** Seoul (KTX) → Gyeongju Station (~2 hr 15 min) · Busan (KTX) → Gyeongju (50–65 min) · Daegu (KTX) → Gyeongju (20 min)

## 🏯 UNESCO heritage — must-sees in your window

### 1) 🏯 Bulguksa Temple (불국사) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Korea's most iconic UNESCO-recognized Buddhist temple complex (est. 528 CE, regal reconstruction 751 CE). Stone terrace (*Seokgatap* and *Dabotap* pagodas), Gilt-bronze Vairocana, Amitabha, and 8,000 small Buddhas
- **Hours:** Daily **07:00–17:30** (sunset closes pavilions)
- **Price:** Adult **₩5,000** (~$3.50); youth ₩3,500; child ₩2,500
- **Official sources:** [bulguksa.kr](http://www.bulguksa.kr) · [gjw.go.kr](http://gjw.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Best autumn foliage flights in early November. **30 min bus from Gyeongju Station** — bus 10 or 11.

### 2) 🗿 Seokguram Grotto (석굴암) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** UNESCO-recognized artificial cave (est. 751 CE) housing the **8 m main Silla Vairocana Buddha statue** — bulbous rosy cheek, considered Korea's masterpiece of Buddhist art
- **Hours:** Daily **07:00–17:30** (closed Mon separately)
- **Price:** Adult **₩5,000** (combined with Bulguksa usually **₩7,000**)
- **Official source:** [bulguksa.kr](http://www.bulguksa.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Closes earlier than Bulguksa** — go to Seokguram first, then down the stone trail to Bulguksa.

### 3) 🏛️ Gyeongju National Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's 2nd most important museum — preserved Silla gold crowns, **Emille Bell** (National Treasure No. 29), ceramics, Buddhist statuary
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (Nov winter hours)
- **Price:** **FREE** (special exhibitions ticketed separately)
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.museum.go.kr](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Right next to **Daereungwon Royal Tombs** — see #4

### 4) 🏛️ Daereungwon Royal Tombs (대릉원) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** UNESCO-listed burial mound park for Silla kings and queens — surrounded by 23 earthen tombs including the famous **Cheonmachong** ("Heavenly Horse Tomb"; you can enter the replica)
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (N/A in winter, closes 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2); Cheonmachong entry adult **₩1,000** extra
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Picturesque maple foliage in early Nov.

### 5) 🏯 Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond (동궁과 월지) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** UNESCO-recognized Silla secondary palace complex, with beautiful pond surrounded by autumn maple trees. Korean *garden pond* architecture masterpiece. Nighttime peaceful illuminations
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–21:30** (Nov starts walking-back). Last entry 21:00
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2); child ₩2,000
- **Official source:** [gjw.go.kr](http://gjw.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Most photogenic at night** — reflections are stunning.

### 6) 🏯 Gyeongju Folk Craft Village & Hwangnam Traditional Food — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Cluster of 30+ hanok crafts workshops and themed restaurants just east of Wolji Pond
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–22:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in; workshops ~₩10,000–30,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Almost possible for half-day shopping.

### 7) 🏛️ Cheomseongdae Observatory (첨성대) — ✅ CONFIRMED — always on (FREE)
- **What:** Asia's oldest surviving astronomical observatory (est. 632 CE under Queen Seondeok of Silla). 9.17 m tall, 362 stones. UNESCO recognized
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Easy 20 min stroll from Wolji Pond.

### 8) 🗿 Poseokjeong Pavilion (포석정) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Pavilion on a Silla-era royal villa grounds featuring an **artificial rocky tide-water channel architecture** displaying Korean landscape art
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Northeast of Wolji — quiet walk.

### 9) 🏯 Gyerim Imbal Geumdong Forest — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Legendary ancient birch forest ringed by the Silla founder's wife, the queen of historic Silla birth myth
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Beginner notes:** Perfect peaceful walk.

### 10) 🏛️ Yangdong Folk Village (양동민속마을) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** UNESCO-recognized 5-century-old aristocrat hanok village — one of 2 in Korea — home to 54 traditional Korean homes, including the celebrated **Seokbinggo** (stone ice-storage)
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov winter)
- **Price:** Adult **₩4,000**
- **Official source:** [yangdongfolk.co.kr](http://www.yangdongfolk.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Combine with the Gyeongju National Museum.

### 11) 🛕 Oksan Seowon Confucian Academy (옥산서원) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** UNESCO-listed Silla/Joseon Confucian academy. The 7-year "World's oldest known" sign for Confucianism heritage
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–18:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gjw.go.kr](http://gjw.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Restful.

## 🍂 Cherry & nature scenic

### 12) 🍁 Namsan Mountain (남산) Silla Foliage Hike — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Mountain in central Gyeongju with ruins of ancient Silla temple sites and Mt. Namsan's 22 icons (Buddhist relics), some of Korea's most beautiful foliage in Nov
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Multiple entrance options; choose the one near your hotel.

### 13) 🌾 Tohamsan Mountain (토함산) Maple Hiking — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Mountain with autumn maple trails above Bulguksa
- **Hours:** Daily 05:00–17:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Quiet connecting trail to Bulguksa.

### 14) 🏛️ Bomunsa Temple — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Hillside temple southwest of downtown
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–18:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)

### 15) 🌊 Bomun Tourist Complex (보문관광단지) — 🔁 always on
- **What:** Crescent-shaped resort with Bomun Lake and 5 golf courses, hotels, hot springs, and an outdoor sculpture walk

### 16) 🌿 Bomun Lake & Sculpture Park — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Manicured lake walks with public-art installations and a famous wave-private island

- **Price:** **FREE** (pedal-boat rental ~₩10,000)
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Sunset view.

### 17) 🌳 Gyeongju World Adventure Theme Park (경월 어드벤처 랜드) — 👀 WATCH

- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 10:00–17:30
- **Price:** Variable

## 🎨 Culture & museums

### 18) 🖼️ Gyeongju Yangdong Folk Village & Cheomseongdae — 👀 WATCH special nights (Nov)
- **What:** Nighttime illumination of Yangdong historic district during the annual Silla Cultural Heritage Festival (held late Oct–early Nov)
- **Hours:** Nov evening illuminations 18:00–22:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)

### 19) 🎭 Gyeongju National Park Silla Cultural Heritage Festival — 👀 WATCH (Oct–Nov 2026)
- **What:** Annual outdoor reenactment at Mt. Namsan, including historical costume parades, lantern processions, and musical tributes
- **Dates:** 2026 TBA - 2024 ran Oct 24–Nov 3, 2025 Oct 24–Nov 2
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** If reserved dates include 10/31 or 11/1, this is a premium event.

### 20) 🖼️ Gyeongju Hwarang Folk Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Price:** **FREE** or low
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)

## 🍴 Gyeongju's culinary heritage

### 36) 🍜 Gyeongju *ssambap* (쌈밥) rice wraps — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Korea's signature salad wrap — 20+ ingredients, your own to wrap with rice. The hack of Gyeongju dining
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–21:00
- **Price:** ~₩8,000–15,000 per head
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)

### 37) 🍚 Gyeongju Hanwoo Kimchi Stew — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Spicy pork / kimchi / canned-clam stew served at legendary downtown restaurants
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–22:00

### 38) 🥟 Hwangnam Bread (황남빵) — daily
- **What:** Red bean-paste cream puffs. A Gyeongju signature since 1939
- **Hours:** Daily 08:00–22:00
- **Price:** ~₩3,000 for a pack

### 39) 🍩 Gyeongju Sticky Sweet Potato Pastry (꿀떡) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Sweet traditional snack
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00–22:00

### 40) 🍑 Gyeongju Apple Pex (농축경가) — daily
- **What:** Concentrated fruit juice, a regional souvenir
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00–21:00
- **Price:** ~₩5,000 per bottle

### 41) 🍃 Nangyang Market (ㄱㅑㄷ w) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Oldest market in Korea — 1,500 booths of traditional foods and 6.000 items including sweet potatoes
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–22:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in
