# 🏛️ Gyeongju — Ancient Silla Capital (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's "Museum Without Walls"** — 2,000 years of Silla Dynasty (57 BCE – 935 CE) heritage packed into a single city between Busan and Daegu on the KTX line. Home to **15+ UNESCO World Heritage sites** — more than any other Korean city. Autumn foliage here is unparalleled.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Bulguksa/Seokguram, Daereungwon Royal Tombs, Hwangridan Street, and Donggung Palace/Wolji Pond.

**Official Gyeongju sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Gyeongju (official tourism): [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- Gyeongju National Museum: [gyeongju.museum.go.kr](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr)
- Bulguksa Temple: [bulguksa.kr](http://www.bulguksa.kr)
- Gyeongju World Culture Expo (occasional): [gcexpo.or.kr](https://www.gcexpo.or.kr)

**Getting there:** Seoul (KTX) → Gyeongju Station (~2 hr 15 min) · Busan (KTX) → Gyeongju (50–65 min) · Daegu (KTX) → Gyeongju (20 min)

## 🏯 UNESCO heritage — must-sees in your window

### 1) 🏯 Bulguksa Temple (불국사) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Korea's most iconic UNESCO-recognized Buddhist temple complex (est. 528 CE, regal reconstruction 751 CE). Stone terrace (*Seokgatap* and *Dabotap* pagodas), Gilt-bronze Vairocana, Amitabha, and 8,000 small Buddhas
- **Hours:** Daily **07:00–17:30** (sunset closes pavilions)
- **Price:** **FREE** — cultural-heritage admission was **abolished on May 4, 2023** for the 65 Jogye Order temples holding state-designated heritage (the government reimburses the temple). Parking is still charged; the on-site Bulguksa Museum is a separate ₩2,000. Older guidebooks still quote Adult ₩5,000 (~$3.50); youth ₩3,500; child ₩2,500 — there is no entry fee.
- **Official sources:** [bulguksa.kr](http://www.bulguksa.kr) · [gjw.go.kr](http://gjw.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Best autumn foliage flights in early November. **30 min bus from Gyeongju Station** — bus 10 or 11.

### 2) 🗿 Seokguram Grotto (석굴암) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** UNESCO-recognized artificial cave (est. 751 CE) housing the **8 m main Silla Vairocana Buddha statue** — bulbous rosy cheek, considered Korea's masterpiece of Buddhist art
- **Hours:** Daily **07:00–17:30** (closed Mon separately)
- **Price:** **FREE** — cultural-heritage admission was **abolished on May 4, 2023** for the 65 Jogye Order temples holding state-designated heritage (the government reimburses the temple). Parking is still charged. Older guidebooks still quote Adult ₩5,000 (combined with Bulguksa usually ₩7,000) — there is no entry fee.
- **Official source:** [bulguksa.kr](http://www.bulguksa.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Closes earlier than Bulguksa** — go to Seokguram first, then down the stone trail to Bulguksa.

### 3) 🏛️ Daereungwon Royal Tombs (대릉원) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** UNESCO-listed burial mound park for Silla kings and queens — surrounded by 23 earthen tombs including the famous **Cheonmachong** ("Heavenly Horse Tomb"; you can enter the replica)
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (N/A in winter, closes 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2); Cheonmachong entry adult **₩1,000** extra
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Picturesque maple foliage in early Nov.

### 4) 🏯 Gyeongju Folk Craft Village & Hwangnam Traditional Food — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Cluster of 30+ hanok crafts workshops and themed restaurants just east of Wolji Pond
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–22:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in; workshops ~₩10,000–30,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Almost possible for half-day shopping.

### 5) 🏛️ Yangdong Folk Village (양동민속마을) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** UNESCO-recognized 5-century-old aristocrat hanok village — one of 2 in Korea — home to 54 traditional Korean homes, including the celebrated **Seokbinggo** (stone ice-storage)
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov winter)
- **Price:** Adult **₩4,000**
- **Official source:** [yangdongfolk.co.kr](http://www.yangdongfolk.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Combine with the Gyeongju National Museum.

### 45) 🛕 Bulguksa Temple — UNESCO (불국사) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The masterpiece of Silla Buddhist architecture, founded 528 and rebuilt 751 — the Dabotap and Seokgatap pagodas, the Blue Cloud Bridge, and **UNESCO World Heritage since 1995**
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–17:00** (Nov; last entry 16:30)
- **Price:** **FREE** — cultural-heritage admission was **abolished on May 4, 2023** for the 65 Jogye Order temples holding state-designated heritage (the government reimburses the temple). Parking is still charged; the on-site Bulguksa Museum is a separate ₩2,000. Older guidebooks still quote Adult ₩6,000; youth ₩4,000; child ₩3,000 — there is no entry fee.
- **Official source:** [bulguksa.or.kr](https://www.bulguksa.or.kr) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/736)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 10/11 from Gyeongju Station (~40 min). Go at opening — by 10:30 it fills with school groups. **Foliage on the approach peaks in early November.**

### 46) 🗿 Seokguram Grotto — UNESCO (석굴암) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The granite grotto shrine above Bulguksa (774 AD) — a 3.5 m seated Buddha under a corbelled dome, facing the sunrise over the East Sea. **UNESCO World Heritage**
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–17:00** (Nov)
- **Price:** **FREE** — cultural-heritage admission was **abolished on May 4, 2023** for the 65 Jogye Order temples holding state-designated heritage (the government reimburses the temple). Parking is still charged. Older guidebooks still quote Adult ₩6,000 — there is no entry fee.
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/736) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 12 up from Bulguksa, roughly hourly. The Buddha is behind glass for conservation. The 20-minute forest walk in from the car park is part of the experience.

### 47) 🌙 Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond (동궁과 월지) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The Silla crown prince's palace and its artificial pond, built 674 — **the single most photographed night view in Korea**, with the pavilions mirrored in black water
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (last entry 21:30)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000**; youth ₩2,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Go after dark — that is the whole point.** In November the lights come on around 17:30, so 18:00–19:00 is ideal and less crowded than summer.

### 48) ⛰️ Daereungwon Tomb Complex & Cheonmachong (대릉원·천마총) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Twenty-three grass-covered Silla royal burial mounds in a city-centre park — and **Cheonmachong**, the one you can walk inside, with its gold crown and painted heavenly horse
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (Nov)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** In the town centre, walkable. **Open until 22:00 and lit at night** — the mounds after dark are extraordinary and almost empty.

### 49) 🔭 Cheomseongdae Observatory (첨성대) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Built around 634 under Queen Seondeok — **the oldest surviving astronomical observatory in East Asia**, 362 stones for the days of a lunar year
- **Hours:** Always open; illuminated at night
- **Price:** **FREE** to view
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Next to Daereungwon. Free, floodlit at night, and the surrounding meadow is planted with seasonal flowers.

### 50) 🌉 Woljeonggyo Bridge (월정교) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A full reconstruction of the Silla royal bridge over the Namcheon, completed 2018 after decades of research — a covered wooden bridge, **spectacularly lit at night**
- **Hours:** Bridge always open; interior exhibition **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Walkable from Gyochon Village. Do Woljeonggyo and Wolji Pond in the same evening — they're 15 minutes apart.

### 51) 🏛️ Gyeongju National Museum (국립경주박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The definitive collection of Silla civilisation — gold crowns, the **Divine Bell of King Seongdeok** (771, Korea's largest bell), and the Wolji Pond finds
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (Sat to 21:00; closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.museum.go.kr](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Walkable from Wolji Pond. **Free entry to one of the great museums of Asia.** Allow at least two hours; the outdoor bell pavilion is free even when halls close.

### 52) 🏘️ Yangdong Folk Village — UNESCO (양동마을) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A 15th-century **living** yangban clan village of 160 tiled and thatched houses on a wooded hillside — UNESCO World Heritage, and still farmed and inhabited
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩4,000**
- **Official source:** [yangdong.invil.org](http://yangdong.invil.org) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1324)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 203 from Gyeongju (~50 min), roughly hourly. **Check the return bus time before you set off** — services are sparse.

### 53) 🏯 Gyochon Traditional Village & Choi Clan House (교촌마을) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The village of the **Gyeongju Choi family**, Korea's most famous 'twelve-generation rich' household, known for its Confucian code of charity — plus the Gyeongju Hyanggyo
- **Hours:** Village always open; house **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**; craft experiences from ₩5,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Next to Woljeonggyo. The Choi house rule that 'no one within 100 li should go hungry' is a genuine part of Korean moral folklore.

### 54) 🍶 Gyochon Gyeongju Beopju & Traditional Liquor (경주교동법주) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The Choi family's **Intangible Cultural Heritage** rice liquor, brewed by the same household for 400 years — tastings and sales in Gyochon Village
- **Hours:** Roughly **10:00–17:00**
- **Price:** Tasting free; bottles from **₩30,000**
- **Official source:** [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** A nationally designated Important Intangible Cultural Property (No. 86-3). Production is tiny — buy at source.

### 55) 👑 Tomb of King Muyeol & Seoak Tombs (무열왕릉) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The tomb of **Kim Chun-chu, King Muyeol (r. 654–661)**, who unified the Three Kingdoms — with the only Silla tombstone base whose inscription confirms the occupant
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩2,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from the centre. The turtle-and-dragon stele base is one of the finest Silla stone carvings anywhere.

### 56) 👸 Tomb of Queen Seondeok & Neungji Tower (선덕여왕릉) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The hilltop tomb of **Korea's first reigning queen (r. 632–647)**, who commissioned Cheomseongdae — reached by a quiet pine-forest path on Nangsan
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus toward Bulguksa, then a 15-minute walk uphill. Free, empty and one of the most atmospheric spots in Gyeongju.

### 57) 🌲 Oreung Royal Tombs (오릉) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Five mounds traditionally held to contain **Bak Hyeokgeose, the founding king of Silla (57 BC)**, and his queen — in a walled pine and zelkova grove
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)

- **Beginner notes:** South of the centre, walkable or a short bus. The grove in November colour is quiet and beautiful.

### 58) 🌳 Gyerim Forest (계림) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The ancient zelkova and willow grove where, by legend, the **Kim clan's founder was found in a golden box** in 65 AD — beside the Wolseong palace site
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Between Cheomseongdae and Woljeonggyo. **Golden foliage here in early November** is one of Gyeongju's best free sights.

### 59) 🏰 Wolseong Palace Site & Seokbinggo Ice Store (월성·석빙고) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The earthwork ramparts of the Silla royal palace, under long-running excavation — plus the 1738 **Seokbinggo**, a stone ice house still standing in the bank
- **Hours:** Always open; excavation viewing platform daylight hours
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Walkable from Cheomseongdae. You can watch active archaeology from the public platform.

### 60) 🗻 Namsan Mountain Buddhist Trails (남산) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** An entire mountain designated **UNESCO World Heritage** — over 100 temple sites, 80 stone Buddhas and 60 pagodas scattered across the slopes of the Silla holy mountain
- **Hours:** Always open; allow 3–5 h for a full crossing
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/976) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus to Samneung for the classic Samneung → Sangseonam → Yongjangsa route. **November is the ideal hiking month** — cool, clear and no insects.

### 61) 🪨 Samneung Valley & Buddha Carvings, Namsan — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The most rewarding single section of Namsan — a pine-forest valley strung with 7th–9th-century rock-carved Buddhas, several of them headless and all of them free
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/976)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 500 series to Samneung. About 90 minutes up to the seated Buddha; the morning mist through the pines is famous among photographers.

### 62) 🛕 Bunhwangsa Temple & Stone Pagoda (분황사) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Founded 634 under Queen Seondeok — its **stone-brick pagoda is the oldest datable pagoda in Silla**, and the temple is still active
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩1,300**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Next to the Hwangnyongsa site. National Treasure No. 30, and rarely crowded.

### 63) 🏛️ Hwangnyongsa Temple Site & History Culture Hall (황룡사지) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The empty platform of what was **the largest wooden building in East Asia** — a 9-storey, 80 m pagoda burned by the Mongols in 1238, now reconstructed at 1:10 scale in the adjacent hall
- **Hours:** Site always open; exhibition hall Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Site **FREE**; hall ₩3,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The scale model in the hall makes the lost pagoda comprehensible. The empty field itself is oddly moving.

### 64) 🥋 Golgulsa Temple & Sunmudo (골굴사) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A cave temple carved into a limestone cliff, home of **Sunmudo**, the Korean Buddhist martial art — with public demonstrations and a well-known templestay
- **Hours:** Daily **08:00–18:00**; demonstrations typically **15:30**
- **Price:** Entry **₩3,000**; templestay from ₩70,000
- **Official source:** [golgulsa.com](https://www.golgulsa.com) · [templestay.com](https://www.templestay.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 100/150 toward Gampo. The cliff Buddha relief is 9th-century; the martial-art demonstration is genuinely impressive.

### 65) 🌊 Underwater Tomb of King Munmu, Daewangam (문무대왕릉) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The rock islet 200 m offshore where King Munmu was buried in 681, having asked to become a **sea dragon guarding Silla from Japanese pirates** — the world's only known underwater royal tomb
- **Hours:** Always open; sunrise is the traditional time
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 150 from Gyeongju (~1 h). Shamans still hold rituals on the beach at dawn — a living, not museum, tradition.

### 66) 🛕 Gameunsa Temple Site & Twin Pagodas (감은사지) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Two 13 m granite pagodas (682) standing alone in a field near the coast — built by King Sinmun for his father Munmu, with a channel under the hall for the sea dragon to enter
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** On the bus 150 route to Daewangam. Free, unstaffed, and among the most powerful sights in Korea at dawn or dusk.

### 67) 🪨 Yangnam Jusangjeolli Columnar Joints (양남 주상절리) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A rare **fan-shaped radial basalt column formation** on the Gyeongju coast, formed 20 million years ago — with a coastal boardwalk and observation deck
- **Hours:** Always open; boardwalk lit until 22:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 150 toward Gampo. The fan-shaped cluster is geologically unusual worldwide — most columnar basalt is vertical.

### 68) 🏖️ Bonggil & Najeong Beach, Gyeongju Coast — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Gyeongju's East Sea beaches — pine-backed, quiet in November, and the sunrise coast for Daewangam
- **Hours:** Always open; **sunrise ~07:05 in November**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 150. Najeong has a cafe strip; Bonggil has the fish market and the boats to nowhere.

### 69) 🐋 Gampo Port & East Sea Fish Market — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The working fishing port on Gyeongju's coast — a morning auction, a raw fish market and snow crab in season
- **Hours:** Market roughly **06:00–19:00**
- **Price:** **FREE** to browse
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 150 terminus. Combine with Daewangam and Gameunsa in one coastal day trip.

### 70) 🌅 Bomun Lake Resort & Lakeside Path (보문관광단지) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Gyeongju's purpose-built resort district around a 1.65 km² lake — a flat 8 km lakeside walking and cycling loop, hotels, a musical fountain and the Gyeongju Tower
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**; bike hire from ₩5,000/h
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 10/100 from the centre. **The lakeside foliage peaks in early-to-mid November**; the loop is the best easy walk in Gyeongju.

### 71) 🗼 Gyeongju Tower & Expo Park (경주타워·경주엑스포대공원) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A 82 m tower whose facade is a **negative silhouette of the lost Hwangnyongsa nine-storey pagoda** — plus the Expo park's exhibition halls and media shows (경주타워 천년대계, 천마의 궁전 *Timeless* media art, 상상동물원, VR studio)
- **Hours:** Park **Mon–Thu 10:00–22:30, Fri–Sun 10:00–23:30**; exhibition halls **10:00–19:00**. Open year-round
- **Price:** Park pass (covers tower + most exhibition halls) adult **₩12,000** / child 36 mths–18 yrs **₩10,000**; groups of 10+ ₩10,000/₩8,000; **65+ ₩4,000**; Gyeongju/Gyeongbuk residents ₩8,000/₩6,000 with ID; under 36 months free. **Luminight** night light-walk is a separate ₩6,000 (sunset–22:00, Mar–Dec)
- **Official source:** [cultureexpo.or.kr](https://www.cultureexpo.or.kr/open.content/ko/) · [fares](https://www.cultureexpo.or.kr/open.content/ko/guide/information.use/) · ☎ 054-748-3011
- **Beginner notes:** At Bomun Lake. ⚠️ The old `gyeongjuexpo.com` domain is dead — **cultureexpo.or.kr** is the operator's site. The evening lighting and the tower media show are the reason to come after dark; the park stays open late, but the indoor halls shut at 19:00.

### 72) ⛔ Silla Millennium Park (신라밀레니엄파크) — ⛔ CLOSED — do not plan a visit
- **What:** The Silla-era theme park with the **Queen Seondeok** drama sets **has been shut since 2016–17** and the Bomun site sits derelict pending a redevelopment/land-use decision. Older guidebooks, blogs and map apps still list it — **it is not operating.** Its former domain no longer belongs to the park
- **Hours:** **Permanently closed**
- **Price:** n/a
- **Official source:** [Gyeongbuk Culture & Tourism Corp (gyeongbuk.go.kr)](https://www.gyeongbuk.go.kr) · [Yeongnam Ilbo report on the derelict site](https://www.yeongnam.com/web/view.php?key=20240925010003029)
- **Beginner notes:** **Instead:** for costumed Silla performance and hands-on crafts go to **Gyeongju Expo Park (#169)**; for rides go to **Gyeongju World (#171)** — both are 5–10 min away in the same Bomun resort area.

### 73) 🎢 Gyeongju World & California Beach (경주월드) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The region's main amusement park at Bomun, with the **Draken** and **Phaethon** roller coasters, among Korea's most intense
- **Hours:** Daily, typically **10:00–20:00** in high season and shorter in Nov — the official site prints the **operating calendar day by day** (운영/운휴), so check the date before you go
- **Price:** Day pass adult **₩54,000**, youth ₩47,000, child ₩39,000; weekday-after-3pm / holiday-after-4pm pass adult ₩39,000. **Foreigners get 30% off on production of a passport**; over-60s ₩10,000
- **Official source:** [gjw.co.kr](https://www.gjw.co.kr/Contents/contents.php) · [English site](https://www.gjw.co.kr/Contents/eng_contents.php) · [fares](https://www.gjw.co.kr/Contents/contents.php?cmsNo=DA0300)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 10 from the centre. Check the winter operating calendar — some rides close in November.

### 74) 🌳 Gyeongju National Park — Toham & Danseoksan sections — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's **only national park designated for its cultural heritage rather than its landscape** — eight separate districts covering Namsan, Toham, Danseok and the coast
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr) · [english.knps.or.kr](https://english.knps.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Trailhead information at each district. Toham-san behind Bulguksa is the classic ridge walk to Seokguram on foot.

### 75) 🍁 Gyeongju Autumn Foliage — Bomun, Bulguksa, Gyerim — ✅ CONFIRMED — peak early-to-mid Nov
- **What:** Gyeongju's ginkgo and maple peak **roughly Nov 1–12** — the Bulguksa approach, Gyerim Forest, Bomun lakeside and the Oreung grove are the four best sites
- **Hours:** Daylight hours
- **Price:** **FREE** except site entries
- **Official source:** [Korea Forest Service](https://www.forest.go.kr) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Gyeongju in early November is arguably the best-timed city in this entire guide. Book accommodation early — Koreans know this too.

### 76) 🌾 Gyeongju Samneung Pine Forest & Morning Mist — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The pine grove around the three royal tombs at the foot of Namsan — famous among Korean landscape photographers for the light through the mist at dawn
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 500 series. **Be there at sunrise (~07:05 in November)** for the mist; by 09:00 it's an ordinary pine wood.

### 77) 🌿 Gyeongju Hwangseong Park & Citizen Forest — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The city's main recreation park with sports fields, a 3 km jogging loop and a large tree-lined avenue — where Gyeongju residents actually go
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** North of the centre, walkable. Useful if you want a run or a quiet hour away from the heritage sites.

### 78) 🎨 Gyeongju Solgeo Art Museum (경주솔거미술관) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The Expo park's public art museum, named after the legendary Silla painter Solgeo — best known for its large **Park Dae-sung (박대성)** ink-painting collection plus rotating special exhibitions
- **Hours:** Inside the Expo park; museum roughly **10:00–18:00** (winter) / to 20:00 in summer, last entry 30 min before close. Exhibition halls follow the park's **10:00–19:00** window — check the current show before travelling
- **Price:** **Included in the Expo park pass** (adult ₩12,000 / child ₩10,000; 65+ ₩4,000)
- **Official source:** [gjsam.or.kr](https://www.gjsam.or.kr/ko/) · [cultureexpo.or.kr](https://www.cultureexpo.or.kr/open.content/ko/)
- **Beginner notes:** At Bomun, up the hill past the "Secret Garden" photo spot. A calm indoor option if the weather turns — but note only part of the collection is hung during special exhibitions, so read the museum's notice board first.

### 79) 🖼️ Wooyang Museum of Contemporary Art (우양미술관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** A serious contemporary art museum at Bomun in a Kim Swoo-geun building — Korean and international modern art, an unexpected find in a heritage city
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (last entry 17:30); closed **Mondays**, Jan 1, Seollal/Chuseok day and between exhibitions
- **Price:** Varies by exhibition — up to adult **₩15,000** / student ₩12,000 / child ₩10,000; Gyeongju residents 20%, seniors & disabled 50% off. Some shows are free
- **Official source:** [wooyangmuseum.org](https://wooyangmuseum.org) · [English](http://en.wooyangmuseum.org/info) · ☎ 054-745-7075
- **Beginner notes:** At Bomun Lake, next to the Hilton. Kim Swoo-geun is Korea's most important modern architect — the building is part of the exhibit.

### 80) 🧸 Gyeongju Teddy Bear Museum & Bird Park — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Two family attractions at Bomun — teddy bear dioramas of Silla history, and a walk-through aviary and botanical garden
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–19:00**
- **Price:** Each roughly **₩10,000–₩18,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** At Bomun Lake. Tourist-trap territory, but genuinely good with small children on a cold day.

### 81) 🛷 Gyeongju Luge World (경주루지월드) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A 1.5 km gravity luge track down a hillside at Bomun, with karts you steer yourself — no engine, just brakes and nerve
- **Hours:** Daily roughly **10:00–18:00** (Nov)
- **Price:** 2 rides **₩25,000**; combo with gondola from ₩33,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** At Bomun. Dress warmly — it's open air and fast.

### 82) 🏛️ Gyeongju Historic Areas — UNESCO overview walk — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The **Gyeongju Historic Areas** UNESCO listing covers five zones — Namsan, Wolseong, Daereungwon, Hwangnyongsa and Sanseong — most of which are free and walkable
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** Mostly **FREE**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/976) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The Wolseong belt from Cheomseongdae through Gyerim to Woljeonggyo is a flat 3 km and entirely free. Start here on day one.

### 83) 🚲 Gyeongju Bicycle Loop & Rental (경주 자전거) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Gyeongju is flat and compact, and the standard local way to see it is by bicycle — rental shops cluster around the station and Bomun
- **Hours:** Rentals roughly **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **₩10,000–₩15,000** per day
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **The single best way to see Gyeongju.** The central heritage belt is entirely bikeable and November weather is ideal for it.

### 84) 🍜 Hwangnidan-gil Cafe & Restaurant Street (황리단길) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Gyeongju's converted old-town street of hanok cafes, craft shops and restaurants, next to Daereungwon — the city's social centre
- **Hours:** Most **11:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Coffee **₩5,000–₩7,000**; meals ₩10,000–₩20,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Directly beside the tomb park. Busy at weekends; hanbok rental shops here serve the Daereungwon photo trade.

### 85) 🥮 Gyeongju Hwangnam-ppang & Chalborideok (황남빵) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The red-bean pastry invented in Gyeongju in 1939, sold from the original shop near Daereungwon — plus the barley-cake *chalborideok* variant
- **Hours:** Roughly **08:00–22:00**
- **Price:** A box of 10 **₩13,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** There's a queue at the original Hwangnam-ppang shop most afternoons. They're best eaten the same day.

### 86) 🍲 Gyeongju Ssambap & Korean Set Meal Restaurants (경주 쌈밥) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Gyeongju's signature meal — a table of 20+ side dishes with leaves for wrapping, served at the restaurant row near Daereungwon and Bulguksa
- **Hours:** Most **10:00–21:00**
- **Price:** Per person **₩15,000–₩22,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** A ssambap spread is the classic Gyeongju lunch. Minimum two people at most restaurants.

### 87) 🧺 Seongdong Market (성동시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Gyeongju's main traditional market beside the old station — a large food alley, dried goods and a famously cheap set-menu hall
- **Hours:** Roughly **07:00–20:00** (closed some Sundays)
- **Price:** Meals **₩6,000–₩10,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Walkable from the town centre. The upstairs food court does full Korean meals for under ₩10,000.

### 88) 🍢 Gyeongju Jungang Market & Night Food Alley — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The older covered market in the town centre, with a night-time street-food section and local specialities
- **Hours:** Roughly **08:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Snacks **₩2,000–₩6,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Less polished than Seongdong and correspondingly cheaper. Good for an evening wander.

### 89) 🍶 Gyeongju Beopju & Local Craft Brewery Tastings — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Beyond the Choi family's Beopju, Gyeongju has a growing craft brewing scene using local rice and barley
- **Hours:** Roughly **12:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Tasting flights from **₩10,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Several are on Hwangnidan-gil. Ask for *makgeolli* made with Gyeongju rice.

### 90) 👘 Gyeongju Hanbok Rental & Free Site Entry — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Wearing hanbok gives **free or discounted entry to several Gyeongju heritage sites**, and the rental shops cluster on Hwangnidan-gil
- **Hours:** Shops roughly **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Rental **₩15,000–₩30,000** for 2–4 hours
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)

### 91) 🏨 Gyeongju Hanok Guesthouse Stay (경주 한옥스테이) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Gyeongju has one of Korea's densest concentrations of hanok guesthouses, many in the Hwangnam and Gyochon districts
- **Hours:** Check-in typically **15:00**
- **Price:** From **₩60,000–₩150,000** per night

### 92) 🧘 Gyeongju Templestay — Golgulsa & Bulguksa — 🔁 daily

### 93) 🚄 Gyeongju via Singyeongju KTX Station — 🔁 daily

- **Beginner notes:** A hot bath after a day on Namsan is the correct sequence. Non-guests are admitted at most of them.

### 101) 🏛️ Gyeongju Silla Arts & Crafts Village (신라공예촌) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Workshops of designated master craftspeople reproducing Silla metalwork, pottery and jewellery — with hands-on classes
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE** to browse; workshops from ₩15,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Near Bulguksa. Making a small gold-crown replica is a better souvenir than buying one.

### 102) 📜 Gyeongju Poseokjeong & Royal Banquet Site (포석정) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The stone water channel where Silla kings floated wine cups at poetry banquets — and where the last king was surprised and killed in 927
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩2,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** At the foot of Namsan, bus 500 series. Small, but the story of the dynasty ending at a drinking party is hard to beat.

### 103) 🗿 Gyeongju Seoak-dong Three-storey Pagoda & Stone Buddhas — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Free, unstaffed Silla stone monuments scattered across the western suburbs — pagodas, Buddha triads and tomb stelae, usually with nobody there
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Gyeongju has hundreds of these. Renting a bike and finding them yourself is the connoisseur's version of the city.

**Second expansion batch** — outlying UNESCO sites, free stone heritage, hikes, night photography timing and practical November logistics.

### 104) 🛕 Girimsa Temple (기림사) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A large, quiet temple founded in 643 in the Hamwolsan valley east of Gyeongju — five hall complexes, a 500-arhat hall and almost no visitors
- **Hours:** Daily **08:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** **FREE** — cultural-heritage admission was **abolished on May 4, 2023** for the 65 Jogye Order temples holding state-designated heritage (the government reimburses the temple). Parking is still charged. Older guidebooks still quote Adult ₩3,000 — there is no entry fee.
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 100/150 toward Gampo. Bigger and older than most famous temples, with a fraction of the crowd.

### 105) 🌊 Yongyeon Waterfall & Gyeongju Hamwolsan Valley — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The waterfall on the road to Girimsa where, in legend, King Sinmun received the magic flute *Manpasikjeok* that could calm all troubles
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** On the bus 150 route. A short stop, but a good one, and the valley is beautiful in autumn.

### 106) 🏯 Gyeongju Yangdong Village Seobaekdang & Hyangdan Houses — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The two most important houses in UNESCO Yangdong — the 1454 **Seobaekdang** clan head house and the unusual H-plan **Hyangdan**, both National Treasures or Treasures
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** Included in Yangdong entry **₩4,000**
- **Official source:** [yangdong.invil.org](http://yangdong.invil.org) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1324)
- **Beginner notes:** Some houses are private homes — only enter those marked open. The hilltop pavilion view over the whole village is the best photo.

### 107) 📚 Oksan Seowon — UNESCO (옥산서원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 1573 Confucian academy honouring the scholar Yi Eon-jeok — one of the nine **UNESCO-listed Korean seowon**, in a stream valley near Yangdong
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1498) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 203 from Gyeongju. The Dongnakdang villa next door, where Yi Eon-jeok lived in retirement, is equally good and equally empty.

### 108) 🏞️ Dongnakdang House & Jasan Seodang, Oksan — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The 1532 retirement villa of Yi Eon-jeok beside the Oksan stream — a masterpiece of Joseon scholar-house design, with pavilions over the water
- **Hours:** Daylight hours
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Next to Oksan Seowon. The stream-side pavilion in November colour is one of the quietest good things in this guide.

### 109) ⛰️ Danseoksan & Sinseonsa Hermitage (단석산 신선사) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Gyeongju's highest peak, with a natural rock cleft containing 10 large **6th-century Buddhist relief carvings** — the oldest in the region, and a National Treasure
- **Hours:** Always open; ~2 h round trip
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Gyeongju toward Geoncheon. The carvings are inside a U-shaped rock enclosure — utterly unlike anything else in Korea.

### 110) 🗿 Gulbulsa Site Four-Buddha Stone (굴불사지 사면석불) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A single boulder carved with Buddhas on all four faces (8th century), standing alone below Sogeumgangsan on the way to Baengnyulsa
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** North of the centre, reachable by bike. Free, unfenced, and 1,300 years old.

### 111) 🛕 Baengnyulsa Temple & Sogeumgangsan (백률사·소금강산) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The temple marking where **Ichadon was martyred in 527**, the event that made Buddhism the Silla state religion — on a small hill with a city view
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** A 20-minute climb from the Gulbulsa boulder. The whole northern hill is a free open-air heritage walk.

### 112) 🏛️ Gyeongju Silla History Science Museum (신라역사과학관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** A private museum devoted to the **engineering** of Silla — how Seokguram's dome, the Divine Bell and the pagodas were actually built, with cutaway models
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩6,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Near Bulguksa. If you found Seokguram impressive, this explains why it should not be structurally possible.

### 113) 🖼️ Gyeongju Arte Museum & Media Art (아르떼뮤지엄 경주) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A large immersive digital-art space at Bomun — room-scale projection installations, indoor and warm, from the team behind the Jeju original
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–20:00**
- **Price:** Adult **₩17,000**
- **Official source:** [artemuseum.com](https://www.artemuseum.com)
- **Beginner notes:** At Bomun Lake. A good option for a cold or wet November afternoon, and popular with families.

### 114) 🌌 Gyeongju Stargazing at Bomun & Namsan — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Gyeongju's low-rise skyline and the ban on tall buildings in the heritage zone make it one of the darkest mid-sized cities in Korea for stargazing
- **Hours:** After dark; **new moon is best**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute](https://www.kasi.re.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The Namsan foothills and the far side of Bomun Lake are the darkest accessible spots. November skies are clear and cold.

### 115) 🚶 Gyeongju Silla Sculpture Path & Wolseong Loop Walk — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A flat, signposted 5 km circuit linking Cheomseongdae, Gyerim, Wolseong, Woljeonggyo and Gyochon — **the single best free walk in the city**
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/976)
- **Beginner notes:** Start at Daereungwon. About two hours at a slow pace, all of it UNESCO-listed, none of it ticketed.

### 116) 🚌 Gyeongju City Tour Bus (경주시티투어) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The official day tour bus with routes covering Bulguksa/Seokguram, the east coast (Daewangam, Gameunsa, Jusangjeolli) and Yangdong
- **Hours:** Departures roughly **09:00–10:00**, returning **17:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **₩20,000–₩30,000** plus site entries
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **The east-coast route is the one worth taking** — Daewangam, Gameunsa and Jusangjeolli are awkward and slow by public bus.

### 117) 🥘 Gyeongju Chalborippang & Gyeongju Bread Culture — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Beyond Hwangnam-ppang, Gyeongju has a whole regional bread culture — barley cakes, walnut breads and the *ssarippang* rice buns sold along Hwangnidan-gil
- **Hours:** Roughly **09:00–21:00**
- **Price:** **₩1,000–₩2,000** each
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Buy a mixed box. They keep about two days and make good train food.

### 118) 🍲 Gyeongju Haejangguk & Local Beef Restaurants — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Gyeongju's inland food traditions — *hanwoo* beef from the surrounding hills and hangover soup houses that open before dawn near Seongdong Market
- **Hours:** Some open **05:00**; most **10:00–21:00**
- **Price:** Soup **₩9,000**; hanwoo from ₩30,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The early-opening soup houses by the market are how you handle a 07:00 sunrise trip to Daewangam.

### 119) 🌙 Donggung & Wolji Pond — November night photography — ✅ CONFIRMED — nightly
- **What:** The definitive Gyeongju photograph. **In November sunset is around 17:20**, so the blue-hour reflection window is roughly 17:30–18:15 — much earlier and less crowded than summer
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00**; blue hour ~**17:30–18:15**
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bring a small tripod; the paths are wide enough. Weekday evenings are dramatically quieter than weekends.

### 120) 🎏 Gyeongju Hanbok Night Walk & Heritage Lighting Combo — 🔁 nightly
- **What:** Hanbok rental shops on Hwangnidan-gil stay open into the evening, and the lit monuments — Woljeonggyo, Cheomseongdae, Daereungwon — are free
- **Hours:** Rentals to roughly **19:00**; lighting to 23:00
- **Price:** Rental from **₩15,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Wear thermals underneath. The lit Woljeonggyo is the best backdrop and costs nothing.

### 121) 🧗 Toham-san Ridge Hike — Bulguksa to Seokguram on foot — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The historic 3.2 km pilgrim path up from Bulguksa to Seokguram — about 90 minutes each way through pine and maple forest
- **Hours:** Always open; allow 3 h round trip
- **Price:** **FREE** (site entries separate)
- **Official source:** [knps.or.kr](https://www.knps.or.kr) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Start early and take the bus back down. **November is the best month for this** — the summer version is unpleasantly humid.

### 122) 🏕️ Bomun Lake Camping & Caravan Sites — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Municipal and private camping grounds around Bomun Lake, open through the autumn with heated caravan options
- **Hours:** Check-in typically **14:00**
- **Price:** Pitches from **₩20,000**; caravans from ₩80,000
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** November nights drop near freezing inland — take a proper sleeping bag or book a heated caravan.

### 123) 🎣 Gyeongju Deokdong Lake & Reservoir Walks — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The reservoirs ringing Gyeongju — Deokdong, Bomun and Sanae — with quiet perimeter paths, waterbirds and morning mist
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [K-water](https://www.kwater.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Deokdong is on the road to Bulguksa. Almost nobody walks these, which is the point.

### 124) 🏛️ Gyeongju Bunhwangsa & Hwangnyongsa Combined Heritage Walk — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The eastern heritage cluster — Bunhwangsa, the Hwangnyongsa platform, the Guhwang-dong pagoda site and the National Museum, all within 2 km
- **Hours:** Sites daylight hours; museum Tue–Sun
- **Price:** Mostly **FREE**; Bunhwangsa ₩1,300
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [gyeongju.museum.go.kr](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** A good half-day on foot or by bike from the town centre, ending at the free national museum.

### 125) 🕯️ Gyeongju Lotus Lantern & Temple Ceremonies — 👀 WATCH
- **What:** Bulguksa, Golgulsa and Girimsa hold public Buddhist ceremonies through the year; the temples publish their own calendars
- **Hours:** Ceremony times vary; dawn services typically **04:00**
- **Price:** **FREE** to attend
- **Official source:** [bulguksa.or.kr](https://www.bulguksa.or.kr) · [golgulsa.com](https://www.golgulsa.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Visitors may attend respectfully. Check each temple's site for November dates.

### 126) 🛍️ Gyeongju Souvenir Crafts & Silla Replica Goldwork — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Licensed reproductions of the Silla gold crown, the *gogok* jade pendants and Cheonmachong metalwork, sold at the museum shop and craft village
- **Hours:** Roughly **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** From **₩10,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.museum.go.kr](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The National Museum shop is the best-quality and most reasonably priced option.

### 127) 🚏 Gyeongju Intercity & Express Bus Terminal Connections — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Frequent buses to Busan (1 h), Daegu (1 h), Pohang (40 min) and Ulsan (50 min) — Gyeongju is the natural hub of the southeast
- **Hours:** Roughly **06:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Busan **₩5,800**; Daegu ₩6,500; Pohang ₩4,500
- **Official source:** [kobus.co.kr](https://www.kobus.co.kr) · [txbus.t-money.co.kr](https://txbus.t-money.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Gyeongju–Pohang and Gyeongju–Busan are both under an hour** — you can combine city files in a single day.

### 128) 🌤️ Gyeongju November Weather & What to Wear — ✅ CONFIRMED
- **What:** Inland Gyeongju in November runs roughly **3–15 °C**, colder than Busan and drier than Seoul, with big day-night swings and excellent visibility
- **Hours:** n/a
- **Price:** n/a
- **Official source:** [Korea Meteorological Administration](https://www.weather.go.kr) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Layers plus a windproof shell. Mornings at Daewangam and on Namsan are genuinely cold; afternoons can be mild and sunny.

### 129) ♻️ Gyeongju Heritage Site Combined Ticket & Discounts — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Gyeongju sells integrated tickets covering several ticketed sites, and holders of a Korea Rail Pass or hanbok often get further discounts
- **Hours:** Ticket offices roughly **09:00–17:00**
- **Price:** Combined tickets from **₩10,000**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Ask at the first site you visit. Note that **most of the best sites here are free anyway.**

### 130) 👨‍👩‍👧 Gyeongju Family Programmes & Children's Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The National Museum's children's wing runs free hands-on Silla archaeology programmes, bookable online
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun, sessions through the day
- **Price:** **FREE** with reservation
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.museum.go.kr](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Reserve online — walk-in places are limited. Conducted in Korean, but largely hands-on.

### 131) 📸 Gyeongju Cheomseongdae Flower Field & Seasonal Planting — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The city plants the meadow around Cheomseongdae seasonally — **pink muhly grass and cosmos run into early November** before the winter clearance
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pink muhly is usually at its best late September to mid-October and fades through early November — go in the first week for the tail of it.

### 132) 🏛️ Gyeongju Silla Wolseong Excavation Public Viewing — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The Wolseong palace dig is one of Korea's largest active archaeological projects, with a public viewing platform and interpretive panels
- **Hours:** Daylight hours
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Between Cheomseongdae and Woljeonggyo. Human sacrificial burials were found here in 2017 — the panels explain the finds.

### 133) 🌏 Gyeongju as a UNESCO triple site — orientation — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Gyeongju holds **three separate UNESCO World Heritage inscriptions**: Bulguksa/Seokguram, the Gyeongju Historic Areas, and Yangdong Village — plus Oksan Seowon in the seowon listing
- **Hours:** n/a
- **Price:** n/a
- **Official source:** [UNESCO Korea listings](https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/kr) · [gyeongju.go.kr](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Four World Heritage inscriptions in one small city is unmatched in Korea. Two full days is the realistic minimum.
