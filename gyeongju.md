# 🏛️ Gyeongju — verified events and activities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Gyeongju contains three distinct UNESCO designations: **Seokguram Grotto and Bulguksa Temple**, the serial **Gyeongju Historic Areas**, and **Yangdong Village** as part of the Historic Villages of Korea. Individual royal tombs and every downtown ruin are not separate UNESCO sites.

This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** from official city tourism, venue/operator, national park, museum, heritage, temple, and UNESCO sources. The former 133-entry version mixed genuine heritage with fabricated places, closed attractions, duplicates, generic advice, and unannounced festivals.

> ✅ = dated 2026 event · 🔎 = verified place/activity · ⏳ = verify schedule/weather.
> The official city calendar showed no major November 2026 festival at review time. The Silla Cultural Festival was **Oct 9–11**, before the trip.

**Official planning:** [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/index.do) · [Visit Gyeongju](https://visitgyeongju.or.kr/) · [Gyeongju Cultural Foundation](https://garts.kr/) · [Gyeongju National Park](https://www.knps.or.kr)

### 1) 🖼️ **The Gyeongju: Gyeongju Chronicle** — ✅ CONFIRMED — through Dec 31, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Renewed immersive media-art exhibition at Gyeongju Arts Center’s Alcheon Museum, Gallery Space I.
- **When:** **Dec 2, 2025–Dec 31, 2026**.
- **Hours/price:** Check the exhibition detail for sessions, admission, and Monday closure.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Cultural Foundation current exhibitions](https://garts.kr/index.do?menuId=00000260)

### 2) 🎨 **2026 Gongyu exhibition-space support project** — ✅ CONFIRMED SERIES — through Dec 13, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Rotating regional-artist exhibitions in Alcheon Museum’s Gallery Dal and Gallery Space II.
- **When:** Foundation program runs **Mar 24–Dec 13, 2026**; verify which artist is installed on the visit date.
- **Price/hours:** Exhibition-specific; check the current-exhibition calendar.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Cultural Foundation](https://garts.kr/index.do)

### 3) 🪨 **Reunited After 83 Years: Stele Fragments Found at Gyeongju Wolseong** — ✅ CONFIRMED — through Dec 31, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** A genuinely lovely small show at **Gyeongju National Museum**: two fragments of a stone stele excavated from the western side of **Wolseong Palace** — separated for **83 years** and now displayed together again. Jointly presented by the museum and the Gyeongju National Research Institute of Cultural Heritage. It's a single-case display in the Silla Millennium Treasure House (신라천년보고) lobby, so it costs nothing and takes ten minutes, but it pairs perfectly with a Wolseong walk.
- **When:** **Apr 13 – Dec 31, 2026** — open your whole trip.
- **Price:** **Free** (the museum's permanent galleries and most special exhibitions are free).
- **⚠️ Closure to plan around:** the museum takes an irregular closed day on the **second Monday of November** — that is **Mon Nov 9, 2026**. Grounds and outdoor exhibits stay open. Regular hours 10:00–18:00.
- **Note:** The big summer blockbusters (*Hwangnyongsa*, and *Silla: l'Or et le Sacré* with the Musée Guimet) both **close before you arrive** — Oct 11 and Aug 31 respectively. Don't plan around them.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju National Museum — special exhibitions](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr/kor/html/sub02/0202.html)

## UNESCO and central Silla heritage

### 4) 🛕 **Bulguksa Temple** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SITE
- **What:** Major Silla Buddhist temple forming one World Heritage property with Seokguram.
- **Hours/price:** Temple entry is free following the 2023 heritage-temple admission change; parking and special facilities may charge. Verify seasonal hours.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/736/) · [Bulguksa](https://www.bulguksa.or.kr)

### 5) 🗿 **Seokguram Grotto** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SITE
- **What:** Eighth-century Buddhist grotto on Tohamsan, managed with restricted interior viewing to protect the sculpture.
- **Hours/price:** General heritage-temple entry is free; parking and shuttle transport are separate. Verify last entry.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/736/) · [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/)

### 6) ⚰️ **Daereungwon Tomb Complex and Cheonmachong** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Downtown Silla royal-tomb landscape; Cheonmachong provides an accessible reconstructed tomb interior and artifact interpretation.
- **Hours/price:** Park and interior hours differ; use the city’s current admission page.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism — heritage](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=2304)

### 7) 🔭 **Cheomseongdae** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Seventh-century stone astronomical structure in the Wolseong heritage district.
- **Hours/price:** Exterior viewing is free; surrounding paths are public.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism — heritage](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=2304)

### 8) 🌙 **Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Excavated Silla palace garden and pond, especially popular after architectural lighting begins.
- **Hours/price:** Ticketed; verify seasonal closing and last entry on the city facilities page.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/)

### 9) 🌉 **Woljeonggyo Bridge** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE RECONSTRUCTION
- **What:** Reconstructed covered bridge across Namcheon, based on archaeological and documentary evidence.
- **Hours/price:** Exterior and riverside viewing are free; interior access has posted hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/)

### 10) 🌳 **Gyerim Forest, Wolseong, and Seokbinggo** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE WALK
- **What:** Connected central heritage landscape containing the Gyerim origin legend, Silla palace site, excavations, and Joseon ice storehouse.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor walking is free; excavation-viewing areas can close.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO Gyeongju Historic Areas](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/976/)

### 11) 🏯 **Gyochon Village and Choi Clan House** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Traditional neighborhood near Woljeonggyo centered on the historic Gyeongju Choi family house and craft/food businesses.
- **Hours/price:** Public streets are free; houses and businesses have separate hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/)

### 12) 🏺 **Gyeongju National Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL MUSEUM
- **What:** Essential Silla collection including royal-tomb finds and the Divine Bell of King Seongdeok.
- **Hours/price:** General admission is free; verify closure days and special-exhibition tickets.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju National Museum](https://gyeongju.museum.go.kr/eng/)

### 13) 🏛️ **Bunhwangsa and Hwangnyongsa site** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE WALK
- **What:** Bunhwangsa’s stone pagoda and the excavated Hwangnyongsa temple complex/history hall.
- **Hours/price:** Facilities have separate hours; temple access and museum exhibitions should be checked individually.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism heritage directory](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=2304)

### 14) 👑 **King Muyeol Tomb and Seoak tombs** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Royal tomb and nearby Silla burial landscape west of central Gyeongju.
- **Hours/price:** Verify city heritage-site hours and admission.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism heritage directory](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=2304)

### 15) 🌀 **Poseokjeong site** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Silla royal-pavilion site known for its surviving curved stone water channel.
- **Hours/price:** Ticketed heritage site; confirm current access.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism heritage directory](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?mnu_uid=2304)

## Villages, seowon, and mountain heritage

### 16) 🏘️ **Yangdong Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SITE
- **What:** Historic clan village jointly inscribed with Hahoe as the Historic Villages of Korea.
- **Hours/price:** Ticketed village; residents live here. Respect private homes and verify seasonal hours.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1324/) · [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/)

### 17) 📚 **Oksan Seowon and Dongnakdang** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE AREA
- **What:** Joseon Confucian academy and nearby historic house connected with scholar Yi Eon-jeok. Oksan Seowon is part of the UNESCO-listed *Seowon, Korean Neo-Confucian Academies*.
- **Hours/price:** Verify building access; some spaces are private or ceremonially restricted.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO Seowon](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1498/)

### 18) ⛰️ **Gyeongju Namsan trails** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL PARK
- **What:** Mountain landscape with Silla temple sites, carved Buddhas, pagodas, and tombs; routes vary greatly in length and difficulty.
- **Hours/price:** Free trails; daylight hiking only. Check national-park closures and route maps.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Korea National Park Service](https://www.knps.or.kr)

### 19) 🪨 **Samneung Valley and pine forest** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Popular Namsan route combining royal tombs, pine forest, and Buddhist remains.
- **Hours/price:** Free trail; use official park maps rather than walking across protected remains.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Korea National Park Service](https://www.knps.or.kr)

### 20) 🥋 **Golgulsa Temple and Sunmudo** — 🔎 VERIFIED TEMPLE
- **What:** Temple known for rock-carved Buddhist imagery and Sunmudo martial-meditation programs.
- **Hours/price:** Ordinary temple entry is free; performances, training, and templestays are scheduled separately.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Golgulsa](https://www.golgulsa.com) · [Templestay](https://www.templestay.com/en/)

### 21) 🛕 **Girimsa Temple** — 🔎 VERIFIED TEMPLE
- **What:** Historic temple on Hamwolsan in eastern Gyeongju.
- **Hours/price:** Ordinary entry is free; verify parking and temple notices.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Girimsa](http://www.kirimsa.net/)

## Coast and Bomun

### 22) 🌊 **Tomb of King Munmu at Daewangam** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Offshore rock formation traditionally identified as King Munmu’s sea burial site.
- **Hours/price:** Beach viewing is free; do not climb protected rocks or enter unsafe surf.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/)

### 23) 🏯 **Gameunsa temple site** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Temple ruins and prominent twin stone pagodas near the coast.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor heritage site; free unless posted otherwise.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/)

### 24) 🪨 **Yangnam Columnar Joint coast** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATURAL HERITAGE
- **What:** Coastal geological formations and walking path, including fan-shaped columnar joints.
- **Hours/price:** Free outdoor access; avoid stormy seas and closed deck sections.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/)

### 25) 🌅 **Bomun Lake loop** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Resort-district lakeside walking and cycling route with parks and cultural attractions.
- **Hours/price:** Public paths are free; hotels and attractions charge separately.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Tourism — Bomun district](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/tour/page.do?cmd=2&mnu_uid=2291&area_uid=31)

### 26) 🗼 **Gyeongju Expo Grand Park and Gyeongju Tower** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Cultural park with tower, exhibitions, landscaped grounds, and Solgeo Art Museum.

### 27) 🎢 **Gyeongju World** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Theme park in the Bomun district. Ride operation varies with temperature, wind, and maintenance.

### 28) 🖼️ **Solgeo Art Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Art museum within Expo Grand Park with collection and temporary exhibitions.
- **Hours/price:** Check park/museum ticket rules and current exhibitions.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeongju Expo Grand Park](https://www.cultureexpo.or.kr)
