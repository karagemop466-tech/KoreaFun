# 🛍️ Myeong-dong — Seoul's Shopping, Street-Food & Theater Quarter (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Seoul's pulse and most-visited tourist district — a 1 km² shopping, dining, and theater neighborhood in Jung-gu, between Seoul Plaza / Namdaemun to the west, Namsan Park to the south, and Cheonggyecheon stream to the east. Packed with hundreds of K-beauty shops, K-pop merchandise stores, street-food stalls, historic theaters, and Seoul's biggest **Myeong-dong Cathedral**.

> 🗺️ **This is a neighborhood of Seoul, not a separate city.** Cross-listed in [`seoul.md`](seoul.md) entry #211 (underground shopping). Paired with N Seoul Tower (seoul.md #33) and Namdaemun Market (seoul.md #61) for full surrounding context.

## 🛍️ Shopping — the headline (the reason people come)

### 1) 🛍️ Myeong-dong Main Street Mega Shopping District — ✅ daily
- **What:** Korea's premier pedestrianized retail corridor (~1 km long, 8 car-free blocks from Myeongdong Station to Lotte Department Store). 1,000+ shops stacked compounding 4–8 stories high — flagship K-beauty outlets (Innisfree, Etude House, Missha, The Face Shop, Laneige, Olive Young's flagship), K-pop idol album shops, fashion flagships (MIXXO, SPAO, 8 Seconds, ALAND), accessory boutiques, sunglasses, tourist souvenir emporiums
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–22:00** (some stores open until 23:00 / midnight)
- **Price:** **FREE** walk-in; purchase depends
- **Official source:** [english.visitseoul.net](https://english.visitseoul.net)
- **Beginner notes:** Most stores offer **tax refund** for purchases ≥ ₩30,000 per shop with passport. Bring your passport everywhere.

### 2) 🛍️ Myeong-dong Underground Shopping Center — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Climate-controlled subterranean retail arcade beneath Myeong-dong main street — K-pop album mega-stores, lightsticks, photocard booths, character merch, K-pop idol-fan specialty shops, jewelry, accessories, stationery, K-beauty bargains, traditional-style hair accessories. **Korea's largest K-pop album market** in particular.
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–22:00** (closed 2nd Tuesday of month for cleaning)
- **Price:** Free entry; pay per item
- **Official sources:** [english.visitseoul.net](https://english.visitseoul.net) · [seoul.go.kr](https://www.seoul.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Excellent rainy-day dry option.

### 3) 🏬 Shinsegae Myeong-dong Main Store (신세계 본점) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Flagship department store of Shinsegae — K-pop fan goods, beauty hall, traditional foods, designer brands, food court
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu **10:30–20:00** · Fri–Sun & holidays **10:30–20:30**
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [shinsegae.com](https://www.shinsegae.com)

### 4) 🏬 Lotte Department Store Myeong-dong Main (롯데백화점 본점) — 🔁 daily

## 🍢 Myeong-dong Street Food — the second-biggest reason (where the city's street food icons live)

### 16) 🥟 Myeong-dong **Sundae (Blood Sausage) King "명동 순대" — 🔁 daily**
- **What:** The single most famous *sundae-gukbap* (blood sausage soup with rice) shop in Korea, since **1969** — vegetable-stuffed blood sausage in clear pork broth with hand-sliced gluey rice cake
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–22:00
- **Price:** ~₩10,000 per bowl
- **Official sources:** [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Lines queue before lunch; tiny 30-seat shop tucked south of KFC. Locals claim outside competing brands are the same family.

## 🏛️ Religious & cultural sites

### 33) ⛪ **Myeong-dong Cathedral (서울 명동성당 주교좌) — 🔁 daily (FREE)**
- **What:** Korea's first Catholic parish (since **1882**) — the largest Catholic cathedral in Korea, designed by French Jesuit architects. **Korea's most beautiful Gothic Revival building** with two 45-meter stained-glass spires
- **Hours:** Daily **05:00–22:00** (open for evening mass; visitors welcome 09:00–22:00)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [mcs.or.kr](http://www.mcs.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Imposing during Christmas season, peaceful for quiet worshippers; entrance is by stairs; modest dress.

### 34) ⛪ **Myeongdong Cathedral Christmas Mass (Seasonal Extension) — 👀 WATCH**

## 🧭 Beginner-friendly essentials

- **Subway:** Line 4 **Myeongdong Station** (8 exits serving different sides), Line 2 **Euljiro 1-ga Station** (Myeong-dong Theater side). Bring T-money for unlimited transfers
- **Day-trip itinerary (Tokyo-style):** Seoul Plaza 09:00 coffee → walk to Myeongdong main 10:00 → K-beauty flagship tour 11:00 → Cathedral 12:30 → street food lunch 13:00 → Lotte DFS & Young Plaza 15:00 → Theater (NANTA) 17:00 → cheonggyecheon walk 19:00 → dinner 20:00
- **Halloween overlay:** if you're arriving Sat Oct 31, Myeongdong's K-bar & costume crowds are a great first-night orientation. Costume-strip in front of Lotte Cinema after 19:00
- **Tax refund:** Spend ≥ ₩30,000 per store for tax refund; ask for a Tax Free receipt; lump-sum claims made at airport / port
- **Currency:** Best rates at major bank branches during weekday business hours; tourist-only money-changers cost more
- **Apps:** Naver Map, Kakao T, Papago — the main 3
