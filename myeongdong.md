# 🛍️ Myeong-dong — verified neighborhood guide (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Myeong-dong is a neighborhood in **Seoul’s Jung-gu**, not a separate city. This file covers Myeong-dong itself and useful places within roughly a 20-minute walk: Hoehyeon/Namdaemun, Namsan’s north side, City Hall/Jeong-dong, and the nearest part of Euljiro.

This guide was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** from official organizer, venue/operator, Seoul City, Jung-gu, heritage, and national-tourism sources. The former 296-entry version mixed real landmarks with invented shops and festivals, obsolete brand locations, generic advice, hotels presented as activities, duplicates, and attractions elsewhere in Seoul.

> **Status**
> - ✅ **Confirmed event** — the organizer has published dates overlapping the trip.
> - 🔎 **Verified place/activity** — the place exists in a relevant official source; ordinary hours and prices can change.
> - ⏳ **Re-check** — the venue/program is real, but its November 2026 schedule or seasonal start date is not yet published.
>
> Store branches change quickly in Myeong-dong. A brand homepage is not proof that a particular branch still exists; this file retains only branches found in an official store listing or established official tourism directory.

## Confirmed in the trip window

### 1) 🎭 **Anthropolis IV: Iocaste — National Theater Company of Korea** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Oct 28–Nov 21, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Fourth installment of the National Theater Company’s five-part *Anthropolis* cycle, inspired by the Theban myths.
- **Where:** Myeongdong Theater (명동예술극장), directly in the shopping district.
- **When:** **Oct 28–Nov 21, 2026**. Use the performance page for individual performance days, language/accessibility information, prices, and booking.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [National Theater Company performance page](https://www.ntck.or.kr/ko/performance/info/257325)

### 2) 🖼️ **GanaArt Collection: Beneath Technology—Scenes at the Threshold** — ✅ CONFIRMED — through Nov 22, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Confirmed 2026 exhibition at the Seoul Museum of Art’s Seosomun Main Branch, an easy walk from Myeong-dong.
- **When:** **Apr 16–Nov 22, 2026**, covering the entire trip and closing on departure day.
- **Hours/price:** SeMA lists Tue–Thu 10:00–20:00, Friday to 21:00, and winter weekends/holidays 10:00–18:00. General admission is normally free; check the exhibition page for exceptions.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [SeMA Seosomun branch](https://sema.seoul.go.kr/en/visit/seosomun)

### 3) 🥁 **Myeongdong NANTA** — ⏳ RE-CHECK NOVEMBER 2026
- **What:** Long-running non-verbal percussion and comedy show in the Myeongdong NANTA Theatre.
- **Status:** The operator has published 2026 performances, but the search-visible calendar was not yet on November. Do **not** copy the November 2025 schedule into 2026.
- **Price:** Operator currently lists Premium ₩70,000, VIP ₩60,000, S ₩50,000, and A ₩40,000; verify when booking.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Myeongdong NANTA schedule](https://www.nanta.co.kr:452/en/show/detail.php?id=1) · [operator ticket information](https://www.nanta.co.kr:452/Nanta/en/ticket/ticketing.aspx)

## Myeong-dong core

### 4) 🚶 **Myeong-dong shopping streets** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Dense pedestrian shopping district between Myeongdong Station and Euljiro 1-ga, with fashion, cosmetics, department stores, restaurants, and evening food stalls.
- **Hours/price:** Public streets are free; every business sets its own hours. There is no single official “10:00–22:00” schedule for the whole district.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Seoul neighborhood guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/editorspicks/myeong-dong_/44370)

### 5) 🍢 **Myeong-dong evening street-food stalls** — 🔎 VERIFIED ACTIVITY
- **What:** Food carts operate along the main shopping streets; offerings and cart locations vary by day. Treat this as one market experience, not a separate attraction for every snack.
- **Hours/price:** Vendor-specific and weather-dependent. Check posted prices before ordering.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Seoul neighborhood guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/editorspicks/myeong-dong_/44370)

### 6) ⛪ **Myeongdong Cathedral** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Cathedral completed in 1898 and a major site in Korean Catholic history. The basement sanctuary contains relics of martyrs.
- **Access:** Enter only during visitor hours; sightseeing is restricted during Mass and services.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [cathedral](https://www.mdsd.or.kr) · [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/myeongdong-cathedral_/4036)

### 7) 📚 **1898 Myeongdong Cathedral cultural complex** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Bookstore, gallery, café, and shops in the underground complex beside the cathedral. This is not a separate “catacombs tour.”
- **Hours/price:** Tenant-specific; public circulation is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Seoul Myeong-dong guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/editorspicks/myeong-dong_/44370)

### 8) 🎭 **Myeongdong Theater** — 🔎 VERIFIED VENUE
- **What:** Historic theater operated by the National Theater Company of Korea. Entry to the auditorium requires a performance ticket.
- **Schedule:** Use the company calendar; the confirmed trip-window production is entry #1.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [National Theater Company performances](https://www.ntck.or.kr/ko/performance/info)

### 9) 🧭 **Myeong-dong Tourist Information Center** — 🔎 VERIFIED SERVICE
- **What:** Seoul-government visitor center with multilingual travel help and official souvenirs at 66 Eulji-ro.
- **Hours:** Visit Seoul lists 09:00–18:00, closed on Seollal and Chuseok; verify before travel.
- **Price:** Free visitor assistance.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Myeong-dong-Tourist-Information-Center_/11472)

### 10) 🛍️ **Seoul My Soul Shop — Myeong-dong** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · NEW
- **What:** Official Seoul-brand merchandise shop inside the tourist information center.
- **Hours:** Seoul City lists 09:00–18:00; closure wording varies between city notices, so check the current listing.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Seoul Metropolitan Government shop directory](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/amusement/seoul-my-soul-shop/)

### 11) 💄 **Olive Young Myeongdong Global** — 🔎 VERIFIED STORE
- **What:** Officially listed Olive Young branch at 53 Myeongdong-gil, useful as a consolidated K-beauty stop.
- **Hours/price:** Check the live store page; last admission is before closing.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Olive Young official store listing](https://m.oliveyoung.co.kr/m/mtn/store/information/D176)

### 12) 🏬 **Lotte Department Store Main Store** — 🔎 VERIFIED STORE
- **What:** Department store and food halls beside Euljiro 1-ga. Lotte Duty Free is a distinct tenant with its own eligibility and collection rules.

### 13) 🏬 **Shinsegae Main Store** — 🔎 VERIFIED STORE

### 15) 🛍️ **NOON Square** — 🔎 VERIFIED STORE

- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Seoul City Tour Bus route](https://www.seoulcitytourbus.co.kr/en/tour-course/traditional-culture)

### 18) 🍜 **Myeongdong Kyoja** — 🔎 VERIFIED RESTAURANT
- **What:** Named kalguksu and mandu restaurant at 29 Myeongdong 10-gil. This replaces multiple generic or fabricated noodle and dumpling “alley” entries.
- **Hours:** Official tourism listing says daily 10:30–21:00; re-check the restaurant page before going.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) · [restaurant](https://www.mdkj.co.kr)

- **What:** Long-running Myeong-dong restaurant specializing in gomtang. It can sell out before its nominal closing time.
- **Hours/price:** Verify with the restaurant; do not rely on old guidebook hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Seoul — Hadonggwan](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/Hadonggwan/ENP005614)

### 20) 🍲 **Da-dong and Mugyo-dong Food Culture Street** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA

## Free and rainy-day museums

### 21) 💴 **Bank of Korea Money Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Currency and economic-history museum in the historic Bank of Korea building opposite Shinsegae.
- **Hours/price:** Visit Seoul lists Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00, closed Monday, free. Check the Bank of Korea site for notices.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Bank of Korea museum](https://www.bok.or.kr/museum/main/main.do) · [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Bank-of-Korea-Money-Museum_/2733)

### 22) ✉️ **Korea Postage Stamp Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM · NEW
- **What:** Postal-history and stamp museum inside Post Tower/Seoul Central Post Office, directly beside Myeong-dong.
- **Hours/price:** Operator information should be checked at travel time; admission is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Korea Postage Stamp Museum](https://stampmuseum.kr) · [Seoul Central Post Office](https://english.visitseoul.net/attractions/Seoul-Central-Post-Office-en/ENP024591)

### 23) 🎨 **Seoul Museum of Art — Seosomun Main Branch** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Municipal contemporary-art museum beside Deoksugung. See entry #2 for the confirmed trip-window exhibition.

- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [SeMA branch page](https://sema.seoul.go.kr/en/visit/seosomun)

### 24) 🏙️ **Seoul Gallery beneath City Hall** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · NEW
- **What:** City exhibition and public space opened below Seoul City Hall in 2026.
- **Hours:** Seoul City lists Mon–Sat, 09:00–20:00 in November–February; closed Sunday and major holidays.

## Namsan side

### 25) 🏘️ **Namsangol Hanok Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Five restored hanok, traditional garden, craft spaces, and Seoul Namsan Gukakdang near Chungmuro Station.
- **Hours:** Official site lists November–March 09:00–20:00, closed Monday, with holiday exceptions.
- **Price:** Village admission is free; workshops and performances may cost extra.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Jung-gu Tourism](https://www.junggu.seoul.kr/tour/content.do?cmsid=14818&sf_category1=CWS1325&mode=view&page=&cid=980666029) · [venue](https://www.hanokmaeul.or.kr)

### 26) 🪁 **Namsangol traditional craft and game programs** — ⏳ RE-CHECK
- **What:** The village runs real on-site craft and traditional-game sessions, but program dates and availability change.
- **Hours/price:** Attend only a session shown on the official 2026 booking calendar.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Namsangol experiences](https://www.hanokmaeul.or.kr/en/h/exp/on-site-traditional-korean-games)

### 27) 🚶 **Jung-gu Namsan Memory Road guided walk** — 🔎 VERIFIED PROGRAM · NEW
- **What:** District-run historical walk from Namsangol Hanok Village through sites connected with Japanese colonial rule to the An Jung-geun Memorial Hall area.
- **Distance:** Jung-gu lists approximately 3 km and two hours.
- **Price/status:** Check availability and reserve through the district page; a route listing does not guarantee an English guide every day.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Jung-gu guided walks](https://www.junggu.seoul.kr/tour/content.do?cmsid=14956)

### 28) 🧱 **Namsan section of Hanyangdoseong (Seoul City Wall)** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Official city-wall trail over Namsan, accessible from Myeong-dong/Namsangol and continuing toward Sungnyemun or Jangchung.
- **Hours/price:** Namsan trail is listed as open 24 hours and free; use caution after dark and in poor weather.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Seoul City Wall — Namsan trail](https://seoulcitywall.seoul.go.kr/en/wallcourse/4.do)

### 29) 🚡 **Namsan Ormi and Namsan Cable Car** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATORS
- **What:** Free inclined elevator from the Hoehyeon side up to the cable-car area, followed by an optional paid cable-car ride.
- **Hours/price:** Check operating notices and weather conditions; Ormi and the cable car are separate systems.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [city-tour route description](https://www.seoulcitytourbus.co.kr/en/tour-course/night-traditional-culture) · [Namsan Cable Car](https://www.cablecar.co.kr)

### 30) 🗼 **N Seoul Tower** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Observation tower and commercial complex at the top of Namsan.
- **Hours/price:** Observatory hours and admission are operator-controlled; the surrounding Namsan park viewpoints are free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [N Seoul Tower](https://www.nseoultower.co.kr)

## City Hall, Jeong-dong, and Namdaemun walk

### 31) 🏯 **Deoksugung Palace** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Royal palace beside City Hall, walkable from Myeong-dong via Euljiro or Seosomun.
- **Hours/price:** Check Korea Heritage Service for current admission, Monday closure, last entry, and any evening program.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Royal Palaces and Tombs Center — Deoksugung](https://royal.khs.go.kr/ENG/contents/E101000000.do)

### 32) 🎭 **National Jeongdong Theater** — ⏳ RE-CHECK PERFORMANCE CALENDAR
- **What:** National performing-arts venue in the Jeong-dong cultural district behind Deoksugung.
- **Status:** The venue is verified, but no specific November 2026 production is included without a dated organizer page.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [venue](https://www.jeongdong.or.kr/eng/main/contents.do?menuNo=400006) · [performance calendar](https://www.jeongdong.or.kr/portal/bbs/B0000252/list.do?menuNo=200001)

### 33) 🚪 **Sungnyemun Gate** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Seoul’s historic south gate, beside Namdaemun Market and about 15 minutes from central Myeong-dong.
- **Hours/price:** City Wall portal lists winter hours 09:00–17:30, closed Monday; grounds are free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Seoul City Wall — Sungnyemun trail](https://seoulcitywall.seoul.go.kr/en/wallcourse/5.do)

### 34) 🛍️ **Namdaemun Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET
- **What:** Large wholesale and retail traditional market immediately west of Myeong-dong.
- **Hours/price:** Each building, shop, and food alley has its own schedule; many sections close Sunday. Do not rely on one market-wide opening time.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [market association](https://namdaemunmarket.co.kr) · [Visit Seoul](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/Namdaemun-Market_/86)

### 35) 🥢 **Namdaemun Market food sections** — 🔎 VERIFIED ACTIVITY
- **What:** Named market food areas and registered vendors, including kalguksu and galchi-jorim sections. This is one market-food activity, not many padded entries.
- **Hours/price:** Vendor-specific; verify on the market association directory.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Namdaemun Market food directory](http://namdaemunmarket.co.kr/03_eat/)

### 36) 🌊 **Cheonggyecheon from Euljiro 1-ga** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Restored public stream and walking path a few blocks north of Myeong-dong. Temporary light or festival installations require a separate dated city announcement.
- **Hours/price:** Public path is free; access can close during flooding or maintenance.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Seoul Facilities Corporation — Cheonggyecheon](https://www.sisul.or.kr/open_content/cheonggye/)

- invented food “alleys,” individual street snacks counted as separate attractions, and unnamed bars, cafés, karaoke rooms, spas, clinics, and photo studios;
- obsolete or unverified beauty and character-store branches presented as permanent flagships;
- hotels, SIM-card advice, apps, tax-refund instructions, transport tips, and itineraries counted as activities;
- duplicates for Myeongdong Cathedral, NANTA, Namsangol, N Seoul Tower, department stores, and nearby markets;
- dozens of attractions in Gangnam, Jamsil, Hongdae, Itaewon, Yongsan, Jongno, and other parts of Seoul that belong in `seoul.md`, not a Myeong-dong neighborhood file.
