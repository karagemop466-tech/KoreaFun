# 🎢 Yongin — Theme Parks & Folk Culture (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's fun capital** — home to **Everland** (the country's largest theme park), the **Korean Folk Village** (a living Joseon-era village), and Samsung Everland's cutting-edge infrastructures. Just 35 minutes south of Seoul via subway + Everline light rail.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Everland, Korean Folk Village, and Yongin's cafe streets.

**Official Yongin sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Yongin (official tourism): [yongin.go.kr](http://www.yongin.go.kr)
- Everland Theme Park: [everland.com](https://www.everland.com/web/en/index.html)
- Korean Folk Village: [koreanfolk.co.kr](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr)

**Getting there:** Seoul Gangnam (Line 3 → Giheung) → Everline Light Rail to **Jeondae-Everland Station** ≈ **60 min** door-to-door. Yongin is a 30 min taxi from Suwon.

## 🎃 Theme parks — Korea's flagship

### 1) 🎡 Everland Theme Park (에버랜드) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily operating; 50th anniversary & Halloween season likely
- **What:** Korea's largest theme park featuring **T-Express** (one of Asia's tallest wooden coasters), **Lost Valley** safari, **European Adventure** village, **Zootopia** animal shows, **Amazon Express** water ride, and the seasonal Halloween **Blood City** immersive horror zone (2026 = Everland 50th anniversary year)
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–21:00** (extended Fri–Sat); **Q-pass** priority entry available
- **Price:** Adult 1-day pass ~**₩62,000** (~$44, 2025 rate). 2026 TBA on the official site. Halloween nights often cost extra
- **Official sources:** [everland.com](https://www.everland.com/web/en/index.html) · [park-everland.com](https://park-everland.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Buy tickets online in advance via the official site or Klook/Trazy. **T-Express single rider** line is much faster. The safari bus is great even on cold days (climate-controlled).

### 2) 🏰 Everland Magic Spring Garden / Rose Garden — 🔁 always on within Everland
- **What:** Four-season European-style floral garden right in the center of Everland. Tulips in spring, sunflowers in summer, dahlias & cosmos in autumn, themed lights in winter.
- **Hours:** Park-wide hours
- **Price:** Included in Everland day pass
- **Official source:** [everland.com](https://www.everland.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Lovely photo op 30 min after entering the park.

### 3) 🏝️ Caribbean Bay Water Park (카리브베이) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Indoor/outdoor water park complex adjacent to Everland, with warm indoor wave pool, hot-spring spa, lazy river, and slides. Operates year-round with heated indoor pools.
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–17:00** (extended Fri–Sun to 18:00)
- **Price:** Adult pass ~**₩45,000–₩55,000** (~$32–40)
- **Official source:** [caribbeanbay.co.kr](https://www.caribbeanbay.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Buy combo ticket with Everland (~$90 combo) — perfect for a cold autumn evening.

### 4) 🎢 Yongin Samsung Bluewings Theme Park Nights (off-season) — 👀 WATCH
- **What:** Occasionally Suwon Samsung Bluewings (Yongin training site) holds special fan events; check [ss-bluewings.com](https://www.ss-bluewings.com)
- **Hours:** Variable
- **Price:** Tickets per event
- **Beginner notes:** Not a daily event — check in October.

### 5) 🎮 Samsung Kids' Tech Park (삼성키즈) — 🔁 Tue–Sun

### 6) 🦕 Samsung Human Tech (삼성 휴먼 테크) — 🔁 Tue–Sun

## 🇰🇷 Heritage & traditional culture

### 7) 🏘️ Korean Folk Village (한국민속촌) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Massive living Joseon-era village (b. 1973) with 250+ relocated traditional houses, a Joseon Living History Theater, farmers' music shows, mask dances, traditional wedding reenactments, and craft demonstrations (pottery, weaving, blacksmithing). Operates year-round with seasonal themes.
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:30–18:00** (Nov winter 9:30–17:30); closed Mondays
- **Price:** Adult **₩25,000** (~$18); K-pop Music Drama tickets extra (~₩30,000)
- **Official sources:** [koreanfolk.co.kr](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr) · [yongin.go.kr](http://www.yongin.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pair Suwon + Folk Village day trip. Shaded hanok house courtyards; full visit takes 4–6 hours. Take snacks for kids; in-park food pricey.

### 8) 🎭 Korean Folk Village K-Pop Music Drama (KPMD) — 👀 WATCH (Nov runs likely)
- **What:** Live-action K-pop historical musical revue combining K-pop hits with traditional Joseon plot; performed on outdoor festival stage
- **Hours:** Daily performances **14:00 & 16:00** (verify on official site)
- **Price:** Folk Village entry + KPMD ticket ~₩55,000 combo
- **Official source:** [koreanfolk.co.kr](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Crowded on weekends; arrive early.

### 9) 👘 Korean Folk Village Hanbok Rental Walk — 🔁 daily (with Folk Village entry)
- **What:** Rent Joseon-style hanbok inside the village (₩10,000–15,000) and roam the historical homes + gardens for the day
- **Hours:** Daily 09:30–17:00
- **Price:** Rental ₩10,000–15,000 (separate from park admission)
- **Official source:** [koreanfolk.co.kr](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The most atmospheric hanbok photo spot in Korea after Gyeongbokgung.

### 10) 🪈 Folk Village Traditional Weddings & Farming Music (Nongak) — 🔁 weekend performances
- **What:** Daily public reenactments of traditional farming music (nongak), mask dance performances, and Joseon wedding processions at scheduled times
- **Hours:** Weekends 11:00–15:00
- **Price:** Included with Folk Village admission
- **Official source:** [koreanfolk.co.kr](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pick up the day's schedule at the main entrance — they ring the gong 5 min before each show.

### 11) 🏘️ Yongin's Yongguksa Temple & Mountain Trail — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Quiet Buddhist temple nestled in Yongin's pine forest foothills (Giheung-gu); 2 km scenic loop from the folk village edge
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [yongin.go.kr](http://www.yongin.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 15 min by taxi from Folk Village — a restful wood-and-bell counterpart.

### 12) 🎍 Yongin Gatcobang (Traditional Straw Mat Weaving Workshop) — 👀 WATCH seasonal
- **What:** Folk Village hosted *gat* (traditional bamboo hat) weaving workshops near the artisan hamlet — schedule posted on the official site. Autumn is peak gat-coil season
- **Hours:** Variable
- **Price:** ₩5,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [koreanfolk.co.kr](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Ask at guest info; weekends more frequent.

## 🧭 Beginner-friendly essentials

- **Seoul day trip:** Seoul 09:00 subway → Giheung (Everline to Jeondae-Everland) → Everland 10:00–19:00 → Seoul 20:30. Buy Everland tickets online Friday evening to skip queues.
- **Folk Village day trip:** Seoul 09:00 subway → Folk Village 10:30 → 18:00 → Folk Village Evening Lights (seasonal) → Seoul 20:30.
- **Seoul ↔ Yongin transport:** Subtotal under ₩5,000 round trip (T-money). Direct Suin-Bundang Line from Wangsimni/Seoul Stn to Giheung available.
