# 🎢 Yongin — verified events and activities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Yongin is a large, spread-out city south of Seoul. Its major attractions are not clustered: Everland and Hoam Museum are in Cheoin-gu, Korean Folk Village and the Gyeonggi museum campus are in Giheung-gu, and Dae Jang Geum Park is much farther east near Baegam.

This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** from official organizer, operator, museum, city, and national-tourism sources. The former 167-entry version contained invented Samsung attractions, nonexistent sports teams and festivals, unnamed businesses, duplicates, and day trips outside Yongin.

> **Status**
> - ✅ **Confirmed event** — a dated organizer or venue page overlaps the trip.
> - 🔎 **Verified place/activity** — a real permanent place supported by an official source; ordinary hours and prices can change.
> - ⏳ **Re-check** — a real venue or seasonal program whose exact November 2026 operation is not confirmed.
>
> Yongin requires planning by area. Do not try to combine Everland, Korean Folk Village, and Dae Jang Geum Park in one day.

**Official planning links:** [Yongin Tourism](https://www.yongin.go.kr/yitour/index.do) · [Yongin event calendar](https://www.yongin.go.kr/user/web/eventyt/BD_selectClturEventPfmcytList.do) · [Everland](https://www.everland.com/everland/home/main) · [Korean Folk Village](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr)

## Confirmed during the trip

### 1) 🌙 **Korean Folk Village Evening Festival** — ✅ CONFIRMED — through Nov 15, 2026
- **What:** Extended evening opening with illuminated village streets and night programming at Korean Folk Village.
- **When:** The operator lists **Apr 11–Nov 15, 2026**, and — importantly — **evening opening runs only Fri, Sat, Sun and public holidays**, not every day. Your window has four usable weekends before it closes: Oct 31–Nov 1, Nov 6–8, Nov 13–15. **Nov 15 is the final night of the season.**
- **Also on:** the ambient night program **"Adding Moonlight" (달빛을 더하다)** runs alongside the ticketed horror attractions.
- **Price/hours:** Admission and closing time are date-specific; use the operator’s live ticket and operating-hours pages.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Korean Folk Village](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr/)

### 2) 👻 **Salgwiok (살귀옥 / A Haunted Prison)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — through Nov 15, 2026
- **What:** Ticketed horror experience inside Korean Folk Village.
- **When:** Official event listing shows **Jun 13–Nov 15, 2026**; the city notice lists weekend/holiday sessions. Verify the selected date and age restrictions before purchase.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [operator event page](https://m.koreanfolk.co.kr/home/promotion/event/2) · [Yongin City notice](https://www.yongin.go.kr/user/bbs/BD_selectBbs.do?q_bbsCode=1013&q_clCode=1&q_bbscttSn=20260410155434320)

### 8) 🎨 **Art Spectrum 2026** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Sep 1–Dec 27, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Hoam Museum of Art’s edition of Art Spectrum, **co-organized with Palais de Tokyo (Paris)** — the first time the 2001-founded series has been staged at Hoam rather than Leeum. **10 countries, 23 artists/teams** working across visual art, architecture, film, design, and music, shown in the galleries **and outdoors**. Expect a moon-like pavilion, skateboarders in a subterranean city, a collapsing house.
- **Confirmed title:** 《방이있고모든라디오가각기다른주파수를향하고있다》 (“There is a room and every radio is tuned to a different frequency”). An earlier draft of this guide flagged the title as provisional — it is now final.
- **When:** **Sep 1–Dec 27, 2026** — open your entire trip.
- **Side programs:** live music from bands of six Asian countries, an art-shop project, cooking workshops, and talks run across the exhibition period.
- **Price/hours:** Timed reservation and ticket details are controlled by the museum.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Leeum/Hoam](https://www.leeumhoam.org) · [Newsis, Jul 7 2026](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260707_0003698789)

### 9) ⚽ **Together, Kick Off: Our Football City Yongin** — ✅ CONFIRMED — through Dec 6, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Municipal exhibition at Yongin City Museum about the city’s football culture.
- **When:** **Mar 20–Dec 6, 2026** — confirmed on the city's event portal, which lists this as the single current exhibition-type event in Yongin.
- **Where/price:** **Yongin City Museum** (용인시박물관), 경기도 용인시 기흥구 동백3로 79 (opposite Dongbaek Library). **Free admission.** Open **Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00; closed Mondays**, Jan 1, and Seollal/Chuseok holidays.
- **⚠️ Phone-number note (Aug 17):** Yongin's event portal prints the contact as `031-6193-4796`, which is **not a valid Korean number** (the 031 area code does not take a 4-digit exchange in that form) — it appears to be a typo on the city's own listing. The museum's actual number, consistent across VisitKorea's accessibility database, Gyeonggi Cultural Foundation and the city's own tourism blog, is **031-324-4796**.
- **Context:** Yongin is a genuine football town — it hosts Yongin FC and the Yongin Mireu Stadium — so this is a local-pride exhibition rather than a general sports-history show.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin City event detail](https://www.yongin.go.kr/user/web/eventyt/BD_selectClturEventPfmcyt.do?q_clCode=2&q_esn=473)

## Theme parks and living history

### 10) 🎢 **Everland** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Major theme park with rides, gardens, animal areas, and seasonal entertainment. Individual rides can close for weather, maintenance, or low temperatures.
- **Hours/price:** Date-tiered tickets and daily operating hours must be checked in the Everland app/site. Do not assume 10:00–22:00 or a fixed ₩62,000 fare for every November date.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Everland](https://www.everland.com/everland/home/main)

### 11) 🎃 **Everland autumn/Halloween or winter transition programming** — ⏳ RE-CHECK
- **What:** Everland runs seasonal festivals, but the specific 2026 November theme and switch-over date require a current organizer announcement.
- **Status:** Older “Blood City,” Halloween, or Christmas dates are not evidence for 2026. Attend only programs shown in the live app/calendar.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Everland](https://www.everland.com/everland/home/main)

### 12) 💦 **Caribbean Bay** — ⏳ RE-CHECK NOVEMBER OPERATION
- **What:** Indoor/outdoor water park beside Everland. In cold months only selected zones and attractions may operate.
- **Hours/price:** Check the operator’s date-specific calendar; do not promise that all pools, slides, or evening shows run in November.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Caribbean Bay](https://www.everland.com/caribbeanbay/home/main)

### 13) 🏘️ **Korean Folk Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR

- **Hours/price:** The operator publishes date-specific hours and tickets. Evening programs through Nov 15 are listed separately above.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Korean Folk Village](https://www.koreanfolk.co.kr/) · [VISITKOREA overview](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=222783)

## Art and museums

### 15) 📺 **Nam June Paik Art Center** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Gyeonggi Cultural Foundation museum devoted to Nam June Paik and contemporary media art, on the same campus as the Gyeonggi Province and Children’s museums.
- **Hours/price:** Official foundation guide lists Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, closed Monday and designated holidays, free admission.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [museum](https://njp.ggcf.kr/) · [Gyeonggi Cultural Foundation guide](https://ggc.ggcf.kr/p/5f313cd7e3d2f456a195fa83)

### 16) 🏺 **Gyeonggi Province Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Provincial archaeology, history, and cultural-history museum beside the Nam June Paik Art Center.
- **Hours/price:** Check current exhibitions, reservations, and closure notices on the museum site; normally closed Monday.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeonggi Province Museum](https://musenet.ggcf.kr/)

### 17) 🧒 **Gyeonggi Children’s Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Hands-on children’s museum on the same campus. It is primarily designed for families with children, not a general art museum.
- **Hours:** Official site lists two timed sessions, 10:00–13:30 and 14:00–17:30, with advance booking rules.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gyeonggi Children’s Museum](https://gcm.ggcf.kr/) · [visitor information](https://gcm.ggcf.kr/boards/visit/articles/120)

### 18) 🏛️ **Hoam Museum of Art and Hee Won garden** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Samsung Foundation art museum and traditional-style garden near Everland. The museum uses timed reservations for exhibitions.
- **Hours/price:** Check the selected date and ticket on the official site; Everland admission is not included or required.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Hoam Museum](https://www.leeumhoam.org/hoam) · [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=111430)

### 19) 🚗 **Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance Mobility Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Automobile and mobility collection near Everland, with transport-history and safety exhibits.
- **Hours:** VISITKOREA lists weekdays 09:00–17:00, weekends 10:00–18:00, closed Monday; check the operator for current tickets and closures.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [museum](https://www.stm.or.kr) · [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=62798)

### 20) 🪔 **Korean Deungjan (Traditional Lamp) Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Specialist museum of Korean lamps and lighting culture near the Jeong Mong-ju tomb area.
- **Hours:** VISITKOREA lists Oct–Mar 10:00–17:00, closed Monday and Tuesday; verify before making the trip.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [museum](http://www.deungjan.org/) · [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/enu/ATR/SI_EN_3_1_1_1.jsp?cid=822930)

### 21) 🏛️ **Yongin City Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Municipal history museum; see entry #9 for the confirmed football exhibition.
- **Hours/price:** Check the museum page for current hours and holiday closures.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin City Museum](https://www.yongin.go.kr/museum/index.do)

## Film, heritage, and religious sites

### 22) 🎬 **Yongin Dae Jang Geum Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** MBC historical-drama production set open to visitors, with architecture representing multiple eras and costume/photo activities.
- **Hours:** VISITKOREA lists Nov–Feb 09:00–17:00; confirm filming restrictions and last admission before the long trip to Baegam.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [MBC park](https://djgpark.imbc.com) · [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=66163)

### 23) 🛕 **Waujeongsa Temple** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Buddhist temple known for a large outdoor Buddha head and reclining Buddha image.
- **Hours/price:** Active religious site; visit respectfully and check city/operator notices rather than assuming unrestricted access.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism attraction directory](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour04/ytsub02/ytsubmn03_01.jsp)

### 24) 🪦 **Jeong Mong-ju tomb and Chungnyeol Seowon area** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE AREA
- **What:** Tomb and Confucian memorial sites connected with the late-Goryeo scholar and official Jeong Mong-ju.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor heritage access; verify cultural-property notices and any guided program.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism cultural heritage directory](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour04/ytsub02/ytsubmn04.jsp)

### 25) 🏯 **Simgok Seowon** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Confucian academy associated with scholar Jo Gwang-jo, listed in Yongin’s official cultural-heritage directory.
- **Hours/price:** Check access before travel; some seowon buildings may not be continuously open.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism cultural heritage directory](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour04/ytsub02/ytsubmn04.jsp)

### 26) 🧱 **Cheoinseong Fortress site** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Historic earthen fortress associated with resistance to the Mongol invasions, preserved as an archaeological and commemorative site.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor site; free unless a special program states otherwise.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism cultural heritage directory](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour04/ytsub02/ytsubmn04.jsp)

### 27) 🚶 **Young Kim Dae-geon pilgrimage trail and Euni Sanctuary** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** City-promoted pilgrimage/walking route connected with Korea’s first Catholic priest, Andrew Kim Dae-geon.
- **Hours/price:** Trail is free; sanctuary buildings and stamp-tour services have their own hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism — Kim Dae-geon trail](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour03/ytsub01/ytexper02.jsp)

### 28) 🧘 **Beomnyunsa or Hwaunsa templestay** — ⏳ RE-CHECK PROGRAM
- **What:** Yongin Tourism lists both temples for templestay experiences. A temple’s existence does not guarantee an English or trip-window program.
- **Price/hours:** Book only a dated program shown by the official Templestay platform.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism experience directory](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour02/yttema03/yttemamn05_07.jsp) · [Templestay](https://www.templestay.com/en/)

## Forests, gardens, and local neighborhoods

### 29) 🌲 **Yongin Natural Recreation Forest** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Forest trails, playgrounds, camping, and reservable lodging on Jeonggwangsan.
- **Hours/price:** VISITKOREA lists park hours 09:00–17:00 and winter last admission 16:00, with admission and parking fees. Lodging requires ForestTrip reservation.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [VISITKOREA](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=87426) · [ForestTrip](https://www.foresttrip.go.kr)

### 30) 🌿 **Hantaek Botanical Garden** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Large private botanical garden in Baegam-myeon with themed plant collections and walking paths.
- **Hours/price:** Seasonal; check the garden’s live visitor page before traveling to eastern Yongin.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Hantaek Botanical Garden](https://www.hantaek.co.kr) · [Yongin Tourism](https://www.yongin.go.kr/tour/trrsrt/BD_selectTrrsrtMng.do?q_tsn=6)

### 31) 🌾 **Yongin Rural Theme Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Municipal agriculture-themed park and experience facilities in Wonsam-myeon.
- **Hours/price:** Programs and some facilities require city reservation; November offerings may be limited.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Rural Theme Park](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour01/yttour05_03.jsp)

### 32) 🚶 **Giheung Lake Park loop** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Reservoir-side walking and cycling route named among Yongin’s official eight scenic places.
- **Hours/price:** Public outdoor route; free. Use daylight and follow any construction detours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism portal](https://www.yongin.go.kr/yitour/index.do)

### 33) ⛰️ **Seoksungsan or Gwanggyosan hiking** — 🔎 VERIFIED ACTIVITY
- **What:** Two mountains listed among Yongin’s official scenic places. Seoksungsan is known for sunrise views; Gwanggyosan spans the Suwon/Yongin boundary.
- **Hours/price:** Free trails; daylight hiking recommended. Check weather and trail notices.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism portal](https://www.yongin.go.kr/yitour/index.do)

### 34) ☕ **Bojeong-dong Café Street** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Named café and restaurant street near Jukjeon/Bojeong, recognized in Yongin’s official tourism directory.
- **Hours/price:** Public street is free; every business sets its own hours. No Halloween or market event is included without a 2026 organizer notice.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism attraction directory](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour01/yttour07.jsp)

### 35) 🛍️ **Yongin Jungang Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET
- **What:** Traditional market in central Yongin, suitable for combining with the city museum or Everline rather than creating unnamed food-district entries.
- **Hours/price:** Vendor-specific; check the official market listing.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yongin Tourism shopping directory](https://www.yongin.go.kr/home/yitour/ytour01/yttour03/yttourmn05.jsp)

### 36) 🎭 **Yongin Poeun Art Hall and city performance calendar** — ⏳ RE-CHECK
- **What:** 1,525-seat municipal performing-arts venue by Jukjeon Station.
- **Status:** No November production is treated as confirmed without a dated 2026 performance page.
