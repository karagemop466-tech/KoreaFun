# 🏯 Suwon — verified events and activities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Suwon is a large city south of Seoul centered on the UNESCO-listed Hwaseong Fortress. The historic core is around Hwaseong Haenggung and Paldalmun, while Gwanggyo Lake Park, the arboretums, Samsung facilities, and sports venues are in other districts.

This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** using official organizer, venue/operator, Suwon City, Suwon Cultural Foundation, museum, league, and heritage sources. The former 211-entry version contained fabricated festivals and attractions, unnamed businesses, duplicate fortress components, out-of-city padding, and ordinary travel advice counted as activities.

> **Status**
> - ✅ **Confirmed event** — a dated organizer or venue page overlaps the trip.
> - 🔎 **Verified place/activity** — a real permanent place supported by an official source; hours and prices can change.
> - ⏳ **Re-check** — a real venue/program whose exact November schedule is not yet confirmed.
>
> Suwon’s named autumn festivals and royal procession take place **before** this trip in 2026. They are not moved into November based on patterns from older years.

## Hwaseong Fortress and historic center

### 9) 🧱 **Suwon Hwaseong Fortress wall circuit** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SITE
- **What:** Fortified late-18th-century city wall built under King Jeongjo. The complete circuit is approximately 5.7 km and includes steep sections on Paldalsan.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor wall paths are generally free and continuously accessible; museums, palace, experiences, and restricted structures have separate hours.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO World Heritage](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/817/) · [Visit Suwon](https://www.suwon.go.kr/sw-www/sw-visitsuwon/sw-visitsuwon-01/sw-visitsuwon-01-01/sw-visitsuwon-01-01-01.jsp)

### 10) 👑 **Hwaseong Haenggung** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Restored temporary royal palace used by King Jeongjo during visits to Suwon.
- **Hours/price:** Check the Cultural Foundation for current admission and last entry. Entry #2 covers the final two nights of the 2026 seasonal night opening.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Suwon Cultural Foundation — Haenggung](https://www.swcf.or.kr/english/?p=37)

### 11) 🚪 **Janganmun, Paldalmun, Changnyongmun, and Hwaseomun gates** — 🔎 VERIFIED FORTRESS FEATURES
- **What:** The four principal gates of Hwaseong. They are components of one fortress visit, not four separate padded activities.
- **Hours/price:** Exterior viewing and connected wall paths are free; road traffic passes around or through some gate areas.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Suwon fortress introduction](https://www.suwon.go.kr/sw-www/sw-visitsuwon/sw-visitsuwon-01/sw-visitsuwon-01-01/sw-visitsuwon-01-01-01.jsp)

### 12) 🌊 **Hwahongmun and Banghwasuryujeong** — 🔎 VERIFIED FORTRESS FEATURES
- **What:** Water gate and northeast pavilion overlooking Yongyeon pond, among the most distinctive sections of the wall.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor viewing is free; use care on stone steps and after dark.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Suwon seasonal route](https://www.suwon.go.kr/sw-www/sw-visitsuwon/sw-visitsuwon-02/sw-visitsuwon-02-06/sw-visitsuwon-02-06-04.jsp)

### 13) ⛰️ **Paldalsan and Seojangdae** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Steeper western fortress section leading to the command post and broad city views.
- **Hours/price:** Free outdoor trail. November leaves and rain can make stone and timber steps slippery.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Suwon fortress introduction](https://www.suwon.go.kr/sw-www/sw-visitsuwon/sw-visitsuwon-01/sw-visitsuwon-01-01/sw-visitsuwon-01-01-01.jsp)

### 14) 🏹 **Traditional Korean archery at Yeonmudae** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Supervised ten-arrow traditional archery experience beside the eastern command post.
- **Hours/price:** Cultural Foundation lists winter hours 09:30–17:00 and ₩3,000 for ten arrows; it does not operate in rain or snow. Re-check maintenance notices.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Suwon Cultural Foundation experience information](https://www.swcf.or.kr/english/?p=13&mode=view&idx=334&rIdx=99999204)

### 15) 🚂 **Hwaseong Eocha tourist trolley** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Paid tourist trolley linking major fortress stops; useful for visitors who cannot comfortably walk the full circuit.
- **Hours/price:** Timetable, route, and operating status vary. The foundation lists adult tickets from ₩6,000.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Suwon Cultural Foundation trolley information](https://www.swcf.or.kr/english/?p=38)

### 16) 🎈 **Flying Suwon tethered balloon** — ⏳ WEATHER-DEPENDENT

- day trips and attractions in Yongin, Hwaseong, Anyang, Icheon, and Yeoju.
