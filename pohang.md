# 🌅 Pohang — Sunrise Steel City & Space Walk (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's coastal steel capital** — home to **POSCO Steel** and the iconic **Hand of Homigot Sunrise Square**. Pohang has reinvented itself into a coastal arts + industry tourism hub featuring the viral **"Space Walk"** observation sculpture and sleepy, photogenic fishing villages. Perfectly placed between Gyeongju and the East Sea coast.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering **Space Walk**, **Homigot Sunrise Square**, **Yeongildae Beach**, and **Jukdo Market**.

## 🌅 Coast — the iconic sunrise

### 1) 🌅 Homigot Sunrise Square & "Hand of Harmony" — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Korea's east-meets-where-the-sun-rises. Famous **Hand of Harmony** sculpture (paired giant hands, one rises from the sea, the other from land) — about 4 km long boardwalk leading out to the rocks
- **Hours:** Open **24/7** daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Best at dawn (or night with reflective stars). The 4 km boardwalk is the easy option.

### 2) 🚶 **Space Walk** (스페이스워크) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** 333-meter-long elevated steel-pedestrian sculpture/ramp near Hwanho Park — Korea's first "art rollout" sculpture. Voted a cultural highlight; STAR WALK-themed (sci-fi)
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (Nov winter hours may close earlier)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [spacewalk.pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Goes up to 25m above the ground. Architectural and Instagram-iconic at sunset.

### 3) 🌊 Yeongildae Beach (영일대 해수욕장) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Iconic city beach with the **Camellia Hill** park and a long 1.6 km boardwalk. Famous for **Saptari poshing** camellias in early winter — the world's only coastal camellia garden
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 8-min walk from Pohang Station.

### 4) 🌉 Hwanho Ocean Bridge Park — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Newly built seaside park joining Yeongildae Beach and the Space Walk
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Best for sunset.

### 5) 🌿 Camellia Forest (동점 వన) — 🔁 daily in November (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's largest **saptari camellia grove** — produces flowers in late Oct/early Nov in red, white and pink
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–17:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Camellia blossoms overlap your first week.

### 6) 🛶 Guryongpo Seafood & Sunset Pier — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Coastal boat harbor and fishing village ~25 km east of city — cluster of seafood restaurants; *Hupo* in Guryongpo
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–midnight
- **Price:** Free walking; meals ~₩15,000–40,000
- **Beginner notes:** 30 min by taxi; take the **Oego** coastal route
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)

### 7) 🌊 Norae Coast Cuttlefish (꼴뚜기) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Famous *Kkolddugi* (cuttlefish) cooking at Norae Coast Village
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–22:00
- **Price:** ₩10,000–30,000
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 200 from downtown; 1 hr each way.

- **What:** Pohang sits on the **East Coast Cycling Route**, part of Korea's certified national bike network running from Goseong to Busan
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**; rental from ₩15,000/day
- **Official source:** [riverguide.go.kr](https://www.riverguide.go.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Certification stamp booths at Homigot and Yeongildae. **The Homigot section is the scenic highlight.**

### 184) 🎡 Pohang Children's & Family Facilities — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Pohang's children's museum, the science centre and the Hwanho Park play areas — indoor options for a cold or wet day
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**–₩5,000
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Space Walk itself has a minimum height requirement — check before promising it to small children.

### 185) 🏛️ Pohang Modern History & POSCO Museum — 🔁 weekdays
- **What:** The story of how a fishing village became a steel city after 1968, told at the POSCO history hall and the city's exhibitions
- **Hours:** Weekdays **09:00–17:00**; booking advised
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [posco.co.kr](https://www.posco.co.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Pohang's population went from 50,000 to 500,000 in thirty years** because of this plant.

### 186) ☕ Pohang Cafe Streets — Yeongildae & Hyoja — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Sea-view cafes along Yeongildae Beach and the independent cafe cluster in Hyoja-dong near POSTECH
- **Hours:** Roughly **10:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Coffee **₩5,000–₩7,000**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **The Yeongildae seafront cafes look straight at the lit steelworks after dark.**

### 187) 🍺 Pohang Nightlife & Yukhoe Alley — 🔁 nightly
- **What:** Pohang's evening districts around Jukdo Market and Yukdaemyeong, with raw-beef restaurants, pubs and late seafood
- **Hours:** Roughly **17:00–02:00**
- **Price:** **₩15,000–₩30,000** per person
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Jukdo Market's second floor stays open late** for fish and drinks — cheaper than the seafront.

### 188) 🛍️ Pohang Shopping — Jukdo, Lotte & downtown — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Jukdo Market for food and goods, plus a Lotte department store and the downtown Yukgeori shopping district
- **Hours:** Market **06:00–21:00**; stores **10:30–20:00**
- **Price:** **FREE** entry
- **Official source:** [lotteshopping.com](https://www.lotteshopping.com) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Dried seafood and gwamegi vacuum-packed for travel are the standard Pohang souvenirs.

### 189) 🏨 Pohang Accommodation — beach vs downtown — 🔁 planning
- **What:** Yeongildae Beach for sea views, downtown near Jukdo for food and buses, and Guryongpo for the fishing-village experience
- **Hours:** Check-in typically **15:00**
- **Price:** From **₩45,000** per night
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Yeongildae is the best base** — the beach, the cafes and the bus routes are all there.

### 190) ♨️ Pohang Bathhouses & Seawater Spas — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Jjimjilbang in central Pohang and seawater bathhouses near the beach, useful after a coastal walk in the wind
- **Hours:** Many **24 hours**
- **Price:** **₩10,000–₩15,000**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Seawater baths are a local speciality on the east coast.

### 191) 🚑 Pohang Emergency & Medical Services — 🔁 24 h
- **What:** Pohang St Mary's and Pohang Semyeong Christian hospitals run 24-hour emergency departments
- **Hours:** Emergency **24 h**
- **Price:** Consultation from **₩20,000** for visitors
- **Official source:** [1339 Medical Hotline](https://www.kdca.go.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **119 for ambulance, 1339 for English medical advice.**

### 192) 🌏 Pohang for foreign visitors — language & payment — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Pohang sees relatively few foreign tourists; English signage exists at major sites but market stalls are cash and Korean only
- **Hours:** N/A
- **Price:** N/A
- **Official source:** [1330 Hotline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr) · [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org)
- **Beginner notes:** **Carry cash for Jukdo Market.** Dial 1330 for free 24-hour interpretation.

### 193) 🌡️ Pohang Wind, Sea State & Cancellation Risk — 🔁 planning
- **What:** The Space Walk, the harbour cruise, the Ulleungdo ferry and the sea boardwalk all close in strong wind — common on this coast in November
- **Hours:** N/A
- **Price:** N/A
- **Official source:** [weather.go.kr](https://www.weather.go.kr) · [Korea Maritime Safety](https://www.komsa.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Have an indoor plan.** The steel art museum, the lighthouse museum and Jukdo Market all work in bad weather.

### 194) 🗺️ Pohang & Gyeongju combined itinerary — 🔁 planning
- **What:** Gyeongju and Pohang are 40 minutes apart; a common plan is Gyeongju heritage by day and Pohang coast and seafood by evening
- **Hours:** Buses roughly every 20 min
- **Price:** Bus **₩4,500**
- **Official source:** [kobus.co.kr](https://www.kobus.co.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Base in Gyeongju, day-trip to Pohang** — or the reverse if you want the sea view from your window.

### 195) 📸 Pohang Photo Spots — the definitive list — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Space Walk against the sky, the Hands of Harmony at sunrise, Yeongildae pavilion with the lit steelworks, and Oeosa's reflected maples
- **Hours:** Best at sunrise and after dark
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Sunrise at Homigot around 07:00 in November** and the steelworks after 18:00 are the two unmissable shots.
