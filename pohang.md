# 🌅 Pohang — Sunrise Steel City & Space Walk (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's coastal steel capital** — home to **POSCO Steel** and the iconic **Hand of Homigot Sunrise Square**. Pohang has reinvented itself into a coastal arts + industry tourism hub featuring the viral **"Space Walk"** observation sculpture and sleepy, photogenic fishing villages. Perfectly placed between Gyeongju and the East Sea coast.

## 🏛️ Culture & history

### 5) 🏛️ Pohang Museum (포항역사관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Regional museum covering prehistoric to the modern era: Bronze Age tombs, ancient textile traditions, POSCO's industrial era
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:30–17:30** (Nov)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohangmuseum.kr](http://www.pohangmuseum.kr)

### 6) 🚶 **Space Walk (스페이스워크)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Hwanho Park
- **What:** Pohang's landmark: a **333 m walkable steel sculpture with 717 steps**, curling like a rollercoaster over the ridge of Hwanho Park. You climb it on foot — it's public art, not a ride — for a panorama over Yeongil Bay, Yeongildae Beach, the city and the steelworks.
- **Hours (winter, which is your trip):** **Nov–Mar: weekdays 10:00–17:00; weekends & holidays 10:00–18:00.** (Apr–Oct runs to 20:00/21:00.) **Closed the first Monday of each month.**
- **Price:** **FREE** — no reservation, and **parking is free** too
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · ☎ Buk-gu Parks & Greenery 054-270-5176
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 9000 from Pohang Station to Hwanho Park (~25 min), or 15 min by car, then **a ~10 min uphill walk** from the car park. Capacity is capped at 150 people, so there can be a wait at sunset. **Closed immediately in strong wind or rain**, and the circular loop section is not walkable. Wear trousers and trainers; under-12s need an adult; minimum height 110 cm. Donated to the city by POSCO. Not for anyone afraid of heights — the structure genuinely moves underfoot.
- **⚠️ Plan around the winter hours:** at 17:00 on a weekday it closes **before sunset** in November — go on a **Saturday or Sunday** if you want the sunset/night view, or come mid-afternoon on a weekday.

### 10) 🎨 Pohang Steel Art Museum (스틸아트 뮤지엄) — ✅ CONFIRMED — Tue–Sun

- **Official source:** [steelartmuseum.com](http://www.steelartmuseum.com)

### 11) 🌊 **Yeongildae Pavilion & Beach Night View (영일대해수욕장)** — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Pohang's city beach, and the **Yeongildae pavilion built out over the water on a footbridge** — the classic Pohang photo. Looking across the bay you see the **POSCO steelworks lit up at night**, which is the view the city is known for.

- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** About 10 min by taxi from Pohang Station, and walkable to Hwanho Park/Space Walk. **The steelworks at night is genuinely the local sight** — best after full dark. Cafes and a *hoe* (raw fish) strip line the beach road.

### 34) 🏪 Jukdo Market (죽도시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** **The largest traditional market on Korea's east coast** — over 1,200 stalls, dominated by the seafood section and the raw-fish alley
- **Hours:** Roughly **06:00–21:00**; some stalls closed 2nd & 4th Sunday
- **Price:** **FREE** to browse; sashimi from ₩30,000
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org)
- **Beginner notes:** **Buy fish at a stall, then pay ₩5,000–₩10,000 per person to have it prepared and eaten upstairs.**

### 35) 🐟 Pohang Mulhoe — cold raw fish soup (물회) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Pohang's signature dish: raw fish in a chilled, sweet-sour chilli broth with vegetables and ice, eaten with noodles or rice
- **Hours:** Most **10:00–21:00**
- **Price:** **₩15,000–₩25,000**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Traditionally a fisherman's dish** eaten on the boat. Jukdo Market and the Yeongildae seafront are the best places for it.

### 36) 🦑 Guryongpo Gwamegi — half-dried herring (과메기) — ✅ CONFIRMED — winter speciality
- **What:** **Gwamegi season runs from November to February** — Pacific saury or herring freeze-dried in the sea wind at Guryongpo, eaten wrapped in seaweed and lettuce
- **Hours:** Season **Nov–Feb**; restaurants **11:00–22:00**
- **Price:** **₩25,000–₩35,000** per plate
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Your November window is the very start of gwamegi season** — this is Pohang's most distinctive seasonal food.

### 37) 🏘️ Guryongpo Japanese House Street (구룡포 일본인가옥거리) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A preserved street of 1920s–30s Japanese fishing-company houses at Guryongpo, used as a location for the drama *Pachinko*
- **Hours:** **Street open 24 h.** The **Guryongpo Modern History Museum** inside it (the restored 1920s Hashimoto house) opens **10:00–17:30, last entry 17:00, closed Mondays**
- **Price:** **Both FREE** — street and museum. Free parking at Guryongpo harbour opposite
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr/phtour](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour) · ☎ 054-276-9605
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 200 from Pohang (~50 min). The museum is a two-storey Japanese timber house built by Hashimoto Zengichi, who **shipped the building materials over from Kagawa**; Pohang City bought it in 2010 and restored it. **Only 15 people inside at once** and the second floor is closed for safety, so there can be a short wait. This was also a filming location for the drama *When the Camellia Blooms* — the stone staircase is the photo spot. **Combine with gwamegi at the port** — that's the whole point of coming out here.

- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org)

### 39) 🏭 POSCO Pohang Steelworks Night View (포항제철소 야경) — 🔁 nightly (FREE to view)
- **What:** **One of the world's largest steel mills**, lit at night into what locals call the steel-flower view — best from Yeongildae Beach or Hwanho Park
- **Hours:** Best after **18:00**

- **Official source:** [posco.co.kr](https://www.posco.co.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **The single most Pohang thing you can look at.** Plant tours require advance booking and are often unavailable.

### 40) 🎨 Pohang Museum of Steel Art (포항시립미술관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** **Korea's only museum specialising in steel art**, in Hwanho Park, reflecting Pohang's identity as the country's steel city
- **Hours:** **Winter (Nov–Mar) 10:00–18:00**; summer (Apr–Oct) 10:00–19:00. **Last entry 30 min before closing. Closed Mondays**, Jan 1, and Seollal/Chuseok day
- **Price:** **FREE** (special ticketed exhibitions occasionally carry a charge)
- **Official source:** [poma.pohang.go.kr](https://poma.pohang.go.kr) · ☎ 054-270-4700
- **Beginner notes:** Directly beside Space Walk — do both in one trip up the hill. **Free English-friendly docent tours** run weekdays 11:00 & 15:00, weekends 11:00 / 14:00 / 16:00. Opened 2009 as Gyeongsangbuk-do's first public art museum and **the only museum in Korea specialising in steel art** — a genuine fit for the city that POSCO built. Address 환호공원길 10.

### 41) 🌲 Hwanho Park & Skywalk (환호공원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)

### 42) ⛰️ Naeyeonsan Provincial Park & Twelve Waterfalls (내연산) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A gorge north of Pohang holding **twelve named waterfalls**, including the 30 m Yeonsan Falls, on a well-graded valley path
- **Hours:** Always open; allow 4 hours
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [Korea Forest Service](https://www.forest.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **One of Korea's best autumn valley walks** and peak colour is early-to-mid November. Bus 5000 from Pohang.

### 43) 🛕 Bogyeongsa Temple (보경사) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A 602 CE temple at the mouth of the Naeyeonsan gorge, the starting point for the twelve waterfalls walk
- **Hours:** Daily, roughly **sunrise–sunset** (temple grounds); the valley trail is unrestricted
- **Price:** **FREE — and so is parking.** Pohang City **abolished the ₩2,000–3,500 cultural-heritage admission on May 4, 2023**, with the government reimbursing the temple. Older guides (and the previous version of this file) still quote a fee — there isn't one.
- **Official sources:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · Bogyeongsa County Park info ☎ 054-240-7555 · Pohang tourism ☎ 054-270-2373
- **Beginner notes:** The **Naeyeonsan Twelve Waterfalls** walk starts at the temple: Sangsaeng → Gwaneum → Yeonsan falls, about **7.5 km round trip in 2.5–3 hours**, and the valley is the real attraction rather than the summit. The Sogeumgang viewpoint looks down over the Seonildae cliffs. **Go on a weekday in November** — weekends are very busy in foliage season.

- **Beginner notes:** **A well-executed urban regeneration** — walk it from downtown out toward Hyoja.

### 51) 🎨 Deokdong Cultural Village & Old Houses (덕동문화마을) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 400-year-old Yeoheung Min clan village in a wooded valley, with old pavilions, a preserved forest and traditional houses
- **Hours:** Daylight hours
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **The Deokdong forest is a designated Natural Monument.** Combine with Naeyeonsan on the same northern loop.

### 52) 🌾 Pohang Yeonorang Seonyeo Park (연오랑세오녀 테마공원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A coastal park built around the *Samguk Yusa* legend of Yeono-rang and Seo-nyeo, who sailed to Japan and took the sun and moon with them
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org)
- **Beginner notes:** On the Homigot road. Sea views, a pavilion and a good stop between Pohang and Guryongpo.

### 53) 🏛️ Pohang New Millennium Memorial Hall & Observatory — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** A hilltop memorial hall and observation deck at Homigot, giving the widest view of the cape and the East Sea
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Beside the Hands of Harmony. Free and worth the extra ten minutes.

### 54) 🥚 Homigot Sunrise Festival & New Year Events — 👀 WATCH — 31 Dec / 1 Jan
- **What:** Homigot hosts one of Korea's largest New Year sunrise gatherings, with a free communal *tteokguk* soup for tens of thousands
- **Hours:** 31 Dec–1 Jan
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Well outside your November window** — but the cape itself is open and empty in November, which is arguably better.

### 55) 🎣 Pohang Sea Fishing & Charter Boats — 🔁 daily (weather-dependent)
- **What:** Charter fishing from Guryongpo and Pohang harbour for rockfish and squid, with autumn among the better seasons
- **Hours:** Departures typically **06:00** and **13:00**
- **Price:** From **₩60,000** per person
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [Korea Maritime Safety](https://www.komsa.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Book through the harbour offices. **Life jackets are mandatory and checked.**

### 56) 🦐 Pohang Seafood — snow crab & squid — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The east-coast catch: snow crab from Guryongpo, fresh squid, and the winter cod season beginning in late autumn
- **Hours:** Markets **06:00–20:00**
- **Price:** Snow crab **₩50,000–₩120,000** depending on size
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Snow crab is sold by weight** — agree the price before it's cooked. Jukdo Market is the main source.

### 57) 🏟️ Pohang Steelers — K League 1 (포항스틸러스) — 👀 WATCH — season ends Nov/Dec
- **What:** **Korea's oldest professional football club, founded 1973**, and the most successful in AFC Champions League history among Korean clubs
- **Hours:** Kick-off typically **14:00** or **16:30**
- **Price:** Tickets from **₩10,000**
- **Official source:** [steelers.co.kr](https://www.steelers.co.kr) · [kleague.com](https://www.kleague.com)
- **Beginner notes:** **Steelyard is Korea's first football-specific stadium** and one of the best atmospheres in the league. Check November 2026 fixtures.

### 58) 🏟️ Pohang Steel Yard Stadium (포항스틸야드) — 🔁 daily (FREE outside matches)
- **What:** Opened 1990 as **Korea's first purpose-built football stadium**, funded by POSCO, with stands right on top of the pitch
- **Hours:** Grounds always open
- **Price:** **FREE** to view
- **Official source:** [steelers.co.kr](https://www.steelers.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from downtown. Small (17,000) and steep — a proper football ground.

### 59) 🎓 POSTECH Campus & Science Facilities (포항공과대학교) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** **One of Asia's leading science universities**, with an open campus and Korea's fourth-generation synchrotron light source nearby
- **Hours:** Campus always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [postech.ac.kr](https://www.postech.ac.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Founded by POSCO in 1986. The lake and modern architecture make a pleasant walk.

### 60) 🔬 Pohang Accelerator Laboratory (포항가속기연구소) — 👀 WATCH — booking required
- **What:** Korea's synchrotron radiation facility and X-ray free-electron laser, with occasional public open days and group tours
- **Hours:** By reservation only
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pal.postech.ac.kr](https://pal.postech.ac.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Not a walk-in attraction** — check for open-day announcements well ahead.

### 61) 🌉 Pohang Canal & Songdo Waterfront (포항운하) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A restored 1.3 km canal linking the Hyeongsan River to the sea, with a walking path and a small cruise boat
- **Hours:** Path always open; cruise **10:00–17:00**
- **Price:** Path **FREE**; cruise ₩12,000
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org)
- **Beginner notes:** The canal was reopened in 2014 after decades as a slum-filled channel. Lit at night.

### 62) 🌳 Hyeongsan River Park & Cycle Path (형산강) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The riverside park running through Pohang to the sea, with cycling paths, sports fields and seasonal flower plantings
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Connects to the Gyeongju cycle route. **Flat, lit and good for a run.**

### 63) 🏛️ Pohang Culture & Arts Hall (포항문화예술회관) — 🔁 by programme
- **What:** Pohang's main concert and theatre venue, with an autumn season of classical music, gugak and touring productions
- **Hours:** Box office **09:00–18:00**; shows **19:30**
- **Price:** **₩10,000–₩50,000**
- **Official source:** [pcaf.or.kr](https://www.pcaf.or.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Check the November 2026 programme when booking.

### 64) 🕯️ Pohang Light Festival & Winter Illumination — 👀 WATCH (Nov–Jan)
- **What:** Pohang typically lights Space Walk, the canal and Yeongildae for winter from late November
- **Hours:** Evenings once lit
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org)
- **Beginner notes:** **Space Walk is lit year-round after dark** regardless of the festival schedule.

### 65) 🚄 Getting to Pohang — KTX & buses — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Pohang has a dedicated KTX station on a branch of the Gyeongbu line, about 2 h 30 from Seoul, plus frequent buses from Gyeongju and Daegu
- **Hours:** Trains roughly **06:00–21:30**
- **Price:** KTX from Seoul **₩53,900**; Gyeongju bus ₩4,500
- **Official source:** [letskorail.com](https://www.letskorail.com) · [kobus.co.kr](https://www.kobus.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Pohang KTX Station is 8 km from downtown** — take a bus or a ₩12,000 taxi.

### 66) 🚌 Pohang City Buses & Coastal Routes — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Pohang's buses reach Homigot (9000), Guryongpo (200) and Naeyeonsan (5000) from the downtown Jukdo Market area
- **Hours:** Roughly **05:30–22:00**
- **Price:** **₩1,500**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **T-money works.** The Homigot bus takes an hour each way — plan accordingly.

### 67) 🧭 Pohang Tourist Information & Free Maps — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Information centres at Pohang KTX Station, the bus terminal and Yeongildae Beach, with free coastal maps and English help
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org) · [1330 Hotline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Ask for the gwamegi season leaflet if you're there in November.

### 68) 🍁 Pohang Autumn Foliage & November Weather — 🔁 seasonal
- **What:** Naeyeonsan and Oeosa peak in **early-to-mid November**; the coast runs 6–17 °C, mild but windy
- **Hours:** Daylight hours
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [weather.go.kr](https://www.weather.go.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Sea wind makes it feel colder than the number** — a windproof layer matters more than thickness.

### 69) 🗺️ Pohang one-day and two-day model routes — 🔁 planning
- **What:** Day 1: Jukdo Market → mulhoe lunch → Hwanho Park + Space Walk + steel art museum → Yeongildae at dusk. Day 2: Homigot cape → Guryongpo Japanese street + gwamegi → Oeosa or Naeyeonsan
- **Hours:** One to two full days
- **Price:** About **₩40,000** total
- **Official source:** [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Pohang pairs naturally with Gyeongju** — 40 minutes apart by bus.

### 70) 🌋 Pohang Yongdeok & Janggi Basalt Columns (주상절리) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Hexagonal basalt columns on the Pohang coast, formed by cooling lava and designated a Natural Monument
- **Hours:** Always open; best at low tide
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** On the coast road south of Guryongpo. **Check the tide** — high water covers the base of the columns.

### 71) 🐚 Pohang Homigot Coastal Trail (호미반도 해안둘레길) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 58 km coastal walking route around the Homi peninsula in four sections, including a boardwalk over the rocks at Seonnyeotang
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [durunubi.kr](https://www.durunubi.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Section 2 with the sea boardwalk is the one to walk** if you only do one — about 3 hours.

### 72) 🪨 Seonnyeotang & Himal Rock Sea Boardwalk (선녀탕) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A steel boardwalk built directly over the tidal rocks on the Homi peninsula trail, passing rock pools and sea caves
- **Hours:** Always open; closed in high seas
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Closed when waves are high** — check before making the trip out.

### 73) 🌊 Janggil-ri & Pohang Southern Fishing Villages — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The small working ports south of Guryongpo, where gwamegi is dried on racks in the open sea wind from November
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE** to view
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **The drying racks appear in November** — the visible start of the season, and a genuinely unusual sight.

### 74) 🏛️ Pohang Cheongha Confucian Sites & Old County Office — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The Joseon county seat at Cheongha, with the old office building, a hyanggyo school and a preserved administrative quarter
- **Hours:** Daylight hours
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** On the way to Naeyeonsan. Also holds the Yeongil Folk Museum.

### 75) 🌲 Pohang Bo-gyeong Valley Forest Bathing & Trails — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The pine and maple forest around Bogyeongsa, with graded forest-bathing paths as well as the waterfall route
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** Temple ticket **₩4,000**
- **Official source:** [Korea Forest Service](https://www.forest.go.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **You don't need to walk all twelve waterfalls** — the first three are within an hour of the temple.

### 76) 🏔️ Unjesan & Pohang Inland Hiking (운제산) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The mountain behind Oeosa, with ridge trails and views over the Pohang plain and the sea
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [Korea Forest Service](https://www.forest.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Three to four hours for the loop from Oeosa. Well signed.

### 77) 🚴 Pohang Coastal Cycle Route & East Sea Path — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Pohang sits on the **East Coast Cycling Route**, part of Korea's certified national bike network running from Goseong to Busan
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**; rental from ₩15,000/day
- **Official source:** [riverguide.go.kr](https://www.riverguide.go.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Certification stamp booths at Homigot and Yeongildae. **The Homigot section is the scenic highlight.**

### 78) 🎡 Pohang Children's & Family Facilities — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Pohang's children's museum, the science centre and the Hwanho Park play areas — indoor options for a cold or wet day
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**–₩5,000
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Space Walk itself has a minimum height requirement — check before promising it to small children.

### 79) 🏛️ Pohang Modern History & POSCO Museum — 🔁 weekdays
- **What:** The story of how a fishing village became a steel city after 1968, told at the POSCO history hall and the city's exhibitions
- **Hours:** Weekdays **09:00–17:00**; booking advised
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [posco.co.kr](https://www.posco.co.kr) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Pohang's population went from 50,000 to 500,000 in thirty years** because of this plant.

### 80) ☕ Pohang Cafe Streets — Yeongildae & Hyoja — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Sea-view cafes along Yeongildae Beach and the independent cafe cluster in Hyoja-dong near POSTECH
- **Hours:** Roughly **10:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Coffee **₩5,000–₩7,000**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **The Yeongildae seafront cafes look straight at the lit steelworks after dark.**

### 81) 🍺 Pohang Nightlife & Yukhoe Alley — 🔁 nightly
- **What:** Pohang's evening districts around Jukdo Market and Yukdaemyeong, with raw-beef restaurants, pubs and late seafood
- **Hours:** Roughly **17:00–02:00**
- **Price:** **₩15,000–₩30,000** per person
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Jukdo Market's second floor stays open late** for fish and drinks — cheaper than the seafront.

### 82) 🛍️ Pohang Shopping — Jukdo, Lotte & downtown — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Jukdo Market for food and goods, plus a Lotte department store and the downtown Yukgeori shopping district
- **Hours:** Market **06:00–21:00**; stores **10:30–20:00**
- **Price:** **FREE** entry
- **Official source:** [lotteshopping.com](https://www.lotteshopping.com) · [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Dried seafood and gwamegi vacuum-packed for travel are the standard Pohang souvenirs.

### 83) 🏨 Pohang Accommodation — beach vs downtown — 🔁 planning
- **What:** Yeongildae Beach for sea views, downtown near Jukdo for food and buses, and Guryongpo for the fishing-village experience
- **Hours:** Check-in typically **15:00**
- **Price:** From **₩45,000** per night
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Yeongildae is the best base** — the beach, the cafes and the bus routes are all there.

### 84) ♨️ Pohang Bathhouses & Seawater Spas — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Jjimjilbang in central Pohang and seawater bathhouses near the beach, useful after a coastal walk in the wind
- **Hours:** Many **24 hours**
- **Price:** **₩10,000–₩15,000**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Seawater baths are a local speciality on the east coast.

### 85) 🚑 Pohang Emergency & Medical Services — 🔁 24 h
- **What:** Pohang St Mary's and Pohang Semyeong Christian hospitals run 24-hour emergency departments
- **Hours:** Emergency **24 h**
- **Price:** Consultation from **₩20,000** for visitors

### 86) 🌏 Pohang for foreign visitors — language & payment — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Pohang sees relatively few foreign tourists; English signage exists at major sites but market stalls are cash and Korean only
- **Hours:** N/A
- **Price:** N/A
- **Official source:** [1330 Hotline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr) · [ipohang.org](https://www.ipohang.org)

### 87) 🌡️ Pohang Wind, Sea State & Cancellation Risk — 🔁 planning
- **What:** The Space Walk, the harbour cruise, the Ulleungdo ferry and the sea boardwalk all close in strong wind — common on this coast in November
- **Hours:** N/A

### 88) 🗺️ Pohang & Gyeongju combined itinerary — 🔁 planning

- **Hours:** Buses roughly every 20 min

### 89) 📸 Pohang Photo Spots — the definitive list — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Price:** **FREE**
