# 🌅 Pohang — Sunrise Steel City & Space Walk (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's coastal steel capital** — home to **POSCO Steel** and the iconic **Hand of Homigot Sunrise Square**. Pohang has reinvented itself into a coastal arts + industry tourism hub featuring the viral **"Space Walk"** observation sculpture and sleepy, photogenic fishing villages. Perfectly placed between Gyeongju and the East Sea coast.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering **Space Walk**, **Homigot Sunrise Square**, **Yeongildae Beach**, and **Jukdo Market**.

## 🌅 Coast — the iconic sunrise

### 1) 🌅 Homigot Sunrise Square & "Hand of Harmony" — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Korea's east-meets-where-the-sun-rises. Famous **Hand of Harmony** sculpture (paired giant hands, one rises from the sea, the other from land) — about 4 km long boardwalk leading out to the rocks
- **Hours:** Open **24/7** daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Best at dawn (or night with reflective stars). The 4 km boardwalk is the easy option.

### 2) 🚶 **Space Walk** (스페이스워크) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** 333-meter-long elevated steel-pedestrian sculpture/ramp near Hwanho Park — Korea's first "art rollout" sculpture. Voted a cultural highlight; STAR WALK-themed (sci-fi)
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (Nov winter hours may close earlier)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [spacewalk.pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Goes up to 25m above the ground. Architectural and Instagram-iconic at sunset.

### 3) 🌊 Yeongildae Beach (영일대 해수욕장) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Iconic city beach with the **Camellia Hill** park and a long 1.6 km boardwalk. Famous for **Saptari poshing** camellias in early winter — the world's only coastal camellia garden
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 8-min walk from Pohang Station.

### 4) 🌉 Hwanho Ocean Bridge Park — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Newly built seaside park joining Yeongildae Beach and the Space Walk
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Best for sunset.

### 5) 🌿 Camellia Forest (동점 వన) — 🔁 daily in November (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's largest **saptari camellia grove** — produces flowers in late Oct/early Nov in red, white and pink
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–17:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Camellia blossoms overlap your first week.

### 6) 🛶 Guryongpo Seafood & Sunset Pier — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Coastal boat harbor and fishing village ~25 km east of city — cluster of seafood restaurants; *Hupo* in Guryongpo
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–midnight
- **Price:** Free walking; meals ~₩15,000–40,000
- **Beginner notes:** 30 min by taxi; take the **Oego** coastal route
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)

### 7) 🌊 Norae Coast Cuttlefish (꼴뚜기) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Famous *Kkolddugi* (cuttlefish) cooking at Norae Coast Village
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–22:00
- **Price:** ₩10,000–30,000
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 200 from downtown; 1 hr each way.
