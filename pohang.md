# 🌅 Pohang — Sunrise Steel City & Space Walk (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's coastal steel capital** — home to **POSCO Steel** and the iconic **Hand of Homigot Sunrise Square**. Pohang has reinvented itself into a coastal arts + industry tourism hub featuring the viral **"Space Walk"** observation sculpture and sleepy, photogenic fishing villages. Perfectly placed between Gyeongju and the East Sea coast.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering **Space Walk**, **Homigot Sunrise Square**, **Yeongildae Beach**, and **Jukdo Market**.

## 🌅 Coast — the iconic sunrise

### 1) 🌅 Homigot Sunrise Square & "Hand of Harmony" — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Korea's east-meets-where-the-sun-rises. Famous **Hand of Harmony** sculpture (paired giant hands, one rises from the sea, the other from land) — about 4 km long boardwalk leading out to the rocks
- **Hours:** Open **24/7** daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Best at dawn (or night with reflective stars). The 4 km boardwalk is the easy option.

### 2) 🚶 **Space Walk** (스페이스워크) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** 333-meter-long elevated steel-pedestrian sculpture/ramp near Hwanho Park — Korea's first "art rollout" sculpture. Voted a cultural highlight; STAR WALK-themed (sci-fi)
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (Nov winter hours may close earlier)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [spacewalk.pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Goes up to 25m above the ground. Architectural and Instagram-iconic at sunset.

### 3) 🌊 Yeongildae Beach (영일대 해수욕장) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Iconic city beach with the **Camellia Hill** park and a long 1.6 km boardwalk. Famous for **Saptari poshing** camellias in early winter — the world's only coastal camellia garden
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 8-min walk from Pohang Station.

### 4) 🌉 Hwanho Ocean Bridge Park — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Newly built seaside park joining Yeongildae Beach and the Space Walk
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Best for sunset.

### 5) 🌿 Camellia Forest (동점 వన) — 🔁 daily in November (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's largest **saptari camellia grove** — produces flowers in late Oct/early Nov in red, white and pink
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–17:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Camellia blossoms overlap your first week.

### 6) 🛶 Guryongpo Seafood & Sunset Pier — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Coastal boat harbor and fishing village ~25 km east of city — cluster of seafood restaurants; *Hupo* in Guryongpo
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–midnight
- **Price:** Free walking; meals ~₩15,000–40,000
- **Beginner notes:** 30 min by taxi; take the **Oego** coastal route
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)

### 7) 🌊 Norae Coast Cuttlefish (꼴뚜기) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Famous *Kkolddugi* (cuttlefish) cooking at Norae Coast Village
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–22:00
- **Price:** ₩10,000–30,000
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 200 from downtown; 1 hr each way.

## 🏛️ Culture & history

### 8) 🏛️ Pohang Museum (포항역사관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Regional museum covering prehistoric to the modern era: Bronze Age tombs, ancient textile traditions, POSCO's industrial era
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:30–17:30** (Nov)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohangmuseum.kr](http://www.pohangmuseum.kr)

### 9) 🏯 Bogyeongsa Temple (보경사) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily (FREE)
- **What:** Remote 13th-century Buddhist temple in the Yeongyang Mountains south of Pohang. Famous for 30+ treasures, Silla stone Buddhas, and autumn foliage
- **Hours:** Daily 06:00–17:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 1h 30m by taxi some tier.

### 10) 🏯 Girimsa Temple — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Quiet mountain temple on Mt. Ungil south of city. Famous for its **flagship Girimsa.style bamboo forest**
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–18:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 15 min by bus from downtown.

### 11) 🗿 Twin-cow Stone Gate (Bansongdae 두면성, 환경) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Remnants of an old traditional stone village gateway in Pohang
- **Hours:** Daily open

### 12) 🏯 Posco Museum (포항제철소 광장) — 👀 WATCH
- **What:** World-class steel museum on the grounds of POSCO's flagship facility
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00; Sat morning only
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [posco.co.kr](https://www.posco.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Reservation required via official site.

### 13) 🏛️ Posco Steel Mill Tour (Posco Steel Signum) — 👀 WATCH
- **What:** Guided tours of POSCO's Pohang Works giant steel plant — outside facing event only
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri only
- **Price:** Free by reservation
- **Official source:** [posco.co.kr](https://www.posco.co.kr)

## 🏯 Modern attractions

### 14) 🎨 Pohang Steel Art Museum (스틸아트 뮤지엄) — ✅ CONFIRMED — Tue–Sun
- **What:** Korea's first steel-pour art gallery and museum — based on POSCO's industrial heritage
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2)
- **Official source:** [steelartmuseum.com](http://www.steelartmuseum.com)

## 🍴 Local food

### 23) 🦐 **Jukdo Market** (죽도시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Korea's biggest seafood market for the East Sea. **Crabs**, abalone, octopus, and mackerel. 200+ stalls selling fresh catch
- **Hours:** Daily **05:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Free walk-in; meals ~₩15,000–50,000
- **Official source:** [jukdomarket.pohang.go.kr](https://www.pohang.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pick fish and have it cooked upstairs for **₩3,000 per dish** — Korea's seafood theater.

### 24) 🍢 Gorae Fish Restaurant (고래의 거리) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Coastal restaurants serving fresh *flounder*, *sea bream*, *hairtail*, and steamer cuisine
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–22:00
- **Price:** ~₩20,000–50,000
- **Beginner notes:** Pair with a sunrise drive.

### 25) 🍕 Pohang Growth Market (Iconic BURGER restaurant) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** First burger in Korea with vintage history — set in a former post office
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–21:00
- **Price:** ₹₩10,000–18,000

### 26) 🥢 Norae Cutlass / Bunal — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Small coastal restaurant serving shellfish and steamer
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00–21:00

### 27) ☕ Pohang Indie Cafe Street (북구 카페 거리) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** 30+ indie cafes and bakeries clustered north of downtown
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–22:00
- **Price:** ~₩5,000–7,000
