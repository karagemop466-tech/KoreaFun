## Coast and city landmarks

### 1) 🚶 **Space Walk** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Walkable steel sculpture in Hwanho Park; the looped section is not accessible.
- **Hours/price:** Free. November weekday closing is early; wind, rain, ice, heat, or maintenance can close it without notice.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 2) 🌲 **Hwanho Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Coastal city park containing Space Walk, paths, lawns, and Pohang Museum of Steel Art.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor park access is free; facilities have separate hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 3) 🌊 **Yeongildae Beach and pavilion** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Urban beach, offshore-style pavilion, promenade, and bay views.
- **Hours/price:** Public promenade and beach access are free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 4) 🌅 **Homigot Sunrise Square and Hands of Harmony** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Cape plaza known for paired hand sculptures and sunrise views.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor plaza is free. The annual sunrise festival is at New Year, outside this trip.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism — Homigot](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/wmap/festival/view.do?menu_idx=29&org_code=0002&date_type=5&cm_idx=950)

### 5) 💡 **National Lighthouse Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL MUSEUM
- **What:** Museum at Homigot covering lighthouse, navigation, and maritime history.
- **Hours/price:** Check current visitor hours and closure days; general admission is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [National Lighthouse Museum](https://www.lighthouse-museum.or.kr/)

### 6) 🥾 **Homi Peninsula Coastal Trail** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Multi-section coastal route around the peninsula, including fishing villages and sea cliffs.
- **Hours/price:** Free; choose a realistic section and check waves, wind, and deck closures.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://pohang.go.kr/phtour/9300/subview.do)

### 7) 🚂 **Pohang Railroad Forest** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Linear park created along a former railway corridor through the city.
- **Hours/price:** Free public path.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 8) 🚤 **Pohang Canal and Songdo waterfront** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Canal-side walking route and waterfront regeneration area.
- **Hours/price:** Public paths are free; cruise operation is separately scheduled.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 9) 🪨 **Igari Anchor Observatory** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Anchor-shaped coastal observation deck north of central Pohang.
- **Hours/price:** Free; high waves and wind can close the deck.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 10) 🏖️ **Wolpo, Chilpo, and Hwajin beaches** — 🔎 VERIFIED COASTAL PLACES
- **What:** Northern Pohang beaches suitable for November walks, not swimming.
- **Hours/price:** Public shore access is free; commercial facilities are seasonal.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

## Guryongpo and southern coast

### 11) 🏘️ **Guryongpo Modern Culture and History Street** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Restored streetscape preserving houses associated with the Japanese colonial-period fishing settlement.
- **Hours/price:** Public street is free; museum buildings have posted hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 12) 🐟 **Guryongpo Gwamegi Culture Hall** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Exhibition about local fishing culture and the production of gwamegi.
- **Hours/price:** City information lists free admission; verify Monday closure and seasonal hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 13) 🌾 **Yeonorang Seonyeo Theme Park and Guibigo** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Coastal park and exhibition center interpreting the Yeonorang and Seonyeo legend.
- **Hours:** Guibigo is listed Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, last entry 17:30, closed Monday.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Cultural Foundation — Guibigo](https://culturalspace.phcf.or.kr/guibigoShilla/guibigo.do)

### 14) 🏯 **Oeosa Temple and Unjesan** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Historic Buddhist temple beside Oeoji reservoir, with access to Unjesan trails.
- **Hours/price:** General temple access is free; verify parking and trail notices.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Oeosa](http://www.oeosa.org/)

## Mountains and inland heritage

### 15) ⛰️ **Naeyeonsan and Twelve Waterfalls trail** — 🔎 VERIFIED HIKE
- **What:** Mountain-valley trail through a sequence of waterfalls in northern Pohang.
- **Hours/price:** Trail access is generally free; daylight hiking and weather checks are essential.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 16) 🛕 **Bogyeongsa Temple** — 🔎 VERIFIED TEMPLE
- **What:** Temple at the Naeyeonsan trail entrance.
- **Hours/price:** General admission has been free since the 2023 heritage-temple change; parking may charge.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Bogyeongsa](http://www.bogyeongsa.org/)

### 17) 🏘️ **Deokdong Cultural Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Rural heritage village with historic houses and local cultural landscape.
- **Hours/price:** Public village routes are free; respect private residences.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

## Museums, markets, and culture

### 18) 🎨 **Pohang Museum of Steel Art** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Municipal art museum in Hwanho Park with modern and contemporary exhibitions and a focus on steel-related art.
- **Hours/price:** Free general admission; check Monday closure and current exhibitions.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Museum of Steel Art](https://poma.pohang.go.kr/)

### 19) 🏭 **POSCO Museum** — ⏳ RESERVATION REQUIRED
- **What:** Company museum about POSCO and Korean steelmaking history.
- **Status:** Verify public access and reserve through POSCO; the operating steelworks is not a walk-in attraction.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [POSCO](https://www.posco.co.kr/)

### 20) 🛒 **Jukdo Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET
- **What:** Large traditional and seafood market in central Pohang.
- **Hours/price:** Vendor-specific; compare posted weights and preparation fees.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 21) 🐟 **Pohang mulhoe and official food directory** — 🔎 VERIFIED LOCAL FOOD
- **What:** Chilled raw-fish dish associated with Pohang. Choose a named current restaurant from the city directory.
- **Hours/price:** Restaurant-specific.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Tourism](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 22) 🦑 **Guryongpo gwamegi** — 🔎 VERIFIED SEASONAL FOOD
- **What:** Semi-dried fish specialty associated with the Guryongpo area; colder months are its normal season.
- **Hours/price:** Shop and restaurant-specific.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Gwamegi Culture Hall](https://www.pohang.go.kr/phtour/)

### 23) 🎬 **Indieplus Pohang** — ⏳ CHECK MONTHLY SCHEDULE
- **What:** Independent-film cinema at Pohang Central Art Hall.
- **Status:** Monthly films and times are published by the Cultural Foundation.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Indieplus Pohang schedule](https://culturalspace.phcf.or.kr/joongangArtHall/indiplusMovieSchedule.do)

### 24) 🎭 **Pohang Culture and Arts Hall** — ⏳ CHECK CALENDAR
- **What:** Municipal performance and exhibition venue.
- **Status:** Attend only an individually dated November program.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Pohang Cultural Foundation calendar](https://culturalspace.phcf.or.kr/cop/bbs/selectArticleList.do?bbsId=BBSMSTR_000000000281)
