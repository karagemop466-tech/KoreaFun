- **What:** Seoul Arts Center stage productions captured in high definition and screened on a big screen. **November's title is confirmed on the foundation's published 2026 lineup as the play 《늙은 부부이야기》 (An Old Couple's Story)** — a well-loved two-hander about love late in life. Korean-language, no subtitles.

## Jinhae

### 3) 🌉 **Yeojwacheon stream and Romance Bridge** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Urban stream corridor famous for spring cherry blossoms.
- **November note:** The walk remains open, but no blossoms are promised.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Changwon Tourism](https://culture.changwon.go.kr/)

### 4) 🚉 **Gyeonghwa Station park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Former railway corridor and park best known for spring cherry trees.
- **Hours/price:** Public park is free; November is an ordinary park visit.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Changwon Tourism](https://culture.changwon.go.kr/)

### 5) 🗼 **Jehwangsan Park and Jinhae Tower** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Hill park and tower overlooking central Jinhae and the harbor.
- **Hours/price:** Park is free; tower and monorail/facility hours should be checked.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Changwon Tourism](https://culture.changwon.go.kr/)

### 6) 🏤 **Jinhae Post Office and modern-history streets** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE WALK
- **What:** Historic post-office building and early-20th-century street fabric around Jungwon Rotary.
- **Hours/price:** Exterior walk is free; interior access varies.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Jinhae Modern Culture History Road](https://www.changwon.go.kr/cwportal/depart/11062/12458/12903.web)

### 7) 🚶 **Jinhae Modern Culture History Road guided route** — 🔎 VERIFIED PROGRAM
- **What:** Official walking route linking naval, independence, commercial, architectural, and park sites.
- **Status:** Reserve only through the current city program; guide availability varies.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Changwon City route and application](https://www.changwon.go.kr/cwportal/depart/11062/12458/12903.web)

### 8) ⚓ **Republic of Korea Naval Academy Museum** — ⏳ ACCESS-RESTRICTED
- **What:** Museum within the Naval Academy/base area.
- **Access:** The academy is not a daily walk-in tourist attraction. Public access is normally tied to announced events such as Gunhangje or approved visits.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Republic of Korea Naval Academy](https://www.navy.ac.kr/)

### 9) 🌊 **Jinhae Marine Park and Marine Solar Tower** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Island/coastal park complex with tower, exhibitions, and sea views.
- **Hours/price:** **09:00–18:00, ticket office closes 17:00. Closed Mondays** — but the **outdoor grounds and the coastal walking path stay open every day**, so a Monday visit still works for the walk and the sea views.
- **Prices:** **Solar Tower ₩3,500 adult / ₩2,500 student and military / ₩1,500 child.** The **Marine Life Theme Park and Fish Ecology Hall are ₩2,500** separately. Changwon residents get a discount on ID — not applicable to you, but it explains differing figures online.
- **✅ The Solar Tower observatory is open again.** It shut on Nov 10, 2023 when its two high-speed lifts were condemned, and stayed shut for a year; Changwon spent ₩1.08 bn replacing them and **reopened it on Nov 16, 2024**. Several travel pages still describe it as closed — that information is out of date.
- **What you get:** a circular observatory on floors **27–28**, ~120 m up, with a glass floor section, looking over Jinhae Bay, the Geoga Bridge and Udo island. The exhibition building below (green-energy displays) is **free**. There is also a **zip line** on site.
- **⚠️ Very exposed to wind** on the tower approach and the shoreline path — this is a November sea-front.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 18):** [Changwon Facilities Corporation](https://www.cwsisul.or.kr/) · [VisitKorea accessible-tourism listing (hours, fares)](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/opentour/detail.do?cotId=bd3fefbe-6d2b-4288-a425-a45890e15feb) · [Changwon City — Solar Tower reopening, Nov 16 2024](https://www.nocutnews.co.kr/news/6239597)

- **Hours/price:** Free; check memorial-hall hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [March 15 National Cemetery](https://www.mpva.go.kr/315/)

### 16) 🎨 **Moonshin Art Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Museum dedicated to sculptor Moon Shin, overlooking Masan.
- **Hours/price:** **09:00–18:00, last entry 17:30.** **₩500 adult / ₩200 child and youth** — one of the cheapest museums in the country. **Free for under-6s and over-65s.** Card accepted.
- **Closed Mondays** (or the next day if that Monday is a holiday), Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok. Allow **1–1.5 hrs**. Free parking.
- **What's there:** Moon Shin's own museum, which he spent 15 years building on the Chusan-dong hillside above Masan harbour — Exhibition Halls 1 and 2, an **outdoor sculpture park**, the Moon Shin Original Form Museum and an art shop. The hilltop setting and harbour view are half the appeal.
- **♿ Step-free access:** lift inside the Original Form Museum and ramps at each hall entrance.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 18):** [Changwon City Museums](https://www.changwon.go.kr/cwportal/depart/11064/11123.web) · [Changwon Tourism — Moonshin Art Museum details](https://changwon.go.kr/tour/index.do?menuCode=001_007000000000)

### 30) 🌼 **Masan Gagopa Chrysanthemum Festival (마산가고파국화축제)** — ⏳ 2026 DATES TBA — recent years land exactly in your window · NEW
- **What:** **Korea's biggest autumn flower festival**, held on the Masan waterfront since 2000 — Changwon (old Masan) is where commercial chrysanthemum growing in Korea began. Around **125,000 chrysanthemums** built into towers, arches and the record-setting 다륜대작 "thousand-bloom" plants, across **3.15 Marine Nuri Park (main site)** and **Happo Waterfront Park**, with night lighting, a drone show, fireworks and food streets backing onto the Masan fish market.
- **⏳ Public 2026 visitor dates are still unpublished.** What the city *has* posted is an occupancy window: the official booking desk closed Happo Waterfront Park's outdoor stage for **2026. 10. 25 – 11. 12** *because of the 2026 Masan Gagopa Chrysanthemum Festival*. That blackout is longer than a typical 9-day public run (setup/teardown), so **do not treat Oct 25–Nov 12 as the visitor calendar**. Recent public runs: **25th edition Nov 1–9, 2025** (09:00–22:00, free); **24th edition Oct 26–Nov 3, 2024**. Expect the visitor window to sit inside the first two trip weeks; pull hours and program from the city festival page in October.
- **Getting there:** 3.15 Marine Nuri Park is on the Masan Happo waterfront — pairs with Masan fish market and the Robot Land coast (#28).
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 18):** [Changwon City booking notice — 국화축제 기간 2026.10.25~11.12, Happo outdoor stage closed to other bookings](https://www.changwon.go.kr/booking/10034/10152.web?amode=view&idx=486) · [2026 flower-production tender naming the 26th festival](https://www.changwon.go.kr/cwportal/10310/10429/11015.web?amode=view&idx=876606&gcode=1009) · 2025 dates/programme via [Gyeongnam Domin Ilbo, Oct 30, 2025](https://www.idomin.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=949146) · [Yonhap — 2024 edition](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20240819063500052)
