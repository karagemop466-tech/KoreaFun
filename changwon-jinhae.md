# 🌸 Changwon & Jinhae — The Naval Base, Mountains & Coastal Gardens (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's largest city by area** — **Changwon** is a sprawling inland industrial-meets-natural city, while **Jinhae** (one-third of Changwon's urban area) is a historic **Korean Naval Academy** port town famous for its spring *cherry blossom* festival — and its autumn galettes, camellia hills, and naval relics. Tucked south of Busan between the KTX line and the south coast.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering **Jinhae Cherry Blossom Avenue** (autumn maple section), **Republic of Korea Naval Academy Museum**, **Gyeonghwa Station**, and **Mt. Bulamsan**.

**Official Changwon sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Changwon (official tourism): [changwon.go.kr](http://www.changwon.go.kr)
- Jinhae Cherry Blossom Festival: [jinhae.go.kr](http://www.jinhae.go.kr)
- Changwon NC Park (baseball): [ncpark.com](https://www.ncpark.com)

**Getting there:** Seoul (KTX) → Changwon via Gupo JCT (≈ 2 hr 50 min) · Busan (Suburban Line) → Changwon (≈ 35–45 min) · Busan (KTX) → Changwon Jungang (≈ 50 min)

## 🌸 Jinhae (the photogenic district)

### 1) 🌸 Jinhae Cherry Blossom Avenue (Camellia Hill) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's **viral 800-meter cherry blossom-lined street** — in late Nov the trees do not bloom, but **autumn foliage** + **camellia shrubs** are gorgeous. The Yeojwacheon stream that runs under cherry fronds becomes a calm autumn stream
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [jinhae.go.kr](http://www.jinhae.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Light night illumination begins ~17:00 in Cherry Blossom Festival period, but November structure equals **leaf-strewn peaceful walk** — actually less crowded.

### 2) 🚉 **Gyeonghwa Station** (경화역) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** KORAIL railway station most famous for K-drama / K-movie Vibes. The platform has a famous "Hong-Soo-jung train" (scent of Jeju). Walls and pillars covered with BTS-related murals and Hanlim (The Guy Cave K-pop)
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–22:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [korail.com](https://www.korail.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Top photo stop in Jinhae (year-round).

### 3) 🌳 **Jehwangsan Park** — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Mountain park stretching over 1.5 km with camellias, Mt. Jehwangsan peak viewpoint, and K-drama iconic pavilions
- **Hours:** Open 24/7
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [changwon.go.kr](http://www.changwon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** A 30-min hill climb rewarding panoramic Jinhae Bay.

### 4) ⚓ **Republic of Korea Naval Academy Museum** — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The Republic of Korea Naval Academy at Jinhae — open to the public during specific hours — see Korea's maritime legends
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00; Sat 10:00–16:00 (closed Sunday)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [navy.mil.kr](http://www.navy.mil.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Show your passport on arrival.

### 5) 🚢 Jinhae Bay Naval Cruise (December-cruise) — 👀 WATCH (Nov schedule)
- **What:** Seasonal visitor cruises (seasonally limited)
- **Hours:** Variable
- **Price:** ~₩15,000–25,000
- **Official source:** [jinhae.go.kr](http://www.jinhae.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Verify schedule in October.

### 6) 🌹 **Jinhae Jungwan Rotary** — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Tree canopied roundabout circling a famous "Night-view" of cherry blossoms in spring. In autumn, ginkgo mandshurica dominance. The cherry-blossom avenue continues from here to **Namgang Calamus**
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–midnight
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [changwon.go.kr](http://www.changwon.go.kr)

### 7) 🌊 **Yeojwacheon Stream Cherry Tree Lane** — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** The length of Yeojwacheon stream on either side lined with cherry trees. 1.6-km walk that is gorgeous in spring but offers glimpses in Nov
- **Hours:** Open 24/7
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [jinhae.go.kr](http://www.jinhae.go.kr)

### 8) 🌷 Jinhae Citrus-Scented Folk Village — 👀 WATCH
- **What:** Citrus bakery and jam alleys serving **mandarin** traditional desserts
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–22:00
- **Price:** Paid per item
