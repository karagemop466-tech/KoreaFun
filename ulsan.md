# 🌊 Ulsan — Coast, Whales & Industry (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's industrial powerhouse** — home to **Hyundai Heavy Industries, Hyundai Motors, and Korea's biggest whale-watching industry**. Surrounded by dramatic coastline, the highest-quality Korean *soju* (Seolmaeum-one), and surprisingly green parks of the **Taehwagang Grand Park bamboo grove**. Perfectly placed between Busan and Daegu on the KTX line.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Daewangam Park, Taehwagang bamboo grove, O-Gukjae "Whale Street", and Songjeong Beach.

## 🌊 Coast (the headline)

### 1) 🌅 Ganjeolgot Cape (간절곶) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's easternmost cape; first place on the mainland to see the sunrise. Coastal lighthouse with sea-cliff boardwalk
- **Hours:** Open 24/7
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** On a clear day, dawn here beats even Seongsan in Jeju.

### 2) 🌿 Taehwagang Grand Park & Bamboo Forest (태화강 띠 초 공원) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Famous local park with a striking 3-km bamboo forest, an Ewha flower zone, and seasonal foliage paths. Ulsan's "soul"
- **Hours:** Daily 06:00–22:00 (bamboo forest 06:00–20:00)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 5-min taxi from Ulsan Airport. 3 km loop — peacocks wander.

### 3) 🌉 Taehwagang River Cruise — 👀 WATCH (seasonal)
- **What:** Seasonal daytime and evening cruise along the Taehwa River
- **Hours:** Variable
- **Price:** Adult **₩10,000–15,000**
- **Official source:** [tawangriverspa.com](http://www.tawangriverspa.com)

### 4) 🐠 Ulsan Daewangam Maritime Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 5 min walk from Daewangam Park.

### 5) 🦭 Ganjeolgot Observatory & Lighthouse — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's eastern-lighthouse — view over the Baekdaegan Coast National Scenic site
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)

### 6) 🤽 O-Gukjae Whale Festival — 👀 WATCH (Oct wave typically)
- **What:** Whale-watch cruise to O-Gukjae Bay near Whale Village — 2 hr ride from downtown. **Go to see migrating whales**

### 43) 🐋 Bangudae Petroglyphs — UNESCO World Heritage (반구대 암각화) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** **Inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in July 2025**: Neolithic rock carvings including **the world's oldest known depictions of whaling**, on a cliff above the Daegokcheon
- **Hours:** Museum Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00**; site always viewable
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1740) · [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Bring binoculars** — the carvings are across the water and hard to see unaided. Water levels affect visibility; autumn is usually good.

### 44) 🖼️ Ulsan Petroglyph Museum (울산암각화박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The interpretation centre for the Bangudae and Cheonjeon-ri petroglyphs, with full-size replicas you can actually see up close
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1740)
- **Beginner notes:** **Visit this before walking to the cliff** — otherwise you won't know what you're looking at.

### 45) 🪨 Cheonjeon-ri Petroglyphs — UNESCO (천전리 각석) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The second **UNESCO-listed** petroglyph panel, with Bronze Age geometric designs and **Silla-era inscriptions dated to 525 and 539 CE**
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1740) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Two kilometres upstream from Bangudae. **Dinosaur footprints are exposed on the riverbed nearby.**

### 46) 🦕 Daegokcheon Dinosaur Footprints & Valley Walk — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Hours:** Always open; best at low water

- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)

### 61) 🛕 Tongdosa Temple — UNESCO Sansa (통도사) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** One of Korea's **Three Jewel Temples** and a **UNESCO World Heritage Sansa**, founded 646 CE, holding relics of the Buddha in place of a statue
- **Hours:** Daily **08:30–17:30** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** **FREE** — cultural-heritage admission was **abolished on May 4, 2023** for the 65 Jogye Order temples holding state-designated heritage (the government reimburses the temple). Parking is still charged. Older guidebooks still quote Adult ₩3,000 — there is no entry fee.
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1562) · [tongdosa.or.kr](http://www.tongdosa.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Technically in Yangsan, 30 minutes from Ulsan. **The 1 km pine approach avenue is superb in November.**

- **Official source:** [kobus.co.kr](https://www.kobus.co.kr) · [letskorail.com](https://www.letskorail.com)
- **Beginner notes:** **Gyeongju in the morning, Ulsan petroglyphs in the afternoon** is a workable single day if you start early.

### 110) 📅 Ulsan November Weather & What's Realistic — 🔁 seasonal
- **What:** Ulsan in November runs **5–17 °C**, milder than inland Korea, dry and often clear — good for coast walks and mountain hiking
- **Hours:** N/A
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [weather.go.kr](https://www.weather.go.kr) · [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Coastal wind is the main factor** — the cable car and boat trips are the things that get cancelled.

### 111) 🎯 Ulsan one-day model route — 🔁 planning
- **What:** Taehwa bamboo grove morning → Ulsan Museum → Jangsaengpo whale village → Ulsan Bridge observatory at dusk → Samsan-dong dinner
- **Hours:** Full day
- **Price:** About **₩25,000** including admissions
- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Add a second day for the UNESCO petroglyphs and the Yeongnam Alps** — they're in the opposite direction from the coast.

### 112) 🌏 Ulsan Foreign Resident Services & Global Center — 🔁 weekdays (FREE)
- **What:** Ulsan's large industrial workforce includes many foreign residents; the Global Center offers free interpretation and advice
- **Hours:** Weekdays **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Useful for anything administrative. For tourism questions, 1330 is faster.

### 113) 🚑 Ulsan Emergency & Medical Services — 🔁 24 h
- **What:** Ulsan University Hospital and several large general hospitals run 24-hour emergency departments with some English capability
- **Hours:** Emergency **24 h**
- **Price:** Consultation from **₩20,000** for visitors
- **Official source:** [uuh.ulsan.kr](https://www.uuh.ulsan.kr) · [1339 Medical Hotline](https://www.kdca.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **119 for ambulance, 1339 for medical advice in English.**
