# 🌊 Ulsan — Coast, Whales & Industry (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's industrial powerhouse** — home to **Hyundai Heavy Industries, Hyundai Motors, and Korea's biggest whale-watching industry**. Surrounded by dramatic coastline, the highest-quality Korean *soju* (Seolmaeum-one), and surprisingly green parks of the **Taehwagang Grand Park bamboo grove**. Perfectly placed between Busan and Daegu on the KTX line.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Daewangam Park, Taehwagang bamboo grove, O-Gukjae "Whale Street", and Songjeong Beach.

**Official Ulsan sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Ulsan (official tourism): [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- Ulsan Hyundai (soccer): [www.hyundai-unipo.com](https://www.hyundai-unipo.com)
- Ulsan Hyundai Mobis Phoebus (pro basketball): [mobis.co.kr](https://www.mobis.co.kr)

**Getting there:** KTX Seoul → Ulsan ≈ 2 hr 30 min · KTX Busan → Ulsan ≈ 30 min · KTX Daegu → Ulsan ≈ 1 hr

## 🌊 Coast (the headline)

### 1) 🌊 Daewangam Park (대왕암공원) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** 127 m coastal park on the east side of Ulsan — Korea's #1 sunrise spot. Stone pines on sea cliffs, 1.7 km pedestrian trail across peninsulas, abundant autumn maple and ginkgo foliage
- **Hours:** Daily **08:00–18:00** (Nov winter)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Take No. 1 or No. 2 bus from Ulsan Station (15 min). Sunset over the East Sea is haunting.

### 2) 🌅 Ganjeolgot Cape (간절곶) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's easternmost cape; first place on the mainland to see the sunrise. Coastal lighthouse with sea-cliff boardwalk
- **Hours:** Open 24/7
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** On a clear day, dawn here beats even Seongsan in Jeju.

### 3) 🌿 Taehwagang Grand Park & Bamboo Forest (태화강 띠 초 공원) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Famous local park with a striking 3-km bamboo forest, an Ewha flower zone, and seasonal foliage paths. Ulsan's "soul"
- **Hours:** Daily 06:00–22:00 (bamboo forest 06:00–20:00)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 5-min taxi from Ulsan Airport. 3 km loop — peacocks wander.

### 4) 🌉 Taehwagang River Cruise — 👀 WATCH (seasonal)
- **What:** Seasonal daytime and evening cruise along the Taehwa River
- **Hours:** Variable
- **Price:** Adult **₩10,000–15,000**
- **Official source:** [tawangriverspa.com](http://www.tawangriverspa.com)

### 5) 🐠 Ulsan Daewangam Maritime Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Small museum on ocean life and the Ulsan shipyards
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 09:00–17:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 5 min walk from Daewangam Park.

### 6) 🦭 Ganjeolgot Observatory & Lighthouse — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's eastern-lighthouse — view over the Baekdaegan Coast National Scenic site
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)

### 7) 🤽 O-Gukjae Whale Festival — 👀 WATCH (Oct wave typically)
- **What:** Whale-watch cruise to O-Gukjae Bay near Whale Village — 2 hr ride from downtown. **Go to see migrating whales**
- **Hours:** Daytime tours ~10:00–14:00; Saturday trips more frequent
- **Price:** Cruise ~**₩25,000–40,000**
- **Official source:** [uft.ulsan.go.kr](https://uft.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Binoculars helpful.

## 🏯 Culture & heritage

### 17) 🏛️ Ulsan Museum (울산박물관) — ✅ CONFIRMED — Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Prehistoric petroglyphs (Bangudae stones replica), Baekje-era gold crowns, regional Silla/Joseon artifacts
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [museum.ulsan.go.kr](http://museum.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 10 min walk from Taehwagang station.

### 18) 🏯 Petroglyphs of Bangudae (반구대 암각화) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** UNESCO-listed prehistoric hunting/whaling engravings dating 3,000+ years. Replica access (the original is by tour)
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00
- **Price:** Adult **₩2,000**
- **Official source:** [bangudae.ulsan.go.kr](https://bangudae.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Shows the earliest **whale-harpooning** art in world history.

### 19) 🏯 Seonbawi Rock (선바위가 있는 호계) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Goddess-of-Ewha cave at a mountain shrine outside Ulsan
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk

### 20) 🏯 Ulsan Petroglyphs Museum (거제) — 🔁 Tue–Sun

## 🆕 More verified Ulsan events & activities (expansion set)

**Verified expansion set** — the newly UNESCO-inscribed Bangudae petroglyphs, whaling heritage, Yeongnam Alps silver grass, the Taehwa National Garden, industrial Ulsan, coast and sport.

### 140) 🐋 Bangudae Petroglyphs — UNESCO World Heritage (반구대 암각화) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** **Inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in July 2025**: Neolithic rock carvings including **the world's oldest known depictions of whaling**, on a cliff above the Daegokcheon
- **Hours:** Museum Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00**; site always viewable
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1740) · [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Bring binoculars** — the carvings are across the water and hard to see unaided. Water levels affect visibility; autumn is usually good.

### 141) 🖼️ Ulsan Petroglyph Museum (울산암각화박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The interpretation centre for the Bangudae and Cheonjeon-ri petroglyphs, with full-size replicas you can actually see up close
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1740)
- **Beginner notes:** **Visit this before walking to the cliff** — otherwise you won't know what you're looking at.

### 142) 🪨 Cheonjeon-ri Petroglyphs — UNESCO (천전리 각석) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The second **UNESCO-listed** petroglyph panel, with Bronze Age geometric designs and **Silla-era inscriptions dated to 525 and 539 CE**
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1740) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Two kilometres upstream from Bangudae. **Dinosaur footprints are exposed on the riverbed nearby.**

### 143) 🦕 Daegokcheon Dinosaur Footprints & Valley Walk — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Cretaceous dinosaur trackways in the streambed beside the Cheonjeon-ri panel, in the same UNESCO-inscribed valley
- **Hours:** Always open; best at low water
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Autumn low water exposes the tracks best** — good timing for a November visit.

### 144) 🐳 Jangsaengpo Whale Culture Village (장생포고래문화마을) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** A recreated 1960s–70s whaling village on the site of Korea's whaling port, with a whale museum, a life-size blue whale model and a monorail
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00; closed Mon)
- **Price:** Village **₩2,000**; combined with museum ₩5,000
- **Official source:** [whalecity.kr](https://www.whalecity.kr) · [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Ulsan was Korea's whaling capital until the 1986 moratorium** — this is where that history is told.

### 145) 🏛️ Jangsaengpo Whale Museum (고래박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** **Korea's only whale museum**, with real skeletons, whaling equipment preserved after the 1986 ban, and a small whale ecology aquarium
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** Adult **₩2,000**
- **Official source:** [whalemuseum.go.kr](https://www.whalemuseum.go.kr) · [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 256 from Ulsan Station area. The blue whale skeleton is the centrepiece.

### 146) ⛴️ Whale Watching Cruise, Jangsaengpo (고래바다여행선) — 👀 WATCH — seasonal, weather-dependent
- **What:** A research-linked cruise into the East Sea looking for minke and dolphin pods; sightings of large dolphin schools are common
- **Hours:** Sailings typically **spring–autumn**, weather permitting
- **Price:** Adult around **₩30,000**
- **Official source:** [whalecity.kr](https://www.whalecity.kr) · [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Confirm the November 2026 sailing schedule** — winter operations are reduced and cancellations are frequent.

### 147) 🎨 Jangsaengpo Modern History Village & 5D Cinema — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Twenty-eight recreated buildings — a barber, a photo studio, a school, a whale-meat restaurant — reconstructing Jangsaengpo's whaling boom
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** Included with village ticket **₩2,000**

### 179) 🚄 Getting to Ulsan — KTX & Ulsan Airport — 🔁 daily

### 180) 🚌 Ulsan City Buses & Regional Connections — 🔁 daily

### 182) 🏞️ Bangucheon Stream Valley Walk & Banguseowon (반구서원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr) · [Ulju-gun](https://www.ulju.ulsan.kr)

### 183) 🚌 Getting to the Bangudae petroglyphs — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The UNESCO site is in rural Ulju County; bus 807 or 1713 from Ulsan runs to Eonyang, then a local bus or taxi covers the last stretch
- **Hours:** Buses roughly **06:00–21:00**
- **Price:** Bus **₩1,500**; taxi from Eonyang ₩12,000
- **Official source:** [its.ulsan.kr](https://its.ulsan.kr) · [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Allow a full half-day.** Public transport is sparse — a taxi from Eonyang is the practical choice.

### 184) 🏛️ Ulsan Daegok Museum (울산대곡박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** A museum of the Daegokcheon valley's history from prehistory through Silla to the Joseon period, beside the petroglyph site
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Combine with the Petroglyph Museum — both are free and 10 minutes apart.

### 185) ⛰️ Sinbulsan Falls & Ulju Mountain Trails (신불산폭포) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Price:** **FREE**

### 186) 🏕️ Yeongnam Alps Welcome Center & Sky Trails — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Ulju County's mountain gateway complex, with an observatory, a planetarium, hiking information and a camping area
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Facilities **FREE**–₩5,000
- **Official source:** [Ulju-gun](https://www.ulju.ulsan.kr) · [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The obvious starting point for any Yeongnam Alps hike. Free maps and English trail advice.

### 187) 🎬 Ulju Mountain Film Festival & Mountain Culture — 👀 WATCH — usually September

### 188) 🌲 Ulsan Daewangam Pine Forest & Songrim-gil — 🔁 daily (FREE)

### 189) 🚶 Ulsan Haemaji-gil Coastal Trail (해맞이길) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

### 190) 🐠 Ulsan Bangeojin Port & Fish Market (방어진항) — 🔁 daily

- **Price:** **FREE** to browse
- **Official source:** [Dong-gu Ulsan](https://www.donggu.ulsan.kr)

### 191) 🌊 Slow City Ulju & Seosaengpo Fortress (서생포왜성) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Official source:** [Ulju-gun](https://www.ulju.ulsan.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)

### 192) 🚂 Ulsan Coastal Rail & Namchang Old Station — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Disused sections of the old Donghae coastal railway in southern Ulsan, converted into walking and cycling paths
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [ulsan.go.kr](https://www.ulsan.go.kr) · [Ulju-gun](https://www.ulju.ulsan.kr)

### 193) 🍶 Eonyang Bulgogi Street (언양불고기거리) — 🔁 daily
