## World Heritage and museums

### 1) 🪨 **Petroglyphs along the Bangucheon Stream** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SITE · NEW STATUS
- **What:** World Heritage property inscribed in 2025, comprising the Bangudae petroglyphs and Cheonjeon-ri inscriptions/petroglyphs.
- **Access:** Viewing conditions and trails are managed for conservation; check visitor-center guidance before travel.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Korea Heritage Service announcement](https://www.korea.net/Government/Briefing-Room/Press-Releases/view?articleId=91260&type=N&insttCode=A260203) · [UNESCO ref. 1740](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1740/)

### 2) 🖼️ **Ulsan Petroglyph Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Museum explaining the Bangucheon rock art, archaeology, ecology, and preservation.
- **Hours/price:** Check current hours, Monday closure, and guided programs.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Petroglyph Museum](https://www.ulsan.go.kr/s/bangudae/)

### 3) 🏛️ **Ulsan Daegok Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Regional archaeology and history museum near the Bangucheon heritage landscape.
- **Hours/price:** Verify current visitor information.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Daegok Museum](https://www.ulsan.go.kr/s/dgmuseum/)

### 4) 🏛️ **Ulsan Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Municipal museum covering Ulsan’s history, industry, and culture.
- **Hours/price:** General admission is normally free; check closures and special exhibitions.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Museum](https://www.ulsan.go.kr/s/museum/)

### 5) 🎨 **Ulsan Art Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Municipal contemporary and media-art museum in central Ulsan.
- **Hours/price:** Exhibition-specific; check the official current-exhibition page and Monday closure.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Art Museum](https://www.ulsan.go.kr/s/uam/)

## River, parks, and city

### 6) 🎋 **Taehwagang National Garden and Simnidaebat bamboo grove** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** National garden and long riverside bamboo forest in central Ulsan.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor garden access is free; individual facilities and programs have posted hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Taehwagang National Garden](https://www.ulsan.go.kr/s/garden/)

### 7) 🐦 **Taehwagang migratory-bird roost** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATURE ACTIVITY
- **What:** River habitat used seasonally by rooks and other wintering birds.
- **Access:** Observe from public paths and designated viewpoints; do not enter roost habitat.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Taehwagang National Garden](https://www.ulsan.go.kr/s/garden/)

### 8) 🌳 **Ulsan Grand Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Large municipal park with trails, gardens, and paid family facilities.
- **Hours/price:** Main outdoor park is free; zoo, pool, and exhibits have separate admission and closure days.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Grand Park](https://www.ulsanpark.com/)

### 9) 🌉 **Ulsan Bridge Observatory** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Observatory over the harbor, bridge, and industrial waterfront.
- **Hours/price:** Check city facility hours and visibility; admission is normally free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Tourism](https://tour.ulsan.go.kr/)

### 10) 🛒 **Ulsan traditional markets and food directory** — 🔎 VERIFIED ACTIVITY

## Coast and whale heritage

### 11) 🌊 **Daewangam Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Pine forest and rocky coastal park in Dong-gu.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor park is free; individual facilities set hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daewangam Park](https://daewangam.donggu.ulsan.kr/)

### 12) 🌉 **Daewangam suspension bridge** — 🔎 VERIFIED FACILITY
- **What:** Pedestrian suspension bridge within Daewangam Park.
- **Hours/price:** Weather and maintenance can close it; check same-day notices.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daewangam Park](https://daewangam.donggu.ulsan.kr/)

### 13) 💡 **Ulgi Lighthouse** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Lighthouse complex within the Daewangam landscape.
- **Access:** Exterior grounds and exhibit access differ; verify with the lighthouse authority.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Regional Office of Oceans and Fisheries](https://ulsan.mof.go.kr/)

### 14) 🏖️ **Ilsan Beach and Jujeon pebble coast** — 🔎 VERIFIED COASTAL PLACES
- **What:** Dong-gu shore destinations for walking and sea views; November is not swimming season.
- **Hours/price:** Public shore access is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Tourism](https://tour.ulsan.go.kr/)

### 15) 🐳 **Jangsaengpo Whale Culture Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Cultural complex interpreting Jangsaengpo’s whaling-era town and modern whale-conservation history.
- **Hours/price:** Check the integrated Jangsaengpo ticket and Monday closures.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Whale Culture Zone](https://www.whalecity.kr/)

### 16) 🐋 **Jangsaengpo Whale Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Museum about whale biology and Ulsan’s whaling history.
- **Hours/price:** Ticketed; verify current operating days.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Whale Culture Zone](https://www.whalecity.kr/)

### 17) ⛴️ **Whale Sea Travel Ship** — ⏳ SEASONAL/WEATHER-DEPENDENT
- **What:** Operator-run coastal cruise; whale sightings are never guaranteed.
- **Status:** Check whether November sailings are offered and confirm weather on departure day.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Whale Sea Travel Ship](https://www.whalecity.kr/)

### 18) 🌅 **Ganjeolgot and lighthouse** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** East-coast cape known for sunrise views and a large postbox landmark.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor access is free; lighthouse interior access may be restricted.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulsan Tourism](https://tour.ulsan.go.kr/)

### 19) 🏖️ **Jinha Beach and Myeongseondo** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Ulju coastal beach and nearby island landscape.
- **Hours/price:** Public shore access is free; island/light programs require current notices.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ulju Tourism](https://tour.ulju.ulsan.kr/)

- **Hours/price:** Trails are free; check weather, daylight, and route closures.

### 21) 🌾 **Ganwoljae silver-grass plateau** — 🔎 VERIFIED HIKE

- **What:** Buddhist temple below Gajisan in Ulju.
