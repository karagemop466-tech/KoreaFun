Seoul is the trip’s main base. This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** under the [manual verification protocol](audit/VERIFICATION-PROTOCOL.md). The former 363-entry version mixed real landmarks and a few confirmed 2026 events with semantic duplicates, generic venue types, out-of-city padding, and several “confirmed” dates that do not match current official pages.

> **How to read this file**
> - ✅ **Confirmed event** — an organizer, venue, league, or official city/festival calendar has published 2026 dates that overlap the trip.
> - 🔎 **Verified place/activity** — the named place exists in an official source; ordinary hours and prices can still change.
> - ⏳ **Re-check** — a real recurring program or planned item whose November 2026 schedule is unpublished, conflicting, or still only on a planning calendar.
> - Myeong-dong detail lives in [`myeongdong.md`](myeongdong.md). Everland, Korean Folk Village, and Hoam belong in [`yongin.md`](yongin.md). Paradise City / Cimer belong in [`incheon.md`](incheon.md).

- **What:** A themed exhibition on **Kim Jeong-hui (1786–1856)**, pen name Chusa — the calligrapher who invented the *chusache* style and is, more or less, the most revered brush artist in Korean history. Shown alongside the circle of scholars and disciples around him.

## Palaces and royal heritage

### 28) 🏯 **Gyeongbokgung** — 🔎 VERIFIED PALACE
- **What:** Joseon main palace, with the twice-daily royal-guard ceremony on open days.
- **Hours/price:** Closed Tuesday. Adult admission is charged; **hanbok wearers enter free**. Ceremony times and last entry change — use the official palace page, not a copied timetable.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Royal Palaces and Tombs Center](https://royal.khs.go.kr)

### 29) 🌸 **Changdeokgung and Huwon (Secret Garden)** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SITE
- **What:** UNESCO palace plus timed Huwon garden tours. Early November is peak foliage season.
- **Reservations:** Huwon tickets are released on a short window on [cdg.go.kr](https://www.cdg.go.kr); October–November slots sell out. Closed Monday.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Changdeokgung](https://www.cdg.go.kr) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org)

### 30) 🌙 **Changgyeonggung** — 🔎 VERIFIED PALACE
- **What:** Palace with Chunjangji pond and Korea’s first Western-style royal greenhouse (1909). Fall Mulbit Yeonhwa is entry #21, not a separate guaranteed show.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Royal Palaces and Tombs Center](https://royal.khs.go.kr)

### 31) 🏰 **Deoksugung and the stonewall walk** — 🔎 VERIFIED PALACE
- **What:** Compact palace by City Hall with Seokjojeon and the Doldam-gil walk. Often open later than the other palaces; confirm winter last entry.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Royal Palaces and Tombs Center](https://royal.khs.go.kr)

### 32) 🌳 **Gyeonghuigung** — 🔎 VERIFIED PALACE
- **What:** Quieter western palace next to Seoul Museum of History. Outdoor grounds are free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Royal Palaces and Tombs Center](https://royal.khs.go.kr)

### 33) 🛕 **Jongmyo** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SHRINE
- **What:** Royal ancestral shrine. Weekdays are typically guided-tour only; Saturday viewing rules differ. Closed Tuesday.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Royal Palaces and Tombs Center](https://royal.khs.go.kr) · [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org)

### 34) 🏡 **Unhyeongung** — 🔎 VERIFIED RESIDENCE
- **What:** Residence where King Gojong grew up; free municipal historic house in Insadong.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [unhyeongung.or.kr](http://www.unhyeongung.or.kr)

### 35) 🧱 **Hanyangdoseong (Seoul City Wall) and gates** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE WALK
- **What:** 18.6 km fortress circuit. Useful November sections: Naksan, Namsan, Inwangsan, and Sungnyemun / Heunginjimun exteriors. Sukjeongmun and some Bugaksan gates require ID.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [seoulcitywall.seoul.go.kr](https://seoulcitywall.seoul.go.kr) · [Sungnyemun trail](https://seoulcitywall.seoul.go.kr/en/wallcourse/5.do)

### 36) 🏛️ **Cheong Wa Dae (Blue House) grounds** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Former presidential complex, now a public historic site. Foreign visitors typically register with a passport; closed Monday.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [cheongwadae.kr](https://www.cheongwadae.kr)

### 37) ⛩️ **Seonjeongneung Royal Tombs** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO SITE
- **What:** Joseon royal tombs in Gangnam. Cheap, quiet, and in foliage in early November.
