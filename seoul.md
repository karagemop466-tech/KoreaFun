## 11) 🍂 Autumn foliage in Seoul — 🔁 seasonal
- **When:** Peak foliage in central Seoul is typically **late Oct – early Nov** — right in your window (official forecast published ~late Sept by the Korea Meteorological Administration / Visit Korea)
- **Where:** Namsan Park + N Seoul Tower, Deoksugung Palace, Seoul Forest, Gyeongbokgung, Bukhansan (all subway-accessible)
- **Price:** Parks free; palaces ~₩3,000 (~$2); N Seoul Tower observatory ~₩21,000 (~$15)
- **Official sources:** [Visit Korea autumn guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr) · [english.visitseoul.net](https://english.visitseoul.net)
- **Beginner notes:** Rent a hanbok (traditional dress) near Gyeongbokgung — palace + hanbok + fall colors = the classic first-timer photo moment.

## 12) 🕹️ PC Bang (Korean gaming cafe) — 🔁 always on
- **Where:** Any "PC방" (PC bang) — Hongdae, Gangnam, Myeongdong, everywhere
- **Price:** ~₩1,500–2,500/hour (~$1–2) + cheap ramen/drinks
- **Beginner notes:** Show ID/passport at the counter, grab a seat, log in with a temp account. League of Legends, Overwatch, Valorant, FC Online, StarCraft — all installed. The local multiplayer culture is part of the trip.
- **Esports heads-up:** The LCK pro season is in off-season in November, and the **LoL Worlds 2026 Grand Final is Nov 14** in Brooklyn, NY — a PC Bang is the best place to watch it live with locals ([lolesports.com](https://lolesports.com)).
