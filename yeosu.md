# 🌅 Yeosu — Korea's South Coast Beauty (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's Mediterranean jewel** — a coastal city on the southern tip of Korea, famous for its **dramatic nighttime bay views**, **maritime cable car**, island gardens, and famous *gopchang* dining. Home to Korea's best-preserved maritime history at *Admiral Yi Sun-sin's* command base. Yeosu is the south coast terminus of the KTX line (via transfer at Suncheon KTX) — covered in your Seoul→Busan→south coast route.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering **Odongdo Island**, **Yeosu Maritime Cable Car**, **Hyangiram Hermitage**, **Yi Sun-sin Plaza**, and **Dolsan Park sunset**.

## 🏝️ THE headline event — the Island Expo is on while you're here

### 1) 🏝️ **2026 Yeosu World Island Exhibition (2026여수세계섬박람회)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — **Sep 5 – Nov 4, 2026**
- **What:** The **world's first international exposition themed on islands**, under the slogan *"Island, connecting the Ocean and the Future."* Eight themed pavilions (Theme Island, Marine Ecology, Future Island, Culture Island, Treasure Island, International Exchange, plus restaurant/market islands), a UAM (urban air mobility) aircraft and hydrogen-vessel display, a 3,000-seat open-air seaside stage running **133 performances** across the run, and island-hopping programmes on Gaedo and Geumodo. 30 countries participating.
- **⏱️ Your window:** The Expo runs until **Wednesday, Nov 4, 2026** — your trip starts Oct 31, so you catch the **final five days**. After Nov 4 it is gone.
- **Dates/Hours:** Sep 5 – Nov 4, 2026 · Main venue daily **10:00–21:00** (ticket office 09:30–20:00; last entry 20:00) · Sub-venues 10:00–18:00
- **Venues:** Main site **Dolsan Jinmo district** (196 Gangnam-haean-ro, Dolsan-eup) · sub-venues at the **Yeosu World Expo 2012 site**, **Gaedo** and **Geumodo** islands
- **Price (official):** Adult **₩15,000** · Youth ₩9,000 · Child ₩6,000 · Family pass ₩32,000 · group and concession rates lower
- **Official sources:** [yeosu2026.or.kr](https://yeosu2026.or.kr) · [hours & pricing](https://yeosu2026.or.kr/content/4_1) · [tickets](https://yeosu2026.or.kr/content/4_2) · English booking via [NOL World / Interpark](https://world.nol.com/en/ticket/places/25001139/products/25013044)
- **Beginner notes:** This is the single biggest reason to put Yeosu on your itinerary in the **first week** rather than later. Site is on Dolsan Island — the Expo runs shuttle buses; allow a full day. **An Expo ticket also gets you free entry to some city museums** (e.g. the Jinnamgwan relics hall) — keep your stub.

### 38) 🛕 Hyangiram Hermitage (향일암) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A 644 CE hermitage clinging to a cliff at the southern tip of Dolsan Island, reached through natural rock crevices — **one of Korea's four great Gwaneum sites**
- **Hours:** Daily **04:00–20:00** (last entry ~18:00) — opens before dawn specifically for sunrise visitors
- **Price:** **FREE** — Korea's Jogye Order temples, including Hyangiram and Heungguksa, **abolished the cultural-heritage admission fee in May 2023** (the government now covers it). It was ₩2,000 before that; ignore older guides that still quote a fee.
- **Parking:** Public lot — first hour free, then ₩200/10 min (₩5,000 daily max)
- **Official sources:** [Yeosu 10 Scenic Views — Hyangiram](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/hyangilam) · [Yeosu Nangman Bus course 1 (lists 입장료 무료)](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/city_tour/course_01)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 111 from Yeosu, about 1 hour. **The sunrise view over the South Sea is the reason people come** — around 07:05 in November. The climb is 10 min by stairs or 15 min on the gentler path; the last stretch squeezes through rock clefts.

- **Price:** Savings of **₩5,000–₩15,000**

### 99) 🧳 Yeosu luggage storage & station facilities — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Coin lockers at Yeosu-Expo Station and the bus terminal, letting you do the city on a train-day layover
- **Hours:** Roughly **06:00–23:00**
- **Price:** **₩2,000–₩5,000** per day

- **Beginner notes:** **Yeosu works as a long stopover** — the station is walking distance from Odongdo and the aquarium.

### 100) 🗺️ Yeosu half-day route from the KTX station — 🔁 planning
- **What:** Arrive Yeosu-Expo → Aqua Planet or Sky Tower → walk the waterfront to Odongdo → Jasan Park → cable car → Jongpo dinner → evening train
- **Hours:** About 6 hours
- **Price:** **₩30,000–₩60,000**
- **Official source:** [yeosu.go.kr](https://www.yeosu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Everything on this route is within 3 km of the station** — no transport needed beyond the cable car.
