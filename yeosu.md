# 🌅 Yeosu — Korea's South Coast Beauty (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's Mediterranean jewel** — a coastal city on the southern tip of Korea, famous for its **dramatic nighttime bay views**, **maritime cable car**, island gardens, and famous *gopchang* dining. Home to Korea's best-preserved maritime history at *Admiral Yi Sun-sin's* command base. Yeosu is the south coast terminus of the KTX line (via transfer at Suncheon KTX) — covered in your Seoul→Busan→south coast route.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering **Odongdo Island**, **Yeosu Maritime Cable Car**, **Hyangiram Hermitage**, **Yi Sun-sin Plaza**, and **Dolsan Park sunset**.

**Official Yeosu sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Yeosu (official tourism): [yscity.go.kr](http://www.yscity.go.kr)
- Yeosu Expo Memorial Park: [expo.go.kr](http://www.expo.go.kr)
- Yi Sun-sin Plaza: [yscity.go.kr](https://yscity.go.kr)

**Getting there:** KTX Seoul → Suncheon KTX (~2 hr 50 min) → bus/ITX train to Yeosu (~40 min) · KTX Busan → Suncheon KTX (~1 hr 50 min) → Yeosu (~40 min) · Direct intercity bus from Seoul Express Bus Terminal (4 hr)

## 🌊 Coast (the headline)

### 1) 🚡 **Yeosu Maritime Cable Car** — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** The world's largest urban cable car system — rides across the bay from Dolsan Park to Odongdo Island over the ocean — 360° sunset sea views
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–21:30** (Nov winter hours may close earlier)
- **Price:** Round-trip adult **₩22,000** (~$16) Crystal Cabin; Crystal Cabin Round Trip **₩26,000**
- **Official source:** [ycablecar.com](http://www.ycablecar.com)
- **Beginner notes:** **Sunset ride** recommended; take an evening "Crystal" cabin for the most romantic experience.

### 2) 🌿 **Odongdo Island** (오동도) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Korea's signature camellia-covered seaside island — about 1.5 km walking loop — 70+ species of camellias bloom in winter (peak Dec–Feb). **Huinnyeoul** of Yeosu coast begins here
- **Hours:** Daily **06:00–22:00** (free walk; ferry 24/7)
- **Price:** **FREE**; round-trip ferry **₩2,500** adult
- **Official source:** [yscity.go.kr](http://www.yscity.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bring a light — short loop 1–2 hrs.

### 3) 🌅 **Hyangiram Hermitage (향일암)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Centuries-old Buddhist hermitage perched dramatically on a 30 m cliff edge facing the South Sea. Stunning sunrise spot
- **Hours:** Daily **05:00–18:00** (Nov winter)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [yscity.go.kr](http://www.yscity.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 30-min bus from downtown.

### 4) 🌃 **Dolsan Park (달산공원)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** Coastal hilltop park framing the bay, with a **Turtle Ship (Geobukson) statue**, gardens, and night city lights spanning the bay
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–22:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [yscity.go.kr](http://www.yscity.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Nighttime most essential.

### 5) 🌌 **Yeosu Marina Bay & Yi Sun-sin Plaza** — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A dramatic plaza on a manmade pool sea. The **Aqua Planet Yeosu** aquarium flanks the plaza; the LED lighting, fountain show, and bay-side vistas are Yeosu's signature postcard
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–midnight
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [yscity.go.kr](http://www.yscity.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Take 18:00 fountain show.

### 6) 🌊 Yi Sun-sin Bridge (이 성인의 다리) Night View — 🔁 nightly (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's iconic suspension bridge, named after the legendary **Admiral Yi Sun-sin**, with brilliant night illumination
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 (lights 18:00–23:00)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [yscity.go.kr](http://www.yscity.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Photographers' favorite.

### 7) 🏖️ Manseongni Black Sand Beach (만성리 검은모래해변) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's signature *black-sand* beach from volcanic olivine particles — black sand black pebbles
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [yscity.go.kr](http://www.yscity.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Distinctive for Korean coast.

### 8) 🏯 **Jinnamgwan Hall** — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Yeosu's representative Joseon-era naval command hall (1603) under Yi Sun-sin — Korea's largest such hall
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [yscity.go.kr](http://www.yscity.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 5-min drive from Yi Sun-sin Plaza.

### 9) 🛶 **Aqua Planet Yeosu** (아쿠아플라넷 여수) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Top ocean aquarium housed in former 2012 Expo marine complex; 280+ marine species
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–19:00**
- **Price:** Adult **₩26,500** (~$19)
- **Official source:** [aquaplanet.co.kr/yeosu](https://www.aquaplanet.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Right next to Yi Sun-sin Plaza.

## 🧭 Beginner-friendly essentials

- **Travel to Yeosu:** KTX Seoul → Suncheon (≈2h 50m) → ITX train to Yeosu (~50 min). Or **direct intercity bus Seoul → Yeosu 4 hr**
- **Main's stay:** 2 nights covers the south coast. Day 1 — Cable car + Odongdo + Dolsan sunset + Yi Sun-sin Plaza fountain; Day 2 — Suncheon Bay Wetland + Naganeupseong
- **Night photo:** The Bay Bridge & cable car LED are top 1 for Korea south coast
- **Currency, transport:** Korea T-money works on buses; cards widely accepted
