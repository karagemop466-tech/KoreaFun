# 🌅 Yeosu — verified events and activities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Yeosu is the south-coast terminus of direct KTX trains from Yongsan. This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** from official city, operator, heritage, and organizing-committee pages. It deliberately omits generic venue types, invented “districts,” duplicate attractions, and unannounced festivals.

> **How to read this file**
> - ✅ **Confirmed event** — the organizer has published 2026 dates.
> - 🔎 **Verified place/activity** — the place exists in an official source; ordinary hours and prices can still change.
> - ⏳ **Re-check** — a real recurring program whose exact November 2026 schedule is not published.
> - “Free” is stated only where the cited official page says so. Ferry, show, and operator timetables must be checked shortly before travel.

**Official planning links:** [Yeosu Tourism](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour?main=true) · [monthly culture calendar](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/culture_festa/month_event) · [KORAIL](https://www.letskorail.com)

## City waterfront and historic center

### 2) 🌳 **Odongdo Island** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Yeosu’s camellia island, reached on foot by breakwater; the city says camellias begin flowering around November and continue into spring.
- **Hours/price:** Official city listing says year-round and free; the optional Camellia Train has a separate timetable and fare.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Odongdo](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/island_beach?mode=view&idx=234) · [Camellia Train information](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/odongdo_open/facilities)

### 3) 🚡 **Yeosu Maritime Cable Car** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Cable car between Dolsan Park and Jasan Park, crossing the harbor entrance; standard and glass-floor cabins are sold.
- **Hours/price:** Check the operator on the visit date. Weather can suspend service.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [operator](http://www.yeosucablecar.com) · [Yeosu Tourism](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/cablecar)

### 4) 🏞️ **Dolsan Park and Dolsan Bridge viewpoint** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Public hilltop park and established night-view stop beside the cable-car terminal. This is **Dolsan Park (돌산공원)**, not “Dalsan Park.”
- **Price:** Park access is free; cable car is separate.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism night-drive course](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/city_tour/night_view)

### 5) 🐢 **Yi Sun-sin Square and Jeolla Left Naval Headquarters heritage** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Waterfront square near Jinnamgwan and the old naval headquarters area, with Yi Sun-sin and turtle-ship interpretation.
- **Price:** Outdoor square is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Yi Sun-sin heritage walking tour](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/lee_sun_sin/storytelling)
- **Correction:** Admiral Yi Sun-sin’s tomb is in **Asan**, not on Dolsan Island. The former “Dolsan Yi Sun-sin Tomb” entry was removed.

### 6) 🏯 **Jinnamgwan** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL HERITAGE
- **What:** National Treasure No. 304, a major wooden hall associated with the Joseon naval headquarters. The present building dates to 1718; the first postwar hall on the site was built in 1599.
- **Access:** Check the city page for restoration or interior-access notices before visiting.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Jinnamgwan](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/jinnamgwan) · [Korea Heritage Service search](https://www.heritage.go.kr)

### 7) 🇳🇱 **Hamel Exhibition Hall** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** City museum about Hendrick Hamel and his group’s years in Joseon and departure from Yeosu.
- **Hours/price:** Official listing says **09:00–18:00, closed Monday, free**.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Hamel Exhibition Hall](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/island_beach?mode=view&idx=839)

### 8) 🎨 **Gosodong Angel Mural Village walking route** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** An official eight-section hillside walking route connecting the old center, Goso Fortress area, maritime views, and murals.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor route; free. Respect residents and avoid loud visits at night.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — route guide](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/walk/goso_angel)

### 9) 🌊 **Jongpo Marine Park and Yeosu Marine Park promenade** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Harbor promenade linking Yi Sun-sin Square, Jongpo, the Hamel area, and the night-sea viewpoints.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor public space; free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Marine Park walk](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/walk/yeosu_marine_park)

### 10) 🌉 **Dolsan Bridge, Geobukseon Bridge, and harbor night views** — 🔎 VERIFIED ACTIVITY
- **What:** A self-guided night-view route using established public viewpoints; no separate “Dolsan Bridge light show” is promised.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor viewpoints are free; lighting times can change.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — night-view course](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/city_tour/night_view)

## Dolsan, coast, and nature

### 16) 🛕 **Hyangiram Hermitage** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Cliffside Buddhist hermitage on Geumosan at the south end of Dolsan, reached by a steep stepped approach and narrow rock passages.
- **Hours/price:** Re-check the official listing; do not rely on old admission-fee guides.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Hyangiram](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/hyangilam)

### 17) 🪨 **Musulmok beach and historic site** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · NEW
- **What:** Dolsan pebble shore and Yi Sun-sin-associated battle site; a practical stop on the way to Hyangiram.
- **Hours/price:** City listing says year-round and free, with free parking.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Musulmok](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/island_beach?mode=view&idx=245)

### 18) 🏖️ **Bangjukpo Beach and pine grove** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · NEW
- **What:** Small Dolsan beach backed by pines. November is for a coast walk, not swimming.
- **Hours/price:** City listing says year-round and free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Bangjukpo](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/island_beach?mode=view&idx=283)

### 19) 🏖️ **Manseongni Black Sand Beach** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Dark-sand and pebble shore north of central Yeosu, near the rail-bike corridor.
- **Hours/price:** Beach access is free; commercial activities are separate.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — beaches directory](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/island_beach)

### 20) 🚲 **Yeosu Ocean Rail Bike** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Pedal rail-bike attraction along the former coastal rail alignment near Manseongni.
- **Hours/price:** Reservation slots and fares are operator-controlled; check on the visit date.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — leisure facilities](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/experience/leisure_facility)

### 21) 🏙️ **Ungcheon Waterfront Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · NEW
- **What:** Urban waterfront park with sandy shore and walking paths; camping is separately charged.
- **Hours/price:** City listing says park access and parking are free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Ungcheon Waterfront Park](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/culture_scenic_spot?mode=view&idx=870)

### 22) 🛕 **Heungguksa Temple and Yeongchwisan trails** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Historic temple and mountain trails north of the city. The famous azalea festival is in spring, **not** in the trip window.
- **Hours/price:** Verify temple access locally; mountain trails are weather-dependent.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — mountains and temples](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/mt_temple) · [Yeongchwisan](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/yeongchwisan)

### 23) 🏥 **Aeyangwon History Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · NEW
- **What:** Museum in the former Aeyang Hospital building documenting the institution’s history and Hansen’s-disease care.
- **Hours:** City listing says 09:00–18:00 and closed public holidays; call before making the out-of-center trip.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Aeyangwon History Museum](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/culture_scenic_spot?mode=view&idx=336)

## Islands — allow a full day and verify sailings

### 24) 🥾 **Geumodo Bireong-gil** — 🔎 VERIFIED ACTIVITY
- **What:** Multi-section cliff-and-coast walking route on Geumodo, one of Yeosu’s official “10 sights.”
- **Hours/price:** Trail access is free; ferry and local transport cost extra. Wind can cancel sailings.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Bireong-gil](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/walk/geumodo) · [KOMSA ferry safety information](https://www.komsa.or.kr)

### 25) 🏝️ **Gaedo island and People’s Trail** — 🔎 VERIFIED ACTIVITY · NEW
- **What:** Island walking destination and one of the 2026 Expo’s satellite-program areas.
- **Hours/price:** Ferry-dependent; confirm which Expo programs remain on Nov 1–4.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Gaedo](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/island_beach?mode=view&idx=415) · [2026 city program guide (PDF)](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/pdfjs_new/pdf/04_tour_gaedo.pdf)

### 26) 🦕 **Sado Island geology and dinosaur-track sites** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Island group known for coastal geology and protected dinosaur-footprint sites.
- **Hours/price:** Ferry-dependent; low-tide phenomena are not guaranteed on a casual visit.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Sado island travel](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/leisure/island/sado)

### 27) 🌊 **Geomundo and Baekdo** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Remote island destination and scenic offshore rock group, officially one of Yeosu’s “10 sights.”
- **Hours/price:** This is not a casual half-day trip. Verify round-trip ferry and excursion availability before leaving central Yeosu.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Geomundo/Baekdo](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/10tour/geomundo)

### 28) 🏖️ **Ando and Ando Island walking** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE · NEW
- **What:** Island connected to Geumodo by bridge, with village, coast, and walking options.
- **Hours/price:** City listing says island access itself is year-round and free; ferry and transport are separate.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Yeosu Tourism — Ando](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/travel/island_beach?idx=505&mode=view)

## Removed during this verification pass

The previous 100-entry version contained real places mixed with duplicates, planning advice presented as “activities,” and unsupported claims. This rebuild removed, among other items:

- the nonexistent **Dolsan Yi Sun-sin Tomb** (the admiral’s tomb is in Asan);
- the unverified **Yeosu Maritime Cable Car Museum** and **Yi Sun-sin Memorial Hall**;
- a supposed free nightly **Big-O Fountain Show** (the official listing describes a scheduled, ticketed show);
- an invented **Odongdo Camellia Festival** in early November;
- duplicate cable-car, Geomundo, Naganeupseong, and Suncheon Bay entries;
- generic “sushi-boat restaurants,” “marina yacht club,” food alleys, budgeting, medical, luggage, and photo-list entries that were not discrete verified attractions;
- out-of-city padding from Hadong, Suncheon, Boseong, and Gwangyang. Those may be valid destinations, but they are not Yeosu entries.

**Final re-check:** On the week of travel, use the [city monthly calendar](https://www.yeosu.go.kr/tour/culture_festa/month_event), [Expo organizer](https://yeosu2026.or.kr), and individual operator pages. Do not infer a 2026 date from an older festival edition.
