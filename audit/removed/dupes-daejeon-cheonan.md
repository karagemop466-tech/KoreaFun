## 81) 🔬 National Science Museum of Korea (국립중앙과학관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's national science museum in Daedeok Science Town — permanent halls on natural history, technology and space, plus a planetarium and an outdoor rocket park
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:30–17:50** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Permanent halls **FREE**; planetarium ₩2,000; special exhibitions extra
- **Official source:** [science.go.kr](https://www.science.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daejeon Station (~30 min). Free entry to the main halls makes this the single best-value day in Daejeon.

## 99) 🌳 Hanbat Arboretum (한밭수목원) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** **Korea's largest inner-city artificial arboretum** — east and west gardens either side of the Expo park, with 1,700+ species and a tropical greenhouse
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (Nov; closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daejeon Line 1 Government Complex Station. Free, huge, and **peak foliage is the first half of November**.

## 91) 🖼️ Daejeon Museum of Art (대전시립미술관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The city's main contemporary art museum, next to the Expo park — strong on media art, reflecting the science-city identity
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩1,000**; often free for special city programmes
- **Official source:** [dma.daejeon.go.kr](https://dma.daejeon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daejeon Line 1 Government Complex Station. ₩1,000 for a full municipal art museum is close to free.

## 124) 🛕 Gakwonsa Temple & Great Bronze Buddha (각원사) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 15 m, 60-tonne seated bronze Buddha on the slope of Taejosan — one of the largest in East Asia, reached up 203 steps
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [cheonan.go.kr](https://www.cheonan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Cheonan Station. Built in 1977 as a prayer for reunification. The view back over Cheonan from the top is excellent.

## 22) 🎭 Daejeon Arts Center November Concert Series (대전예술의전당) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daejeon's premier performing arts complex in Dunsan-dong (Arboretum complex). Features the Ensemble Hall and Art Hall hosting classical philharmonic concerts, ballet, and traditional Korean theater
- **Hours:** Performance times vary
- **Price:** Varies by show (~₩10,000–₩50,000)
- **Official source:** [djac.or.kr](https://www.djac.or.kr) · Seo-gu Dunsan-dong (Hanbat Arboretum complex)
- **Beginner notes:** Adjacent to Hanbat Arboretum (#16) and Daejeon Museum of Art (#19).

## 24) 🌳 Ppuri Park - Surname Heritage & Patriot Park (뿌리공원) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Unique riverfront park at the foot of Mt. Bo문산 in Jung-gu dedicated to Korean family lineage and patriotic ancestors. Features 244 stone sculptures representing Korean family clans, a suspension bridge, water pedal boats, and forest walking paths
- **Hours:** Park open **06:00–22:00** daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [junggu.daejeon.kr](https://www.junggu.daejeon.kr) · Jung-gu Ppuri-gongwon-ro
- **Beginner notes:** Peaceful, scenic river valley park 15 minutes by bus from central Daejeon.

## 103) 🌲 Jangtaesan Recreational Forest (장태산자연휴양림) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Korea's best **metasequoia** forest — a canopy skywalk 20 m up through the redwoods, spectacular when the needles turn rust-orange in November
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov)
- **Price:** Adult **₩2,000**; parking ₩3,000
- **Official source:** [daejeon.go.kr](https://www.daejeon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 22 from Daejeon Station (~50 min). **Metasequoia colour peaks in early-to-mid November** — this is the single most photogenic thing near Daejeon in your window.

## 127) ✉️ Postal Museum of Korea (우정박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)

## 123) 🍲 Byeongcheon Sundae Street (병천순대거리) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Korea's most famous *sundae* (blood sausage) town — a street of specialist restaurants serving Byeongcheon-style sundae and sundae-gukbap
- **Hours:** Most **09:00–21:00**
- **Price:** Sundae-gukbap **₩9,000–₩12,000**
- **Official source:** [cheonan.go.kr](https://www.cheonan.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Cheonan Station (~40 min). Byeongcheon sundae is thin-skinned and vegetable-heavy — the benchmark version of the dish.

## 85) 💰 Korea Minting & Security Printing Currency Museum (화폐박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's official mint museum — the history of Korean money from Goryeo coins to modern banknotes, plus counterfeiting-prevention technology
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–17:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [komsco.com](https://www.komsco.com)
- **Beginner notes:** In Daedeok Science Town. You can buy commemorative medal strikes in the shop — a genuinely unusual souvenir.

## 135) 🌾 Gongju — Gongsanseong Fortress, UNESCO Baekje (공산성) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The hilltop Baekje royal fortress at Gongju, part of the **UNESCO Baekje Historic Areas** — a 2.6 km rampart walk above the Geum River
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩1,200**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1477) · [gongju.go.kr](https://www.gongju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daejeon (~50 min). The rampart circuit takes about an hour and the river view is excellent in autumn.
