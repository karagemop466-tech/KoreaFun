### 2) 🏯 Gyeonggijeon Shrine (경기전) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** 1410 CE Joseon-era Jinseng shrine dedicated to King Taejo (founder of Joseon), holding his royal portrait. Beautiful old pine tree at the main gate is one of Korea's most photographed
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2)
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Photo-worthy gate.
