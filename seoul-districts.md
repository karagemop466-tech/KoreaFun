## 🏬 Dongdaemun — details

### 1) 💡 **DDP “Dream in Light” — nightly media lighting** — 🔎 VERIFIED CITY PROGRAM — free
- **What:** DDP's permanent, year-round evening lighting programme on Zaha Hadid's 222 m façade: *2026 Seoul Colour* (30″), *Sky Light* (10′, colour driven by live Seoul weather data), *BIG MOVE with Lia Kim* (7′47″, motion-capture of the 1MILLION dance artist) and *COSMO WALKER* (3′30″). Whole cycle ≈ 25 minutes.
- **When/where:** **Jan 9 – Dec 31, 2026**, **18:00–22:00 on the hour**, at the DDP front façade, in front of the Ruins Exhibition Hall, and above Mirae-ro. Runs every night of your trip.
- **Price:** **Free.** Produced by Seoul Metropolitan Government / Seoul Design Foundation.
- **Why it matters here:** the seoul.md DDP entry (#53) covers the building; this is the actual nightly *thing to watch*, and it is the easiest free evening plan in Dongdaemun after the markets open.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Seoul Culture Portal event page](https://culture.seoul.go.kr/culture/culture/cultureEvent/view.do?cultcode=156491&menuNo=200013) — period, 18:00–22:00 hourly, free, contents table · [DDP official site](https://www.ddp.or.kr/?menuno=228) (site hours 10:00–20:00; closed Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok day)

### 2) 🏛️ **DDP Architecture Tour 2026 — free, with a daily English run** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR PROGRAM — free
- **What:** A 60-minute guided walk of Zaha Hadid's building and the Dongdaemun site's history, run by Seoul Design Foundation.
- **When:** **Tuesday–Sunday**, three departures: **10:30 (Korean), 13:30 (English), 15:30 (Korean)**. Closed Mondays and on New Year's Day / Seollal / Chuseok day.
- **Price:** **Free.** Ages **13+** (under-13s need an accompanying adult). Meet at the **DDP Museum 1F tour desk**.
- **Booking:** online reservation recommended (individuals 1–8, groups 9–20); leftover places are taken on the spot from 10 minutes before departure. Enquiries 02-2153-0310/0311, ddptour@seouldesign.or.kr, Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00 (lunch 12:00–13:00).
- **⚠️ Don't trust third-party listings:** an aggregator page circulates a "₩10,000 adult / ₩5,000 child" fare for this tour. The operator's own page says **free** — book through DDP.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [DDP — 건축투어 program page](http://www.ddp.or.kr/?menuno=782) · [same notice, DDP news board](https://ddp.or.kr/index.html?menuno=239&siteno=2&bbsno=1&boardno=145&bbstopno=1&act=view&subno=1)

### 3) 🏟️ **Dongdaemun History Museum & Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM — free
- **What:** Two small free museums inside the DDP park, run by Seoul Museum of History. The History Museum displays what the 2008–09 excavation of the old Dongdaemun Stadium site produced — the buried **Hanyangdoseong wall, the chiseong bastion, the Igansumun water gate**, and Joseon military-camp (Hadogam / Hullyeonwon) remains, plus a children's gallery. The Stadium Memorial covers the demolished 1925 stadium itself.
- **Price:** **Free.**
- **Hours (resolved in round 4):** **10:00–18:00, last entry 17:30, with a daily closure from 12:00 to 13:00.** **Closed Mondays**, Jan 1, and Seollal/Chuseok day. Free.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Dongdaemun History Museum — exhibition guide](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/exhibition/ddmHistory/ddmExhGuide.jsp) · [Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/exhibition/ddmGround/ddmGExhGuide.jsp) · [branch visitor page with hours and the lunchtime closure](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/www/intro/annexIntro/annex_18/annex_18_01.jsp) · [network index](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/NR_index.do)

### 4) 🧱 **Hanyangdoseong Museum (한양도성박물관)** — 🔎 VERIFIED CITY MUSEUM — free
- **What:** The city-wall museum, at the foot of **Heunginjimun (Dongdaemun)** in the Seoul Design Support Center, floors 1–3. Pairs directly with the Naksan / Heunginjimun wall walk already in [`seoul.md`](seoul.md) #36.
- **Hours/price:** **Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00, last admission 17:30. Free. Closed Mondays and Jan 1.**
- **Now showing:** special exhibition **《여민공수 與民共守》** listed as *전시중* (on view) on the museum site at review; several 360° online exhibitions are also free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Seoul Museum of History — Hanyangdoseong Museum](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/NR_index.do) (hours/closures/free admission printed on the landing page) · corroborated by [Seoul city culture directory](https://news.seoul.go.kr/culture/archives/505240)

### 5) 🪆 **Seoul Folk Flea Market (서울풍물시장)** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET — free entry
- **What:** The city-run successor to the old Hwanghak-dong / Dongdaemun Stadium flea market: two floors of antiques, vinyl, old cameras, militaria, hanbok and secondhand everything, plus **Cheongchun 1-beonga**, a recreated 1960s–70s shopping street, and a cheap food alley.
- **Hours (operator notice):** market **10:00–19:00**; **food alley 10:00–22:00**; Cheongchun 1-beonga 10:00–18:00; **weekend merchants' street market Sat–Sun 10:00–18:00**. **Closed every Tuesday.**
- **⚠️ Two versions of the closure rule are online.** Older city articles say "2nd and 4th Tuesday"; the market's own 2026 notices say **every Tuesday**. Follow the operator.
- **Getting there:** Sinseol-dong Station (Lines 1, 2 branch, Ui–Sinseol) exit 9, ~290 m. One stop from Dongdaemun — easy to bolt onto a DDP evening.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [pungmul.or.kr — monthly operating notice](https://pungmul.or.kr/kwa-1117814-427) (hours table, Tuesday closure) · [notice board](https://pungmul.or.kr/kwa-1117813)

### 6) 🌿 **Seoul Yangnyeongsi Herbal Medicine Museum / Seoul K-Medi Center** — ⏳ HOURS VERIFIED, FEE NOT
- **What:** Museum of Korean herbal medicine at the edge of **Seoul Yangnyeongsi**, the country's largest medicinal-herb market (Jegi-dong), with a foot-bath and herbal-pack experience room and a hanok annexe café.
- **Hours (official):** **Nov–Feb 10:00–17:00** (Mar–Oct 10:00–18:00). **Closed Mondays**, Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok. Your whole trip falls in the winter-hours season — it shuts at 17:00.

### 39) ⛔ **National Hangeul Museum — CLOSED until October 2028** — ⚠️ STATUS CORRECTION

## 🏬 Dongdaemun-gu — the Hongneung cluster (three sites, one stop)

Three of the round-3 entries sit within 400 m of each other at **Hongneung**, north-east of Dongdaemun (Line 6 **Korea University** exit 3, or Line 1 **Cheongnyangni** exit 2). Together they make a genuinely good half-day that almost no tourist does.

### 53) 🌲 **Hongneung Forest (홍릉숲 / 홍릉시험림)** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL FOREST — free
- **What:** Korea's **first arboretum**, planted from 1922 as the colonial forestry experiment station and still run as a research forest by the **National Institute of Forest Science**. About **2,035 plant species** in themed sections (medicinal plants, conifers, broadleaf), and it is registered as **National Forest Culture Asset No. 1**.
- **⭐ Why it is newly worth listing:** the forest was **reservation-only at weekends for 33 years** and only opened to weekday walk-ins on **March 28, 2026**. Most guidebooks still say "weekends only, book ahead."
- **Hours/price:** **Free.** The institute's own page gives **weekend hours Nov–Feb 09:00–17:00** (Mar–Oct 09:00–18:00); the city's post-opening listing gives **Tue–Fri 10:00–17:00** for weekdays. **Closed Mondays, May 1 and all public holidays.**
- **Free forest-guide walks (60–90 min):** Tue–Fri **10:30 / 13:30 / 15:30**, weekends **10:30 / 14:00**, booked on the Forest Service's 숲나들e platform (₩0) or by phone; walk-ins if places remain.
- **⚠️ Rules:** **no parking**, no food, no picnic mats, no pets. Take the subway.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [National Institute of Forest Science — Hongneung Forest visitor guide](http://know.nifos.go.kr/mobile/intro/guide.do) (free, closures, weekend hours, guide times) · [Seoul Metropolitan Government — Mediahub on the March 2026 weekday opening](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2017611)

### 54) 📜 **Sejong the Great Memorial Museum (세종대왕기념관)** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** A 1970s memorial hall for **King Sejong** run by the Sejong the Great Memorial Society: Hunminjeongeum materials, replica astronomical instruments (self-striking water clock, rain gauge), royal-tomb stonework moved here from the original Yeongneung, and a garden. **With the National Hangeul Museum closed until 2028 (see [`seoul.md`](seoul.md) #40), this is the main place in Seoul to see Hangeul-creation material during your trip.**
- **Hours/price:** **Nov–Feb 09:00–17:30** (Mar–Oct 09:00–18:00), last entry 30 minutes before closing. **₩3,000 adult / ₩1,500 youth (children included). Closed Mondays**, Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Korea Tourism Organization accessible-tourism database — Sejong the Great Museum](https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/ms/detail.do?cotId=652c5ef2-2f68-485c-b39a-11d3878f6428) (seasonal hours, fares); society site sejongkorea.org

### 55) ⛰️ **Yeonghwiwon & Sunginwon (영휘원·숭인원)** — ⏳ FEE VERIFIED, HOURS TO CONFIRM
- **What:** Two small **imperial-era garden tombs** in the same block: **Yeonghwiwon**, for Lady Sunheon Eom (mother of Crown Prince Yeong, d. 1911), and **Sunginwon**, for her grandson Yi Jin, who died at two. Managed by the **Royal Palaces and Tombs Center** as part of the Joseon Royal Tombs system, with a 600-year-old zelkova and, in November, a very quiet leaf-covered lawn.
- **Price (official fare table):** **₩1,000 adult** (₩800 for groups of 10+), for Korean and foreign visitors alike; **free for everyone on the last Wednesday of each month (Culture Day)** — in your window that is **Wed Nov 25, after departure**, so budget the ₩1,000. Dongdaemun-gu residents get 50% off.
- **⏳ Hours:** the site's own 관람정보 page returned **HTTP 500** on Aug 19. Comparable Seoul tombs in the same system run roughly **09:00–17:00/18:00 with ticketing to one hour before**, and all are **closed Mondays** — check royaltombs.cha.go.kr before going rather than trusting a number here.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Royal Tombs — Yeonghwiwon & Sunginwon](https://royaltombs.cha.go.kr/tombs/selectTombInfoList.do?tombseq=135&mn=RT_01_07_02) (location, history) · [Royal Tombs fare and Culture Day rules, incl. the Yeonghwiwon/Sunginwon resident discount](https://royaltombs.cha.go.kr/multiBbz/selectMultiBbzView.do?bbzId=tombs&id=58&mn=RT_01_07_04&no=58)

## 🏯 Central Seoul — round-4 details

### 67) 🏅 **Sohn Kee-chung Memorial Hall (손기정기념관)** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM — free
- **What:** The museum to **Sohn Kee-chung**, who won the 1936 Berlin Olympic marathon under a Japanese flag and covered the emblem with a laurel sapling on the podium. It occupies his old school, **Yangjeong Uisuk**, and opened in 2012 for his birth centenary; the sapling he was given is now the huge "Sohn Kee-chung monument tree" in the park outside.
- **Hours/price:** **Nov–Feb 10:00–17:00** (Mar–Oct 10:00–18:00), last admission one hour before closing. **Permanent exhibition free**; special exhibitions may charge. **Closed Mondays** (next day if Monday is a holiday), Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok.
- **Foreign-language help:** free smartphone loan with **English, Japanese, Chinese and German** audio guides — leave a passport or ID at the desk.
- **Where:** Sonkeechung-ro 101, Jung-gu — 15 min walk from Chungjeongno or Seoul Station; combine with Seosomun Shrine Museum (#48).
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [National Museum of Korean Contemporary History partner-museum record (seasonal hours, closures)](https://www.much.go.kr/cooperation/net/son.do) · [Jung-gu official tourism page](https://www.junggu.seoul.kr/tour/content.do?cmsid=14979&sf_category1=CWS1331&mode=view&cid=981688384)

### 68) 🔔 **Bosingak bell-striking ceremony (보신각 상설타종행사)** — ✅ RUNNING DAILY — free
- **What:** The city's daily noon bell ceremony at the **Bosingak** pavilion in Jongno — the bell that once opened and closed the city gates. It has run as a **permanent public ceremony since Nov 21, 2006**, and members of the public do the striking: **12 strikes at 12:00**, in period costume if you want, with a **certificate issued in the Seoul mayor's name**.
- **When:** **every day at 12:00 except Mondays**, plus closures for March 1, Liberation Day and New Year's Eve ceremonies, and in extreme heat/cold.
- **⭐ Tuesdays are the foreigner slot:** run with the Korea Tourism Organization for **foreign visitors on the spot** — **traditional-costume experience at 11:00** and **bell-striking at 11:40**, joined by signing the visitors' book. No online application needed. (Korean-resident online applications open on the 1st of each month for the following month, max 3 teams a day.)
- **Price:** **Free.**
- **Where:** Jongno 54 — **Jonggak Station exit 4**, 10 minutes' walk from Myeong-dong.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Seoul Culture Portal — Bosingak ceremony page](https://culture.seoul.go.kr/culture/main/contents.do?menuNo=200147) (daily noon, Monday closure, Tuesday foreigner programme) · [application page](https://culture.seoul.go.kr/culture/main/contents.do?menuNo=200049) · [Seoul Mediahub explainer](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/1088292)

### 69) 💂 **Deoksugung Royal Guard Changing Ceremony** — ✅ RUNNING DAILY — free
- **What:** The reconstructed Joseon palace-gate guard change in front of **Daehanmun**, running since 1996 — Korea's answer to the Changing of the Guard, in full costume with drums and a *sunra* patrol procession after the morning round. Deoksugung is [`myeongdong.md`](myeongdong.md) #31; this happens **outside the ticket gate, so it costs nothing**.
- **When:** **11:00 and 14:00 daily except Mondays**, about 15–20 minutes each. Some official-adjacent listings add a **15:30** round — check the board at the gate. **Cancelled in rain, snow and extreme heat/cold.**

- **Price:** **Free** to watch. Enquiries 02-2133-1117 / 120.
- **⚠️ Don't confuse it with Gyeongbokgung's**, which is a different ceremony at 10:00 and 14:00 and is **closed Tuesdays** (Deoksugung closes Mondays) — between the two you can catch a guard ceremony on any day of the week.
- **Sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** Seoul Metropolitan Government royal-guard programme (02-2133-1117) via [Deoksugung visitor listings](https://www.i-rang.net/place/deoksugung-seoul) · [ceremony timetable comparison (Gyeongbokgung vs Deoksugung, closure days)](https://namu.wiki/w/수문장%20교대의식) — *treated as corroboration only; times to confirm on the day*

### 83) 🏗️ **Seoul Architecture Fair at SETEC** — ⏳ ORGANIZER'S OWN PAGE CONTRADICTS ITSELF
- **What:** A consumer building-and-interiors fair (materials, prefab housing, interior products) at **SETEC**, the Gangnam exhibition centre at Hagyeoul Station — the district's second venue after COEX.

### 86) 🛍️ **Korea Sale FESTA 2026** — ⏳ STILL UNANNOUNCED — and a warning
- **What:** The government-backed nationwide shopping-discount period, listed as ⏳ in [`seoul.md`](seoul.md) #28. It matters for this file because it is the one thing that would change prices in **Myeong-dong, Dongdaemun and Gangnam** during the trip.
- **⚠️ Verification result — read this before believing a date:** searching for the 2026 dates returns a cluster of **AI-generated SEO pages** confidently quoting mutually exclusive schedules — "Nov 1–15", "Nov 1–30", "2,500 companies", "2,600 companies" — several of them claiming the writer *phoned the ministry* to confirm. **No official announcement from the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy or the Korea Sale FESTA committee was retrievable on Aug 19, 2026.** Recent editions have run for roughly the first three weeks of November, which is why this stays a ⏳ planning note and not a date.
- **What to do:** check **koreasalefesta.kr** in mid-October. If it lands in your window, department stores in all three districts run extra discounts and tax-refund events.
- **Sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** no official source found — the only hits were unverifiable aggregator posts, which is itself the finding.
