## 🏬 Dongdaemun — details

### 1) 💡 **DDP “Dream in Light” — nightly media lighting** — 🔎 VERIFIED CITY PROGRAM — free
- **What:** DDP's permanent, year-round evening lighting programme on Zaha Hadid's 222 m façade: *2026 Seoul Colour* (30″), *Sky Light* (10′, colour driven by live Seoul weather data), *BIG MOVE with Lia Kim* (7′47″, motion-capture of the 1MILLION dance artist) and *COSMO WALKER* (3′30″). Whole cycle ≈ 25 minutes.
- **When/where:** **Jan 9 – Dec 31, 2026**, **18:00–22:00 on the hour**, at the DDP front façade, in front of the Ruins Exhibition Hall, and above Mirae-ro. Runs every night of your trip.
- **Price:** **Free.** Produced by Seoul Metropolitan Government / Seoul Design Foundation.
- **Why it matters here:** the seoul.md DDP entry (#53) covers the building; this is the actual nightly *thing to watch*, and it is the easiest free evening plan in Dongdaemun after the markets open.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Seoul Culture Portal event page](https://culture.seoul.go.kr/culture/culture/cultureEvent/view.do?cultcode=156491&menuNo=200013) — period, 18:00–22:00 hourly, free, contents table · [DDP official site](https://www.ddp.or.kr/?menuno=228) (site hours 10:00–20:00; closed Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok day)

### 2) 🏛️ **DDP Architecture Tour 2026 — free, with a daily English run** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR PROGRAM — free
- **What:** A 60-minute guided walk of Zaha Hadid's building and the Dongdaemun site's history, run by Seoul Design Foundation.
- **When:** **Tuesday–Sunday**, three departures: **10:30 (Korean), 13:30 (English), 15:30 (Korean)**. Closed Mondays and on New Year's Day / Seollal / Chuseok day.
- **Price:** **Free.** Ages **13+** (under-13s need an accompanying adult). Meet at the **DDP Museum 1F tour desk**.
- **Booking:** online reservation recommended (individuals 1–8, groups 9–20); leftover places are taken on the spot from 10 minutes before departure. Enquiries 02-2153-0310/0311, ddptour@seouldesign.or.kr, Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00 (lunch 12:00–13:00).
- **⚠️ Don't trust third-party listings:** an aggregator page circulates a "₩10,000 adult / ₩5,000 child" fare for this tour. The operator's own page says **free** — book through DDP.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [DDP — 건축투어 program page](http://www.ddp.or.kr/?menuno=782) · [same notice, DDP news board](https://ddp.or.kr/index.html?menuno=239&siteno=2&bbsno=1&boardno=145&bbstopno=1&act=view&subno=1)

### 3) 🏟️ **Dongdaemun History Museum & Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM — free
- **What:** Two small free museums inside the DDP park, run by Seoul Museum of History. The History Museum displays what the 2008–09 excavation of the old Dongdaemun Stadium site produced — the buried **Hanyangdoseong wall, the chiseong bastion, the Igansumun water gate**, and Joseon military-camp (Hadogam / Hullyeonwon) remains, plus a children's gallery. The Stadium Memorial covers the demolished 1925 stadium itself.
- **Price:** **Free.**
- **⏳ Hours gap:** the museum group's index page publishes 09:00–18:00 for the Hanyangdoseong Museum but the Dongdaemun branch keeps a separate 관람정보 page that this pass did not retrieve — check it before you go, and assume **closed Mondays** like the rest of the network.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Dongdaemun History Museum — exhibition guide](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/exhibition/ddmHistory/ddmExhGuide.jsp) · [Dongdaemun Stadium Memorial](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/exhibition/ddmGround/ddmGExhGuide.jsp) · [network index](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/NR_index.do)

### 4) 🧱 **Hanyangdoseong Museum (한양도성박물관)** — 🔎 VERIFIED CITY MUSEUM — free
- **What:** The city-wall museum, at the foot of **Heunginjimun (Dongdaemun)** in the Seoul Design Support Center, floors 1–3. Pairs directly with the Naksan / Heunginjimun wall walk already in [`seoul.md`](seoul.md) #36.
- **Hours/price:** **Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00, last admission 17:30. Free. Closed Mondays and Jan 1.**
- **Now showing:** special exhibition **《여민공수 與民共守》** listed as *전시중* (on view) on the museum site at review; several 360° online exhibitions are also free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Seoul Museum of History — Hanyangdoseong Museum](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/scwm/NR_index.do) (hours/closures/free admission printed on the landing page) · corroborated by [Seoul city culture directory](https://news.seoul.go.kr/culture/archives/505240)

### 5) 🪆 **Seoul Folk Flea Market (서울풍물시장)** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET — free entry
- **What:** The city-run successor to the old Hwanghak-dong / Dongdaemun Stadium flea market: two floors of antiques, vinyl, old cameras, militaria, hanbok and secondhand everything, plus **Cheongchun 1-beonga**, a recreated 1960s–70s shopping street, and a cheap food alley.
- **Hours (operator notice):** market **10:00–19:00**; **food alley 10:00–22:00**; Cheongchun 1-beonga 10:00–18:00; **weekend merchants' street market Sat–Sun 10:00–18:00**. **Closed every Tuesday.**
- **⚠️ Two versions of the closure rule are online.** Older city articles say "2nd and 4th Tuesday"; the market's own 2026 notices say **every Tuesday**. Follow the operator.
- **Getting there:** Sinseol-dong Station (Lines 1, 2 branch, Ui–Sinseol) exit 9, ~290 m. One stop from Dongdaemun — easy to bolt onto a DDP evening.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [pungmul.or.kr — monthly operating notice](https://pungmul.or.kr/kwa-1117814-427) (hours table, Tuesday closure) · [notice board](https://pungmul.or.kr/kwa-1117813)

### 6) 🌿 **Seoul Yangnyeongsi Herbal Medicine Museum / Seoul K-Medi Center** — ⏳ HOURS VERIFIED, FEE NOT
- **What:** Museum of Korean herbal medicine at the edge of **Seoul Yangnyeongsi**, the country's largest medicinal-herb market (Jegi-dong), with a foot-bath and herbal-pack experience room and a hanok annexe café.
- **Hours (official):** **Nov–Feb 10:00–17:00** (Mar–Oct 10:00–18:00). **Closed Mondays**, Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok. Your whole trip falls in the winter-hours season — it shuts at 17:00.
- **⏳ Price:** widely listed as **₩1,000** (with paid add-ons for the foot bath / herbal pack) but this pass could not open a live official fee page. Treat ₩1,000 as unverified and carry small change.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [National Museum of Korean Contemporary History — partner museum record](https://www.much.go.kr/cooperation/net/swy.do) (address and seasonal hours) · [Seoul city article on the market](https://opengov.seoul.go.kr/mediahub/4383796)

### 10) 🛢️ **Culture Tank / Oil Tank Culture Park (문화비축기지)** — 🔎 VERIFIED CITY PARK — free

### 11) ⛪ **Jeoldusan Martyrs' Shrine & Korean Martyrs Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE SITE
- **What:** The "Beheading Hill" cliff over the Han River at Hapjeong, where Catholic believers were executed in the 1866 persecution; today a shrine, a riverside sculpture terrace and a museum of Korean Catholic history. Ten minutes from Hongdae by subway, and one of the quietest spots in Mapo.
- **Hours:** **09:30–17:00, closed Mondays** (lunch break signposted on site).
- **Price:** no fixed admission — a **voluntary offering**. Groups of 10+ should book two weeks ahead (02-2126-2299).
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Catholic Bishops' Conference of Korea — Jeoldusan shrine page](https://cbck.or.kr/koreanmartyrs/Shrines/73) (museum hours, Monday closure, group rule) · [CBCK national shrine directory](https://missa.cbck.or.kr/Shrines) · shrine site jeoldusan.or.kr

### 12) 📚 **Gyeongui Line Book Street (경의선책거리)** — ⏳ REAL PLACE, HOURS FROM TOURISM PAGES
- **What:** A 250 m book-themed promenade on the buried Gyeongui rail line directly outside **Hongik Univ. Station exit 6**, run by Mapo-gu with the Association of Writers: themed bookshop pavilions, outdoor seating, author events.
- **Hours/price:** **Tue–Sun 11:00–20:00, closed Mondays**, free. A shortened 11:00–18:00 timetable was used in an earlier period, so confirm on the day.

### 15) 🌳 **Yongsan Children's Garden (용산어린이정원)** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL SITE — free
- **What:** The returned US-garrison land beside the future Yongsan Park: lawns, a fountain garden, tree-lined walks, and preserved American base buildings you can walk among. Despite the name it is not a playground — it reads as a very strange, very large open space in the middle of Seoul.
- **When/hours:** **Tue–Fri and Sun 09:00–18:00; Saturday 09:00–21:00; closed Mondays**, Jan 1, Seollal and Chuseok day.
- **Price and access:** **Free, and since Dec 30, 2025 there is no pre-booking, no ID check and no security screening** — the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport scrapped the reservation system and deleted the entry-restriction clauses. Blogs and guidebooks written before 2026 still tell you to reserve 14 days ahead; ignore them.
- **Getting there:** Line 4 **Sinyongsan** exit 1, 5 min (main gate) or Ichon exit 2 (side gate). **No visitor parking** — take the subway. Free carts circulate from the information centre.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Korea.kr / MOLIT policy briefing, Dec 30, 2025 — full opening, hours, no reservation](https://www.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148957330)

### 16) 🏝️ **Nodeul Island (노들섬)** — ⏳ NOVEMBER PROGRAM NOT POSTED

## 🏙️ Gangnam & Seocho — details

### 17) 🥁 **National Gugak Center — 토요명품 (Saturday Masterpieces)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Nov 7, 14, 21
- **What:** The National Gugak Center's flagship weekly showcase of Korean court and folk music — the most reliable way to see live gugak with a fixed date, at the state institution for it.
- **When/where:** **Saturdays Nov 7, Nov 14 and Nov 21, 2026, 15:00**, in **Umyeondang (우면당)** at the National Gugak Center, Seocho-gu (Line 3 Nambu Terminal + bus, or Line 2 Seocho).
- **Price:** **A seat ₩30,000 / B seat ₩20,000.** Discounts published by the centre include 50% for disabled visitors/people of national merit and seniors 65+, 30% military, 20% groups of 20+. Online booking closes 18:00 the day before; leftover tickets sell at the box office from one hour before.

### 18) 🇬🇧 **Gugak Museum — English Tours** — ✅ CONFIRMED — free, every Saturday
- **What:** A **free English-language guided tour of the Gugak Museum** (Korean instruments, court music history) on the same Seocho campus — explicitly labelled by the centre as a museum programme, not a performance.
- **When:** **Every Saturday at 14:00**, including **Nov 7, 14, 21 and 28**. Duration **about 30–40 minutes**.
- **Price:** **Free. Admission to the Gugak Museum is free.** Sign up via the Google form linked from the programme page. Enquiries 02-580-3130.

### 19) 🐠 **SEA LIFE COEX Aquarium** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR — open 365 days
- **What:** The rebranded COEX aquarium under Starfield COEX Mall — 16 themed zones, ~20,000 animals, a glass-bottom shark boat and a VR room. Gangnam's default rainy-day / late-afternoon indoor option, and it sits under the Starfield Library already listed in [`seoul.md`](seoul.md) #54.
- **Hours:** **Daily 10:00–20:00, last admission 19:00, open 365 days a year.**
- **Prices (official ticket page, Aug 19, 2026):** general ticket **from ₩31,000 list / ₩25,000 online for a child (36 months–12)**; **couple ticket (2 adults) ₩70,000 list / ₩51,000 online**; **family ticket (2 adults + 1 child) ₩101,000 list / ₩74,000 online**; child + VR package ₩39,000 list / ₩29,500; child + jelly-tank package ₩50,000 / ₩34,500; couple + digital photo ₩100,000 / ₩57,000. **Under 36 months free** with a guardian and proof of age. Seniors 65+ get a discount on production of ID; a disabled visitor's companion gets 61% off at the on-site desk.
- **⚠️ Note on the adult single price:** the operator's page advertises the general ticket "from" the child rate and sells adults mostly through the couple/family bundles — no standalone adult figure is printed. Two adults = the ₩70,000 / ₩51,000 couple line above. Do not copy the ₩28,000 figure that old blogs still show.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [SEA LIFE COEX — official ticket page with the full price table](https://www.visitsealife.com/coex-seoul/tickets-passes/) · [opening hours page](https://www.coexaqua.com/plan-your-visit/before-you-visit/opening-hours/)

### 20) 🧘 **Bongeunsa — Thursday Temple Life (English)** — ✅ CONFIRMED WEEKLY PROGRAM — ₩30,000

- **Official source (reviewed Aug 19, 2026):** [Bongeunsa Templestay — Temple Life page](http://temple.bongeunsa.org/public_html/explain/temple_life.asp) (Thursday 14:00–16:00, foreigners, ₩30,000, English, minute-by-minute programme)
