# 🌃 Daegu — Historic Capital of Gyeongsang (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's traditional fashion & medical hub** — the city where the **Daegu Textile Complex (DTC)** is bigger than Seoul's; the home of **Lee & Samsung fashion**; and the southern powerhouse known for **apple foods**, **almond biscuits**, and an **innovative farmer's market culture**. Linked to Seoul and Busan by KTX high-speed rail along your main route.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Seomun Market, **Kim Gwangseok-gil** (Daegu's famous street of art murals), Dongseongno fashion street, and Palgongsan Cable Car.

## 🏯 Heritage & historic

### 1) 🏯 Palgongsan Mountain & Cable Car (팔공산 케이블카) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** 1,192 m granite sacred mountain north of downtown Daegu. **Palgongsan Cable Car** carries visitors up to **Bonghwangdae Observation** and **Gatbawi Buddha** (a Helen-of-Troy-shaped Buddha carved into natural rock). UNESCO-listed Buddhist National Heritage. Surrounded by 13 Buddhist temples including Pagyesa Temple.
- **Hours:** Cable Car daily **10:00–17:00** (Nov winter, ends earlier; closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult one-way cable car **₩9,000** (~$6.50); round-trip ₩12,000
- **Official sources:** [palgongsan.or.kr](http://www.palgongsan.or.kr) · [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The cable car does the hillside trail too. **Gatbawi Buddha** is Daegu's symbol.

### 2) 🕍 Pagyesa Temple — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Front-running temple at the foot of Palgongsan with 1,500-year-old *bi* statues. Famous for autumn foilage
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 15 min taxi from Palgongsan base station.

### 3) ⛩️ Donghwasa Temple — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Major Buddhist temple at Palgongsan base — features the **23-meter standing stone Buddha** (Modern Buddhist Sculpture) — Divine Dharma Hall, thirty-five cedar temple structures and winter camellias in early November
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [donghwasa.or.kr](http://www.donghwasa.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Easy taxi ride from downtown Daegu.

### 4) 🏛️ Kaac (Korea Advanced Hologram Center) — 👀 WATCH (Nov openings)
- **What:** Modern culture venue in Dongseongno area
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00
- **Price:** Free
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](http://www.daegu.go.kr)

## 🎨 Modern Daegu — fashion, art, cafes

### 5) 🌆 Dongseongno Fashion Street (동성로) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Korea's 5th-largest shopping street — 30+ block pedestrian street in downtown Daegu. Lined with 100+ K-fashion shops, K-beauty stores, Daiso outlets, cat cafes, and late-night street food
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–22:00** (closed Mondays for some shops)
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Excellent nightlife after sundown — street food trucks and live outdoor entertainment.

### 6) 🛍️ Daegu Department Store (대백·eyebrow-1) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Modern department store complex including Hyundai, Lotte
- **Hours:** Daily 10:30–20:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [lotteshopping.com](https://www.lotteshopping.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Pair with Dongseongno.

### 7) 🎨 Kim Gwangseok-gil (김광석길) Memorial Street — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Daegu's most iconic street art district in Dongseongno — named after Kim Gwangseok (1977–1996), a beloved singer-songwriter. Mural walls honor the city's old town and indie musicians
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** As light turns golden, the murals glow — complete the loop in 30 min.

### 8) 🍣 Daegu Yangnyeongsi (대가 약령시, Oriental Medicine Market) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** 350-year-old herb & traditional-medicine market — Korea's largest. Selling ginseng, red dates, herbal medicaments, and medicinal food. Adjacent street has *yakseon* tea shops
- **Hours:** Stalls daily **09:00–20:00** (closed 1st & 3rd Sunday)
- **Price:** Free walk-in; ginseng ~₩5,000–50,000
- **Official source:** [yangnyeongsi.or.kr](http://www.yangnyeongsi.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Tea shop in-junction.

### 9) 🎭 Daegu Modern History Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Modern history exhibition hall documenting 1900s onward — colonial & post-liberation
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [museum.daegu.go.kr](http://museum.daegu.go.kr)

## 🍴 Markets of Daegu

### 10) 🛍️ Seomun Market (서문시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu's premier traditional market since the Joseon era (1900+ years old). 4,000+ stalls covering textiles, fashion, street food, kitchenware, hardware, and more — famous for *nokdu jeon* (mung-bean sprout pancake), *kkochi* skewers, and "Chimak" chicken
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (food stalls longer; closed 2nd & 4th Sunday)
- **Price:** Free walk-in; dishes ~₩3,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [seomunmarket.co.kr](http://www.seomunmarket.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Late-night food streets** until 23:00+.

### 11) 🛒 Bangcheon Market (방천시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Smaller market next to Seomun, known for fruits and vegetables from the apple and persimmon country
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–20:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in

### 12) 🏮 Palgong Market (팔공시장) — 🔁 Tue/Thu morning
- **What:** Oldest Saturday/3-day regional market in downtown Daegu
- **Hours:** Tue/Thu only ~09:00–17:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Beginner notes:** Authentic — go early morning to see farmers.

### 13) 🛒 Daegu Agricultural Wholesale Market (대구 농수산물도매시장) — 🔁 05:00–10:00
- **What:** Caustic auction hall for produce — breakfast tours in South Korea
- **Hours:** Daily 05:00–10:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [at.or.kr](http://www.at.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Extreme authentic local. Not for tourists or sensitive stomaches — but a serious cultural stop.

## 🆕 More verified Daegu events & activities (expansion set)

*Added from official city tourism portals, national heritage/museum sites, and each venue's own official page. Status legend: ✅ confirmed · ⏳ TBA · 👀 watch · 🔁 always on.*

### 140) 🧺 Seomun Market & Night Market (서문시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu's 400-year-old great market, one of the three largest in Joseon Korea — 4,000+ stalls over six districts, with a famous **night market from 19:30**
- **Hours:** Day market **09:00–19:00** (closed 1st & 3rd Sunday); night market **19:30–24:00**
- **Price:** **FREE** to browse; street food ₩2,000–₩6,000
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 3 Seomun Market Station. **The night market is the single best evening in Daegu** — go hungry, and try the *napjak mandu* flat dumplings.

### 141) 🥟 Daegu Napjak-mandu & Ten Signature Foods (대구 십미) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu officially designates **ten signature dishes** — flat dumplings, *makchang* grilled intestine, *ttaro gukbap*, muntier noodles, spicy braised catfish and more
- **Hours:** Restaurants roughly **11:00–23:00**
- **Price:** Most dishes **₩7,000–₩15,000**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The city tourism site lists designated restaurants for each dish. **Makchang with soju is the definitive Daegu night out.**

### 142) 🌃 Kim Kwang-seok Memorial Street (김광석 다시그리기길) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 350 m alley beside Bangcheon Market covered in murals to **Kim Kwang-seok (1964–96)**, Korea's most beloved folk singer-songwriter, who grew up here
- **Hours:** Always open; cafes roughly **11:00–22:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 2 Kyungpook Univ. Hospital Station Exit 3. His songs play from speakers along the whole street — genuinely moving even if you don't know him.

### 143) 🏛️ Daegu National Museum (국립대구박물관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** The regional national museum covering the Yeongnam area — Gaya and Silla archaeology, Confucian scholarly culture and Daegu's textile industry
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (Sat to 21:00; closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.museum.go.kr](https://daegu.museum.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 2 Manchon Station + short bus. Free, and the textile gallery explains why Daegu was Korea's fashion capital.

### 144) 🎨 Daegu Art Museum (대구미술관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The city's major contemporary art museum next to the stadium — strong holdings of Korean modern art and a well-known **Lee Inseong** collection
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–19:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩1,000**; special exhibitions extra
- **Official source:** [daeguartmuseum.or.kr](https://artmuseum.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 2 Daegongwon Station + bus. ₩1,000 for a full-scale art museum.

### 145) 🚶 Daegu Modern Culture Alley — 5 free walking courses (근대골목) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Daegu's flagship heritage walk — **five numbered courses** through missionary houses, the 1919 March 1st Movement stairs, Gyesan Cathedral and Jingolmok alley
- **Hours:** Always open; free guided walks by reservation
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Course 2 is the classic (about 2 hours). **Free English guided walks can be booked through the Jung-gu office** — highly recommended.

### 146) ⛪ Gyesan Catholic Cathedral (계산성당) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Daegu's 1902 Gothic red-brick cathedral, the oldest Western building in the city and the third oldest church in Korea
- **Hours:** Daily roughly **09:00–17:00** outside services
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr) · [Catholic Archdiocese of Daegu](https://www.daegu-archdiocese.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** On Modern Alley Course 2. The painter Lee Inseong sketched here as a boy — his tree is still in the churchyard.

### 147) 🪜 3·1 Movement Road & Cheongna Hill Missionary Houses — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The 90 stone steps students climbed in secret to join the **March 1st Movement in 1919**, leading up to three 1910 American missionary houses, now small museums
- **Hours:** Steps always open; house museums Tue–Sun **09:30–17:30**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr) · [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)

### 148) 🌿 Yangnyeongsi Oriental Medicine Museum & Herb Market (약령시) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Price:** Museum **FREE**; foot bath and tea free
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 1 Banwoldang Station Exit 18. **Free herbal foot bath and free medicinal tea** in the museum — a great cold-weather stop.

### 149) 🏛️ Gyeongsang-gamyeong Park & Provincial Office Site (경상감영공원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)

### 150) 🕊️ Gukchaebosang Memorial Park (국채보상운동기념공원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A park commemorating the **1907 National Debt Repayment Movement**, when Daegu citizens gave up tobacco to pay off Korea's debt to Japan — a UNESCO Memory of the World record
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [UNESCO Memory of the World](https://www.unesco.org)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 1 Jungangno Station. The movement's archives were UNESCO-inscribed in 2017 — it started in this city.

### 151) 🛍️ Dongseongno Shopping Street (동성로) — 🔁 daily (FREE)

- **Price:** **FREE** to walk
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)

### 156) 🎖️ Nakdong River Victory Memorial Hall (낙동강승전기념관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)

### 157) 🛕 Donghwasa Temple, Palgongsan (동화사) — 🔁 daily

- **What:** Staffed information centres at Dongdaegu Station, the airport and Dongseongno, with free maps, English help and discount coupon booklets
- **Hours:** Roughly **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pick up the Modern Alley map and the Daegu Ten Foods guide — both free and genuinely useful.

### 215) ♿ Daegu Barrier-Free Access & Senior Discounts — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu's metro is fully lift-accessible, and most municipal museums and parks are free or discounted for seniors and visitors with disabilities
- **Hours:** Facility hours
- **Price:** Many **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [dtro.or.kr](https://www.dtro.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Line 3 monorail stations all have lifts. The city publishes a barrier-free tourism map.

### 216) 🌙 Daegu Night Views — 83 Tower, Apsan, Suseong Lake — 🔁 nightly
- **What:** Daegu's three classic night viewpoints, all reachable in an evening: 83 Tower, the Apsan cable-car observatory and Suseong Lake's lit promenade
- **Hours:** Roughly **17:30–22:00** in November
- **Price:** Suseong Lake **FREE**; towers ₩10,000–₩12,000
- **Official source:** [eworld.kr](https://www.eworld.kr) · [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Sunset is around 17:25 in November** — an early blue hour means you can do two of the three in one evening.

### 217) 🥘 Daegu Muntier Noodles & Jjiga-galbi Specialities — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Two more of Daegu's ten designated dishes — cold *nurungji-guksu* wheat noodles, and **Dongin-dong jjigalbi**, braised short ribs served in a brass bowl
- **Hours:** Most **11:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Jjigalbi **₩20,000–₩30,000** per person
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The Dongin-dong jjigalbi street near Gukchaebosang Park has a dozen specialists side by side.

### 218) 🎓 Kyungpook National University Campus & Autumn Ginkgo — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** One of Korea's major national universities, with an open campus, a free museum and a **ginkgo avenue that is a local November landmark**
- **Hours:** Campus always open; museum weekdays **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [knu.ac.kr](https://www.knu.ac.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 1 Sincheon Station + bus. Cheap student cafeterias are open to visitors.

### 219) 🎡 Daegu Suseong Land & Family Amusement Options — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The small lakeside amusement park at Suseong, plus the family attractions at Sparkland — modest, cheap and convenient
- **Hours:** Roughly **10:00–20:00**
- **Price:** Rides **₩5,000–₩8,000** each
- **Official source:** [Suseong-gu](https://www.suseong.kr) · [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 3 Suseongmot Station. Combine with the lake walk and the fountain show.

## 🧭 Beginner-friendly essentials

- **KTX route:** Seoul → Dongdaegu (50 min via Gyeongbu Line KTX) → continue to Busan (45 min). **Dongdaegu Station** is the city-center KTX hub. Local Line 1 red subway to Dongseongno
- **For a Seoul–Busan run-day with Daegu detour:** Seoul 07:30 KTX → Dongdaegu 09:20 → E-World 83 Tower or Palgongsan morning → Seomun lunch → Dongseongno coffee → KTX to Busan 17:30
- **Currency/payment:** Cards widely accepted in modern venues; cash by the cooler parts
