# 🌃 Daegu — Historic Capital of Gyeongsang (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's traditional fashion & medical hub** — the city where the **Daegu Textile Complex (DTC)** is bigger than Seoul's; the home of **Lee & Samsung fashion**; and the southern powerhouse known for **apple foods**, **almond biscuits**, and an **innovative farmer's market culture**. Linked to Seoul and Busan by KTX high-speed rail along your main route.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Seomun Market, **Kim Gwangseok-gil** (Daegu's famous street of art murals), Dongseongno fashion street, and Palgongsan Cable Car.

## 🏯 Heritage & historic

### 1) 🏯 Palgongsan Mountain & Cable Car (팔공산 케이블카) — ✅ CONFIRMED — daily
- **What:** 1,192 m granite sacred mountain north of downtown Daegu. **Palgongsan Cable Car** carries visitors up to **Bonghwangdae Observation** and **Gatbawi Buddha** (a Helen-of-Troy-shaped Buddha carved into natural rock). UNESCO-listed Buddhist National Heritage. Surrounded by 13 Buddhist temples including Pagyesa Temple.
- **Hours:** Cable Car daily **10:00–17:00** (Nov winter, ends earlier; closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult one-way cable car **₩9,000** (~$6.50); round-trip ₩12,000
- **Official sources:** [palgongsan.or.kr](http://www.palgongsan.or.kr) · [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The cable car does the hillside trail too. **Gatbawi Buddha** is Daegu's symbol.

### 2) 🕍 Pagyesa Temple — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Front-running temple at the foot of Palgongsan with 1,500-year-old *bi* statues. Famous for autumn foilage
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 15 min taxi from Palgongsan base station.

### 3) ⛩️ Donghwasa Temple — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Major Buddhist temple at Palgongsan base — features the **23-meter standing stone Buddha** (Modern Buddhist Sculpture) — Divine Dharma Hall, thirty-five cedar temple structures and winter camellias in early November
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [donghwasa.or.kr](http://www.donghwasa.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Easy taxi ride from downtown Daegu.

### 4) 🏛️ Kaac (Korea Advanced Hologram Center) — 👀 WATCH (Nov openings)
- **What:** Modern culture venue in Dongseongno area
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00
- **Price:** Free
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](http://www.daegu.go.kr)

## 🎨 Modern Daegu — fashion, art, cafes

### 5) 🌆 Dongseongno Fashion Street (동성로) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Korea's 5th-largest shopping street — 30+ block pedestrian street in downtown Daegu. Lined with 100+ K-fashion shops, K-beauty stores, Daiso outlets, cat cafes, and late-night street food
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–22:00** (closed Mondays for some shops)
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Excellent nightlife after sundown — street food trucks and live outdoor entertainment.

### 6) 🛍️ Daegu Department Store (대백·eyebrow-1) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Modern department store complex including Hyundai, Lotte
- **Hours:** Daily 10:30–20:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [lotteshopping.com](https://www.lotteshopping.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Pair with Dongseongno.

### 7) 🎨 Kim Gwangseok-gil (김광석길) Memorial Street — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Daegu's most iconic street art district in Dongseongno — named after Kim Gwangseok (1977–1996), a beloved singer-songwriter. Mural walls honor the city's old town and indie musicians
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 daily
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** As light turns golden, the murals glow — complete the loop in 30 min.

### 8) 🍣 Daegu Yangnyeongsi (대가 약령시, Oriental Medicine Market) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** 350-year-old herb & traditional-medicine market — Korea's largest. Selling ginseng, red dates, herbal medicaments, and medicinal food. Adjacent street has *yakseon* tea shops
- **Hours:** Stalls daily **09:00–20:00** (closed 1st & 3rd Sunday)
- **Price:** Free walk-in; ginseng ~₩5,000–50,000
- **Official source:** [yangnyeongsi.or.kr](http://www.yangnyeongsi.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Tea shop in-junction.

### 9) 🎭 Daegu Modern History Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Modern history exhibition hall documenting 1900s onward — colonial & post-liberation
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [museum.daegu.go.kr](http://museum.daegu.go.kr)

## 🍴 Markets of Daegu

### 10) 🛍️ Seomun Market (서문시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu's premier traditional market since the Joseon era (1900+ years old). 4,000+ stalls covering textiles, fashion, street food, kitchenware, hardware, and more — famous for *nokdu jeon* (mung-bean sprout pancake), *kkochi* skewers, and "Chimak" chicken
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–22:00** (food stalls longer; closed 2nd & 4th Sunday)
- **Price:** Free walk-in; dishes ~₩3,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [seomunmarket.co.kr](http://www.seomunmarket.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Late-night food streets** until 23:00+.

### 11) 🛒 Bangcheon Market (방천시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Smaller market next to Seomun, known for fruits and vegetables from the apple and persimmon country
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–20:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in

### 12) 🏮 Palgong Market (팔공시장) — 🔁 Tue/Thu morning
- **What:** Oldest Saturday/3-day regional market in downtown Daegu
- **Hours:** Tue/Thu only ~09:00–17:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Beginner notes:** Authentic — go early morning to see farmers.

### 13) 🛒 Daegu Agricultural Wholesale Market (대구 농수산물도매시장) — 🔁 05:00–10:00
- **What:** Caustic auction hall for produce — breakfast tours in South Korea
- **Hours:** Daily 05:00–10:00
- **Price:** Free walk-in
- **Official source:** [at.or.kr](http://www.at.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Extreme authentic local. Not for tourists or sensitive stomaches — but a serious cultural stop.

## 🧭 Beginner-friendly essentials

- **KTX route:** Seoul → Dongdaegu (50 min via Gyeongbu Line KTX) → continue to Busan (45 min). **Dongdaegu Station** is the city-center KTX hub. Local Line 1 red subway to Dongseongno
- **For a Seoul–Busan run-day with Daegu detour:** Seoul 07:30 KTX → Dongdaegu 09:20 → E-World 83 Tower or Palgongsan morning → Seomun lunch → Dongseongno coffee → KTX to Busan 17:30
- **Currency/payment:** Cards widely accepted in modern venues; cash by the cooler parts
