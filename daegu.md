# 🌃 Daegu — Historic Capital of Gyeongsang (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's traditional fashion & medical hub** — the city where the **Daegu Textile Complex (DTC)** is bigger than Seoul's; the home of **Lee & Samsung fashion**; and the southern powerhouse known for **apple foods**, **almond biscuits**, and an **innovative farmer's market culture**. Linked to Seoul and Busan by KTX high-speed rail along your main route.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Seomun Market, **Kim Gwangseok-gil** (Daegu's famous street of art murals), Dongseongno fashion street, and Palgongsan Cable Car.

## 🏯 Heritage & historic

### 1) 🕍 Pagyesa Temple — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Front-running temple at the foot of Palgongsan with 1,500-year-old *bi* statues. Famous for autumn foilage
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** 15 min taxi from Palgongsan base station.

### 2) ⛩️ Donghwasa Temple — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Major Buddhist temple at Palgongsan base — features the **23-meter standing stone Buddha** (Modern Buddhist Sculpture) — Divine Dharma Hall, thirty-five cedar temple structures and winter camellias in early November
- **Hours:** Daily dawn–dusk
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [donghwasa.or.kr](http://www.donghwasa.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Easy taxi ride from downtown Daegu.

### 3) 🏛️ Kaac (Korea Advanced Hologram Center) — 👀 WATCH (Nov openings)
- **What:** Modern culture venue in Dongseongno area
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00
- **Price:** Free
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](http://www.daegu.go.kr)

### 5) 🎸 Kim Kwang-seok Story House (김광석스토리하우스) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The small dedicated museum at the south end of Kim Kwang-seok Street, opened June 2017 — handwritten scores, notebooks, unreleased photographs, and listening stations for the singer's recordings
- **Hours:** **Nov–Mar 10:00–18:00**; Apr–Oct 10:00–19:00. **Closed Mondays** and on Seollal/Chuseok day

- **Beginner notes:** Note the **shorter 18:00 winter closing** — you will be here in November. The street itself outside is free and open 24 h; this is the paid indoor part, and it is worth the ₩2,000 only if the music means something to you. Address 대구 중구 동덕로8길 14-3.

### 6) 🍣 Daegu Yangnyeongsi (대가 약령시, Oriental Medicine Market) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** 350-year-old herb & traditional-medicine market — Korea's largest. Selling ginseng, red dates, herbal medicaments, and medicinal food. Adjacent street has *yakseon* tea shops
- **Hours:** Stalls daily **09:00–20:00** (closed 1st & 3rd Sunday)
- **Price:** Free walk-in; ginseng ~₩5,000–50,000
- **Official source:** [yangnyeongsi.or.kr](http://www.yangnyeongsi.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Tea shop in-junction.

### 7) 🎭 Daegu Modern History Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)

- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**

## 🍴 Local food

### 19) 🍜 Daegu *Nokdujeon* (Green Mung-bean Pancake) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu's signature street-food — savory pancake made from mungbean sprouts, leek, beef and kimchi
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–21:00
- **Price:** ~₩5,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Found at every market; if dining alone, go for ½ portion.

### 20) 🥩 Daegu Spicy Beef Stew (갈비찜/대구찜갈비) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu-style braised pork ribs marinated with spice paste, served with noodles and vegetables

- **Official source:** [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pair with steamed pancake at a traditional banquet house in the Jung-gu *Jangshigol* alley.

### 21) 🍲 *Cowałim 찰 흙 비지전* (Daegu-style steamed *Biji* pancake) — 🔁 daily

- **Beginner notes:** **A serious full-day hike.** Check National Park Service closure notices; some sections shut in winter fire season from mid-November.

### 105) 🏛️ Daegu Bangjja & Otgol Village Traditional Houses (옻골마을) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 17th-century Gyeongju Choi clan village in eastern Daegu, with a 350-year-old head house and a protective zelkova grove — a complete Joseon village inside a modern city
- **Hours:** Daylight hours
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [Dong-gu Daegu](https://www.dong.daegu.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Ayanggyo. Twenty minutes from downtown and almost unknown to visitors.

### 106) 🎋 Daegu Hyanggyo & Confucian Ceremonies (대구향교) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Daegu's Confucian school, founded 1398 — with public *seokjeon* rites, tea ceremony programmes and free hanbok-wearing sessions
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 1 Banwoldang Station. Free traditional-culture programmes run most weekends — check the district site.

### 107) 📿 Yeongnam Jeilgwan & Dalseong Park (달성공원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** **Daegu's oldest park**, on a 1,500-year-old earthen fortress mound, with a small zoo, the restored Yeongnam Jeilgwan gate and old trees
- **Hours:** Daily **05:00–21:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 3 Dalseong Park Station. Free entry including the zoo — old-fashioned, but a genuine piece of local life.

### 108) 🚉 Chilseong Night Market & Daegu Wholesale Markets — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Chilseong Market, Daegu's big wholesale complex near the station, with a flower market, a dawn produce auction and a night food section
- **Hours:** Wholesale from **03:00**; retail **08:00–20:00**
- **Price:** **FREE** to browse
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 1 Chilseong Market Station. The 04:00 flower market is a genuine spectacle if you're up.

### 109) 🌸 Daegu Chrysanthemum & Autumn Flower Exhibitions — 👀 WATCH (late Oct–early Nov)
- **What:** Daegu's arboretum, Duryu Park and district offices mount large chrysanthemum displays each autumn, typically running into the first week of November
- **Hours:** Daylight hours once open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The Daegu Arboretum display is the largest. Dates are published on the city site in October.

### 110) 🎤 Daegu Live Music, Busking & Rock Festival Scene — 🔁 weekends (FREE)
- **What:** Daegu has one of Korea's strongest live-music cultures — free busking on Dongseongno, small live clubs in Bongsan-dong, and the city-run busking programme
- **Hours:** Weekend evenings roughly **18:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Busking **FREE**; club entry ₩10,000–₩20,000
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Kim Kwang-seok came out of this scene. Dongseongno on a Saturday evening is free and consistently good.

### 111) 🎨 Bongsan Culture Street Galleries (봉산문화거리) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Over 30 commercial art galleries along one downtown street, nearly all free to enter — Korea's densest gallery cluster outside Seoul
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun roughly **10:30–18:30** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Jung-gu Daegu](https://www.jung.daegu.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 2 Banwoldang Station. Just walk in — commercial galleries in Korea expect browsers.

### 112) 🧵 Daegu Textile Complex & Fashion Industry Heritage — 🔁 by event
- **What:** Daegu was Korea's textile capital, and the industry's museums, the Daegu Textile Complex and the annual Daegu Fashion Fair still reflect it
- **Hours:** By event; DTC museum Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Mostly **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [exco.co.kr](https://www.exco.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The Daegu National Museum's textile gallery is the best free introduction to this history.

### 113) 🏞️ Geumho River Cycle Path & Nakdong Confluence — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The riverside cycle route along the Geumho to its confluence with the Nakdong — part of Korea's national bike-certification network
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**; bike hire from ₩10,000/day
- **Official source:** [riverguide.go.kr](https://www.riverguide.go.kr) · [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Certification stamp booths along the route. Flat, separated and good in cool November weather.

### 114) 🌾 Sagunjin & Hwawon Recreation Area Nakdong Cliffs — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The riverside cliffs and pavilion at Hwawon overlooking the Nakdong — a Joseon-era scenic spot with a modern observation deck and cafe
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [Dalseong-gun](https://www.dalseong.daegu.kr) · [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus from Daegok Station. **Sunset over the Nakdong from the deck is the best free view in western Daegu.**

### 115) 🕌 Daegu Islamic Center & Multicultural Districts — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Daegu's mosque and the multicultural food streets around Kyungpook National University serving Central Asian, Uzbek and Pakistani food
- **Hours:** Mosque prayer times; restaurants **11:00–22:00**
- **Price:** **FREE** to visit
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Near Kyungpook National University. Halal options in Daegu are concentrated here.

### 116) 🏥 Daegu Medical Tourism & Oriental Medicine Experience — 🔁 weekdays
- **What:** Daegu markets itself as a medical tourism city; the Yangnyeongsi district offers short traditional-medicine consultations and herbal experience programmes
- **Hours:** Weekdays roughly **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Experience programmes from **₩10,000**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [Korea Health Industry Development Institute](https://www.khidi.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Book through the Yangnyeongsi museum. Making your own herbal sachet is a good hour.

### 117) 🛌 Daegu Hanok Stay & Guesthouse Districts — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Restored hanok guesthouses in the Modern Alley and Otgol areas, plus a large cheap guesthouse cluster near Dongdaegu Station
- **Hours:** Check-in typically **15:00**
- **Price:** From **₩40,000** per night
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu is markedly cheaper than Seoul or Busan for accommodation.

### 118) 🛍️ Daegu Department Stores — Shinsegae & Hyundai — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Shinsegae Daegu at Dongdaegu Station includes an aquarium, a rooftop theme park and a cinema — one of Korea's largest department stores
- **Hours:** Daily **10:30–20:00** (food halls later)
- **Price:** **FREE** entry
- **Official source:** [shinsegae.com](https://www.shinsegae.com) · [ehyundai.com](https://www.ehyundai.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Directly attached to Dongdaegu KTX Station — a good place to spend a couple of hours before a train.

### 119) 🧊 Daegu Winter Illumination & Seasonal Ice Rinks — 👀 WATCH (from mid-Nov)
- **What:** E-World, Suseong Lake and Dongseongno typically switch on winter lighting **from mid-to-late November**, with outdoor rinks opening around the same time
- **Hours:** Lighting roughly **17:30–23:00** once lit
- **Price:** Street lighting **FREE**; rinks ₩1,000–₩5,000
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [eworld.kr](https://www.eworld.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **E-World's illumination runs year-round**, so it's the reliable option regardless of switch-on dates.

### 120) ✨ E-World Starlight Festival (이월드 별빛축제) — 👀 WATCH — mid-Nov to late Feb
- **What:** E-World's flagship winter illumination — millions of LEDs across themed "starlight" flower gardens (rose, hydrangea and lily gardens), plus the 83 Tower lit above it. Each season opens with a **tree-lighting ceremony and a fireworks show** on the first evening
- **Dates:** **2026–27 dates TBA — but the pattern is firm.** The festival has opened in **mid-November** every year and runs to late February (2025–26 ran **Nov 15 – Feb 28**, lighting ceremony 19:50, fireworks 20:00). **An opening in the second half of November very likely falls inside your trip**
- **Hours:** Night-ticket entry from **17:00**; park closes 21:00 Mon–Fri, 22:00 Sat–Sun
- **Price:** **Night pass around ₩22,900–₩31,000** at the gate depending on season; day pass ~₩30,900–₩49,000. **Buy online** — Naver, Coupang, Myrealtrip and telecom-membership discounts routinely cut 20–40% off the gate price
- **Official source:** [eworld.kr](https://www.eworld.kr) · [tour.daegu.go.kr](https://tour.daegu.go.kr) · ☎ 053-620-0001
- **Beginner notes:** **Check eworld.kr in late October for the 2026 opening date** — if it lands before you leave Daegu, this is the single best night out in the city. Subway Line 2 to Duryu Station Exit 15, then 15 min on foot. It gets genuinely cold after dark: gloves and a heavy coat. Parking ₩3,000 for pass holders.

### 121) 🎿 Daegu-area Ski & Winter Sports Access — 👀 WATCH — opens late Nov/Dec
- **What:** The nearest ski resorts to Daegu (Eden Valley near Yangsan, and the Gyeongbuk resorts) typically open **from late November or December**
- **Hours:** Season-dependent
- **Price:** Lift passes from **₩50,000**
- **Official source:** [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr) · [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Probably just outside your Nov 22 window** — check resort sites in early November if you're hoping for snow.

### 122) 🚌 Daegu Intercity Bus & Regional Day Trips — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu is within an hour of Gyeongju, Andong, Pohang and Gimcheon by bus or train — the best base for the northern Yeongnam region
- **Hours:** Roughly **06:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Gyeongju **₩6,500**; Andong ₩9,000
- **Official source:** [kobus.co.kr](https://www.kobus.co.kr) · [txbus.t-money.co.kr](https://txbus.t-money.co.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Dongdaegu and Seobu terminals. **Andong (Hahoe Village, UNESCO) is 1 h 30 and makes an excellent day trip.**

### 123) 🏘️ Andong Hahoe Folk Village day trip — UNESCO (하회마을) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The most famous **UNESCO-listed** Korean clan village, on a river bend north of Daegu — with the Hahoe mask dance performed on weekends
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00); mask dance typically **14:00** weekends
- **Price:** Adult **₩5,000**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1324) · [andong.go.kr](https://www.andong.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus Daegu → Andong (1 h 30), then local bus 246. **Check the mask dance schedule** — it's the highlight and it isn't daily in November.

### 124) 📚 Byeongsan Seowon, Andong — UNESCO day trip (병산서원) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Widely considered **the most beautiful of the nine UNESCO Korean seowon** — a 1613 academy whose Mandaeru pavilion frames the cliff and river opposite
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00** (Nov: to 17:00)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1498) · [andong.go.kr](https://www.andong.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Near Hahoe Village — combine the two. The view from the Mandaeru floor is the single best piece of Confucian architecture in Korea.

### 125) 🍗 Andong Jjimdak & Regional Food day trip — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Andong's braised soy-sauce chicken, invented in its old market in the 1980s and now sold nationwide — plus salted mackerel and Andong soju
- **Hours:** Most **11:00–21:00**
- **Price:** Jjimdak for 2–3 people **₩35,000–₩45,000**
- **Official source:** [andong.go.kr](https://www.andong.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Andong Jjimdak Alley in the old market. Combine with Hahoe Village on the same day trip from Daegu.

### 126) 🏛️ Gimcheon, Gumi & Nakdong Valley day trips — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The Nakdong valley towns north of Daegu — Gimcheon's Jikjisa temple and Gumi's Geumosan provincial park, both under an hour away
- **Hours:** Temples roughly **08:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Jikjisa **₩3,000**
- **Official source:** [jikjisa.or.kr](https://www.jikjisa.or.kr) · [gimcheon.go.kr](https://www.gimcheon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Train or bus from Daegu. Jikjisa's approach avenue is a well-known autumn foliage walk.

### 127) 🧭 Daegu Tourist Information & Discount Passes — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Staffed information centres at Dongdaegu Station, the airport and Dongseongno, with free maps, English help and discount coupon booklets
- **Hours:** Roughly **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pick up the Modern Alley map and the Daegu Ten Foods guide — both free and genuinely useful.

### 128) ♿ Daegu Barrier-Free Access & Senior Discounts — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Daegu's metro is fully lift-accessible, and most municipal museums and parks are free or discounted for seniors and visitors with disabilities
- **Hours:** Facility hours
- **Price:** Many **FREE**
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr) · [dtro.or.kr](https://www.dtro.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Line 3 monorail stations all have lifts. The city publishes a barrier-free tourism map.

### 129) 🌙 Daegu Night Views — 83 Tower, Apsan, Suseong Lake — 🔁 nightly
- **What:** Daegu's three classic night viewpoints, all reachable in an evening: 83 Tower, the Apsan cable-car observatory and Suseong Lake's lit promenade
- **Hours:** Roughly **17:30–22:00** in November
- **Price:** Suseong Lake **FREE**; towers ₩10,000–₩12,000
- **Official source:** [eworld.kr](https://www.eworld.kr) · [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Sunset is around 17:25 in November** — an early blue hour means you can do two of the three in one evening.

### 130) 🥘 Daegu Muntier Noodles & Jjiga-galbi Specialities — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Two more of Daegu's ten designated dishes — cold *nurungji-guksu* wheat noodles, and **Dongin-dong jjigalbi**, braised short ribs served in a brass bowl
- **Hours:** Most **11:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Jjigalbi **₩20,000–₩30,000** per person
- **Official source:** [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The Dongin-dong jjigalbi street near Gukchaebosang Park has a dozen specialists side by side.

### 131) 🎓 Kyungpook National University Campus & Autumn Ginkgo — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** One of Korea's major national universities, with an open campus, a free museum and a **ginkgo avenue that is a local November landmark**
- **Hours:** Campus always open; museum weekdays **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [knu.ac.kr](https://www.knu.ac.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 1 Sincheon Station + bus. Cheap student cafeterias are open to visitors.

### 132) 🎡 Daegu Suseong Land & Family Amusement Options — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The small lakeside amusement park at Suseong, plus the family attractions at Sparkland — modest, cheap and convenient
- **Hours:** Roughly **10:00–20:00**
- **Price:** Rides **₩5,000–₩8,000** each
- **Official source:** [Suseong-gu](https://www.suseong.kr) · [daegu.go.kr](https://www.daegu.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Daegu Line 3 Suseongmot Station. Combine with the lake walk and the fountain show.

## 🧭 Beginner-friendly essentials

- **KTX route:** Seoul → Dongdaegu (50 min via Gyeongbu Line KTX) → continue to Busan (45 min). **Dongdaegu Station** is the city-center KTX hub. Local Line 1 red subway to Dongseongno
- **For a Seoul–Busan run-day with Daegu detour:** Seoul 07:30 KTX → Dongdaegu 09:20 → E-World 83 Tower or Palgongsan morning → Seomun lunch → Dongseongno coffee → KTX to Busan 17:30
- **Currency/payment:** Cards widely accepted in modern venues; cash by the cooler parts
