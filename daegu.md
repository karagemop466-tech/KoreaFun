## Central Daegu and modern history

### 6) 🚶 **Daegu Modern Culture Alley** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Official walking route linking Cheongna Hill, missionary houses, March 1st Movement Road, Gyesan Cathedral, and old commercial streets.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor route is free; building interiors have separate schedules.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Tourism](https://tour.daegu.go.kr/)

### 7) ⛪ **Gyesan Cathedral and Cheongna Hill** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE AREA
- **What:** Historic Catholic cathedral and missionary-house area central to Daegu’s modern-history route.
- **Access:** Active religious site; sightseeing is restricted during services.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Tourism](https://tour.daegu.go.kr/)

### 8) 🏛️ **Daegu Modern History Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Museum in a former bank building covering the city’s modern transformation.
- **Hours/price:** Verify current hours and Monday closure; general admission is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Modern History Museum](https://daegu.go.kr/dmhm/)

### 9) 🌿 **Gyeongsang-gamyeong Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Park preserving buildings and the site of the former provincial government headquarters.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor park is free; building access has posted hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Tourism](https://tour.daegu.go.kr/)

### 10) 💰 **National Debt Redemption Movement Memorial Park and Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Park and museum commemorating the civic movement that began in Daegu in 1907.
- **Hours/price:** Park is free; museum hours should be checked separately.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [National Debt Redemption Movement Memorial](https://www.gukchae.com/)

### 11) 🎸 **Kim Kwang-seok Road and Bangcheon Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Mural/music street beside Bangcheon Market commemorating singer Kim Kwang-seok, born in 1964.
- **Hours/price:** Public street is free; businesses set their own hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Tourism](https://tour.daegu.go.kr/)

### 12) 🌿 **Yangnyeongsi Herb Medicine Museum and market streets** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Museum and historic medicinal-herb commercial district.
- **Hours/price:** Museum and shops have separate schedules; do not confuse the permanent district with the annual festival.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Yangnyeongsi Museum](https://www.daegu.go.kr/dgom/)

### 13) 🛍️ **Dongseong-ro** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Central pedestrian shopping and entertainment district.
- **Hours/price:** Public streets are free; no generic shop, bar, or light show is treated as a separate attraction.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Tourism](https://tour.daegu.go.kr/)

### 14) 👟 **Hyangchon Cultural Center and Daegu Literature Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Cultural-history complex interpreting postwar arts, literature, music, and downtown life.
- **Hours/price:** Check venue visitor information.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Hyangchon Cultural Center](https://www.hyangchon.or.kr/)

## Parks and viewpoints

### 19) ⛰️ **Apsan Park and cable car** — 🔎 VERIFIED ACTIVITY
- **What:** Mountain park with trails, observatories, memorial sites, and a privately operated cable car.
- **Hours/price:** Trail access is free; cable-car hours vary by weekday, season, weather, and maintenance.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Apsan Cable Car](http://www.apsan-cablecar.co.kr/)

### 20) 🌊 **Suseongmot Lake** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Urban lakeside park with walking loop, cafés, and evening views.
- **Hours/price:** Park is free; musical fountain is seasonal and should not be promised in November.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Suseong-gu Tourism](https://www.suseong.kr/tour/)

### 21) 🌳 **Daegu Arboretum** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Former landfill converted into a municipal arboretum with extensive plant collections.
- **Hours/price:** Free; verify seasonal closing and Monday/holiday rules.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Arboretum](https://www.daegu.go.kr/Forestry/)

### 22) 🌉 **The ARC and Dalseong Wetland** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** River-culture building and waterside paths near the Geumho–Nakdong confluence.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor paths are free; ARC exhibition hours vary.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [The ARC](https://www.kwater.or.kr/website/arc/)

### 23) 🏺 **Bullo-dong Ancient Tomb Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Large Three Kingdoms-period burial landscape with walking paths.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor access is free; remain on marked paths.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Daegu Tourism](https://tour.daegu.go.kr/)

### 24) 🌲 **Palgongsan National Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL PARK
- **What:** Mountain park spanning Daegu and North Gyeongsang with temples and hiking routes.
- **Hours/price:** Free trails; check closures and weather.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Korea National Park Service](https://www.knps.or.kr)

### 25) 🛕 **Donghwasa Temple** — 🔎 VERIFIED TEMPLE
- **What:** Major Buddhist temple on Palgongsan.
- **Hours/price:** General entry is free; parking and programs may charge.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Donghwasa](http://www.donghwasa.net/)

### 26) 🗿 **Gatbawi Stone Buddha** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE HIKE
- **What:** Stone Buddha reached by a steep stair-and-ridge approach on Palgongsan.
- **Hours/price:** Free trail; allow daylight and check weather.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Korea National Park Service](https://www.knps.or.kr)

### 27) 🚡 **Palgongsan Cable Car** — ⏳ WEATHER-DEPENDENT
- **What:** Private cable-car route to a Palgongsan viewpoint.
- **Hours/price:** Verify daily operation and maintenance directly.
