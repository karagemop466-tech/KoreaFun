# 🌊 Incheon — verified events and activities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Incheon is a metropolitan city spread across the historic port, Songdo, Yeongjong/airport islands, inland districts, and Ganghwa County. These areas are far apart: Chinatown to Songdo is roughly 40 minutes by transit, and central Incheon to Ganghwa can take well over an hour.

This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** from official operator, museum, city/district, cultural-foundation, heritage, and tourism sources. The former 175-entry version contained fabricated attractions and festivals, nonexistent airport terminals, duplicate neighborhoods, unnamed businesses, and out-of-city padding.

## Open Port, Chinatown, and Sinpo

### 1) 🧧 **Incheon Chinatown** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Historic Chinese-Korean commercial district beside Incheon Station, with gates, restaurants, murals, and cultural institutions.
- **Hours/price:** Public streets are free; every business sets its own hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City tourism](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

### 2) 🍜 **Jjajangmyeon Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Museum in the former Gonghwachun restaurant building about the Korean-Chinese noodle dish and Chinatown food history.
- **Hours/price:** Check the district museum page; normally Tue–Sun and closed Monday.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City — Jjajangmyeon Museum](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?contentid=2034160)

### 3) 🏦 **Incheon Open Port Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Museum in a former bank building documenting the 1883 port opening and early modern exchange.
- **Hours/price:** Normally Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00; verify holiday closures and combined tickets.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City Open Port guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

### 4) 🏛️ **Open Port Modern Architecture Exhibition Hall** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Exhibition in another former bank building interpreting surviving treaty-port architecture.
- **Hours/price:** Normally Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00; check the district site.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City Open Port guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

### 5) 🇨🇳 **Korean-Chinese Cultural Center** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Exhibitions and programs about cultural exchange and Incheon’s Chinese-Korean community.
- **Hours/price:** Verify current exhibitions and Monday closure.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City Chinatown guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

### 6) 🌳 **Jayu Park and Jemulpo Club** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE AREA
- **What:** Hilltop park overlooking the port and restored international social club from the Open Port era.
- **Hours/price:** Park is free; Jemulpo Club has museum hours and may close Monday.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City Open Port guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)
- **Correction:** The MacArthur statue commemorates the **1950 Incheon Landing**, not a 1945 landing.

### 7) 🧚 **Songwol-dong Fairy Tale Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Residential hillside decorated with fairy-tale murals and installations, next to Chinatown.
- **Hours/price:** Public lanes are free. Respect residents and avoid loud night visits.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City tourism](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

### 8) 🎨 **Incheon Art Platform** — 🔎 VERIFIED ART CENTER
- **What:** Arts residency, galleries, performance spaces, and public square in restored warehouses and offices.
- **Hours/price:** Exhibition-specific; many exhibitions are free and galleries commonly close Monday.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon Art Platform](https://inartplatform.kr)

### 9) ⛪ **Dapdong Cathedral and Naeri Church** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACES
- **What:** Two important churches in the old center connected with Incheon’s modern religious history.
- **Access:** Active worship sites; sightseeing is restricted during services.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City Open Port guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

### 10) 🍗 **Sinpo International Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET
- **What:** Covered traditional market known for prepared food including dakgangjeong.
- **Hours/price:** Vendor-specific; market entry is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City Sinpo guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

## Wolmi and the port

### 11) 🚝 **Wolmi Sea Train** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Elevated tourist monorail circuit connecting Incheon Station, Wolmi Park, museums, and waterfront areas.
- **Hours/price:** Timetable and service days change; check reservations and maintenance notices.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Wolmi Sea Train](https://www.wolmiseatrain.or.kr) · [Jung-gu route guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

### 12) 🌲 **Wolmi Park, Traditional Garden, and Observatory** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Forested hill park with walking paths, reconstructed traditional gardens, and port views.
- **Hours/price:** Park access is free; individual facilities have separate hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Wolmi Park](https://wolmi.incheon.go.kr)

### 13) 🧳 **Museum of Korea Emigration History** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Municipal museum focused on the history of Korean emigration beginning through Incheon.
- **Hours/price:** **09:00–18:00, last entry 17:30. Free.** Closed **Mondays** (except when the Monday is a public holiday), **Jan 1**, and **the day after a public holiday** — that last rule is unusual and easy to trip over. ☎ 032-440-4710.
- **Allow 40–60 minutes.** Four permanent halls trace emigration from Incheon port, which is the starting point of Korean emigration history — a natural pairing with the Maritime Museum (#14), both on Wolmi.
- **Groups of 20+** must reserve by phone a week ahead; individuals just turn up.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City museum guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/en/EN030111)

### 14) ⚓ **Incheon National Maritime Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL MUSEUM
- **What:** National museum on maritime history, trade, navigation, ports and ocean culture, opened on **Wolmi Island in Dec 2024** — so it's genuinely new. Permanent halls cover maritime exchange history, ports and shipping, fishing-village culture, plus a media room and a children's museum.
- **Hours/price:** **10:00–18:00, last entry 17:30. Closed every Monday**, Jan 1, Lunar New Year's Day and Chuseok. **Free** — confirmed on the museum's own English homepage.
- **✅ Two big Greece exhibitions run right through your trip:** 《그리스, 바다가 빚은 위대한 문명》 (*Greece: A Great Civilization Shaped by the Sea*), the headline special exhibition, **Aug 11–Dec 6, 2026**, and the companion theme show 《The First Wave: 조선造船, 그리스로 가다》, **Aug 10–Dec 6, 2026**. Both are on for every day you're in Korea.
- **⚠️ Children's museum needs advance online booking** and admits children only with an accompanying adult.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon National Maritime Museum](https://www.inmm.or.kr/en/index.do)

### 15) 🎡 **Wolmi Culture Street and amusement parks** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Seafront promenade, restaurants, arcades, and privately operated rides.
- **Hours/price:** Public promenade is free; every ride has its own schedule, safety restrictions, and fare.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City Wolmi guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

### 16) ⛴️ **Wolmido–Yeongjong ferry** — ⏳ RE-CHECK SAILINGS
- **What:** Vehicle/passenger ferry linking Wolmido and Yeongjong, useful only when the current timetable fits the route.
- **Hours/price:** Weather- and operator-dependent; verify same-day sailings.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon City Wolmi guide](https://www.incheon.go.kr/world/wd030800/view?curPage=18&contentid=1963110)

## Songdo

### 17) 🌳 **Songdo Central Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Large urban park built around a seawater canal, with paths, lawns, boat facilities, and skyline views.
- **Hours/price:** Public park is free; boat and water-taxi services are paid and seasonal.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [VISITKOREA Songdo guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=137611)

### 18) 🔭 **G-Tower Observatory** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** 33rd-floor public observatory overlooking Songdo, Central Park, and the coast.
- **Hours/price:** Admission is free. Verify current weekday/weekend hours and security closures; it is **not** a paid ₩5,000 attraction.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [IFEZ](https://www.ifez.go.kr/world/main.do)

### 19) 🎭 **Tri-Bowl** — 🔎 VERIFIED VENUE
- **What:** Distinctive three-bowl cultural building hosting exhibitions and performances beside Central Park.
- **Schedule:** Building exterior and surrounding landscape are viewable anytime; interior access is event-specific.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Tri-Bowl](https://www.tribowl.kr) · [VISITKOREA Songdo guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/infoHtmlView.do?menuSn=177&vcontsId=137611)

### 20) 🔤 **National Museum of World Writing Systems** — 🔎 VERIFIED NATIONAL MUSEUM
- **What:** Only the **third museum in the world devoted to writing systems** (after Champollion in France and the National Museum of Chinese Writing), opened June 2023 in Songdo. Permanent exhibition is in B1, temporary galleries and a children's hands-on room on 1F, cafeteria on 2F looking over Songdo Central Park.
- **Hours/price:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00, last entry 17:30. Closed Mondays**, Jan 1, and Lunar New Year's/Chuseok day. **Free** (paid special exhibitions excepted). ☎ 032-290-2000.
- **⚠️ Monday-holiday rule:** if a Monday is a public holiday the museum **opens that Monday and closes the next weekday instead** — worth checking if your visit falls near a holiday.
- **Free guided tours (Korean), 60 min:** Tue–Fri 10:30 / 14:00 / 15:30, weekends and holidays 10:30 / 14:00, first-come on the day.
- **Children's hands-on room requires online advance booking** and an accompanying adult.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [National Museum of World Writing Systems](https://mow.or.kr/eng/index.do)

### 21) 🏢 **Songdo Convensia** — ⏳ CHECK EVENT CALENDAR
- **What:** Convention and exhibition center. It is not an attraction merely because a future event might occur there.
- **Status:** Attend only a dated event appearing on its official calendar.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Songdo Convensia](https://songdoconvensia.visitincheon.or.kr)

### 22) 🛍️ **Triple Street and Hyundai Premium Outlet Songdo** — 🔎 VERIFIED SHOPPING AREA
- **What:** Connected modern retail, dining, cinema, and entertainment complexes near Technopark Station.
- **Hours/price:** Free entry; tenant-specific hours.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Triple Street](https://www.triplestreet.co.kr) · [Hyundai Premium Outlets](https://www.ehyundai.com/newPortal/outlet/DP/DP000000_V.do?branchCd=B00174000)

## Yeongjong and airport area

### 23) ✈️ **Incheon International Airport cultural programs** — 🔎 VERIFIED PROGRAM
- **What:** Terminal cultural performances, exhibitions, traditional-culture experiences, observation areas, and transit services.
- **Hours/price:** Program-specific; many are free for passengers. There are **two passenger terminals**, not four.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon Airport](https://www.airport.kr/ap/en/trn/trnTourMain.do)

### 24) 🚌 **Incheon Airport transit tours** — ⏳ RESERVATION/ELIGIBILITY REQUIRED
- **What:** Airport-run short tours for eligible transit passengers, with routes varying by layover length and operating conditions.
- **Price/status:** Typically free, but immigration eligibility, advance registration, and minimum connection time apply.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon Airport transit tours](https://www.airport.kr/ap/en/trn/trnTourMain.do)

### 25) 🎨 **Paradise Art Space** — 🔎 VERIFIED GALLERY
- **What:** Contemporary-art exhibition space at Paradise City near the airport.
- **Hours/price:** Exhibition-specific; check the resort calendar before visiting solely for the gallery.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Paradise City Art](https://www.p-city.com/front/artSpace/overview)

### 26) ♨️ **Cimer at Paradise City** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Ticketed spa and pool complex near the airport.
- **Hours/price:** Date-specific and subject to maintenance days, age rules, and zone closures.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Cimer](https://www.p-city.com/front/cimer/overview)

### 27) 🎪 **Wonderbox at Paradise City** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Indoor family amusement facility within Paradise City.

### 28) 🏖️ **Eurwangni, Wangsan, and Masian beaches** — 🔎 VERIFIED COASTAL AREA
- **What:** West-coast beaches and tidal landscapes on Yeongjong/Yongyu; November is for walking and sunset, not swimming.
- **Hours/price:** Public shore access is free; parking and facilities may charge.

### 29) 🏝️ **Muuido and Hanagae Beach** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Bridge-accessible island with shore walks, trails, and beach facilities.

## Inland parks, markets, and museums

### 30) 🌳 **Incheon Grand Park and Incheon Arboretum** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Large municipal park with walking/cycling routes, arboretum, and seasonal landscapes.
- **Hours/price:** Main park access is free; individual facilities have separate hours and Monday closures.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon Grand Park](https://www.incheon.go.kr/park/)

### 31) 🧂 **Sorae Wetland Ecological Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Restored salt-field and wetland landscape with trails, bird habitat, and salt-production interpretation.
- **Hours/price:** Free outdoor access during posted hours; stay on marked paths.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon Parks](https://www.incheon.go.kr/park/)

### 32) 🐟 **Sorae Pogu Fish Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET
- **What:** Working seafood market near Sorae Port.
- **Hours/price:** Vendor-specific; check posted weights, preparation, and cooking fees.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Incheon Tourism](https://itour.incheon.go.kr/incheon/incheon.do)

### 33) 🏛️ **Incheon Metropolitan City Museum and Landing Operation Memorial Hall** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUMS
- **What:** Adjacent institutions covering regional history and the 1950 Incheon Landing.
- **Hours/price:** Check city museum hours and Monday/holiday closures; general admission is normally free.

### 34) 🏘️ **Sudoguksan Museum of Housing and Living** — 🔎 VERIFIED MUSEUM
- **What:** Museum reconstructing hillside working-class neighborhood life in 1960s–70s Incheon.
- **Hours/price:** Verify reopening/renovation status before travel; do not assume access from an old listing.

### 35) 🛍️ **Bupyeong Underground Shopping Mall** — 🔎 VERIFIED SHOPPING AREA

- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Bupyeong-gu tourism](https://www.icbp.go.kr/tour/)

## Ganghwa County

### 36) 🪨 **Ganghwa Dolmen sites** — 🔎 VERIFIED UNESCO HERITAGE
- **What:** Prehistoric dolmens forming the Ganghwa component of the Gochang, Hwasun and Ganghwa Dolmen World Heritage property.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor viewing is generally free; museum facilities have posted hours.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/977/) · [Ganghwa tourism](https://www.ganghwa.go.kr/open_content/tour/)

### 37) 🛕 **Jeondeungsa Temple and Samnangseong** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Historic Buddhist temple within fortress walls on southern Ganghwa.
- **Hours/price:** Active temple; verify access, parking, and templestay separately.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Jeondeungsa](https://www.jeondeungsa.org) · [Ganghwa tourism](https://www.ganghwa.go.kr/open_content/tour/)

### 38) 🛡️ **Gwangseongbo coastal fortress** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Fortification complex associated with Ganghwa’s coastal defenses and 19th-century conflicts.
- **Hours/price:** Check Ganghwa County for seasonal hours and admission.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ganghwa tourism](https://www.ganghwa.go.kr/open_content/tour/)

### 39) 🔭 **Ganghwa Peace Observatory** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Observatory overlooking the estuary and North Korea from northern Ganghwa.
- **Access:** Identification and security rules may apply; weather can limit views.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ganghwa tourism](https://www.ganghwa.go.kr/open_content/tour/)

### 40) 🥾 **Ganghwa Nadeul-gil** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALKING NETWORK
- **What:** Waymarked island routes linking coast, villages, fortifications, and heritage sites.
- **Hours/price:** Trails are free; route conditions and bus access vary.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ganghwa Nadeul-gil](https://www.ganghwa.go.kr/open_content/tour/trip/nadeulgil.jsp)

### 41) ♨️ **Seokmodo Mineral Hot Spring** — 🔎 VERIFIED OPERATOR
- **What:** Managed mineral-bath facility with outdoor pools facing the West Sea.
- **Hours/price:** Check Ganghwa County for current closure day, attire rules, capacity, and admission before the long trip.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Ganghwa tourism](https://www.ganghwa.go.kr/open_content/tour/)

### 42) 🖼️ **Incheon ArtShow 2026 (INAS)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Thu Nov 19–Sun Nov 22, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Contemporary art fair at Songdo Convensia, organized by the Incheon ArtShow Organizing Committee.
- **When (exact, from the organizer's own table):** **Nov 19–22, 2026.** Thu Nov 19 **11:00–19:00** (VIP preview + general open; last entry 18:00, opening ceremony 15:00 "subject to change") · Fri Nov 20–Sat Nov 21 **10:00–19:00** (last entry 18:00) · **Sun Nov 22 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00)**.
- **Theme:** *Beyond Asia, Connect Global*. Alongside the booths the organizer runs a docent tour, art-talk programme, an art academy and an artist talk by actor-painter **Park Shin-yang**.
- **✅ The one event that actually works on departure day.** Songdo Convensia is on the Incheon side, roughly 30–40 min from ICN — so unlike everything else competing for Nov 22 (G-STAR in Busan, the E-Land finale in Seoul), you can do a couple of hours here and still make an afternoon or evening flight. Just respect the **17:00 last entry**.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 18):** [Incheon ArtShow 2026 — viewing schedule table](https://iartshow.imweb.me/)
