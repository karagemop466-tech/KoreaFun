# ✈️ Incheon — Gateway City, Chinatown & Coast (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's classic port of arrivals** — west of Seoul, home to **Incheon International Airport (ICN)** in Yeongjong Island, the historic **Incheon Chinatown**, and the futuristic new-town district of **Songdo International Business City**. Linked to Seoul by AREX Airport Railway, subway, and the Gyeongin Expressway.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering the Open Port historical streets, Chinatown, and Songdo Central Park.

**Official Incheon sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Incheon (official tourism): [incheon.go.kr](https://www.incheon.go.kr)
- Incheon Tourism Organization: [itourtv.com](https://www.itourtv.com)
- Paradise City Hotel (resort near airport): [p-city.com](https://www.p-city.com)
- Songdo Convensia: [songdoconvensia.com](https://www.songdoconvensia.com)

**Getting there:** Seoul Station (Line 1) ↔ Incheon (AREX, 60-min to airport, ~70-min to Incheon Station); KTX from Seoul Station to Incheon International Airport Terminal ≈ 50 min

## ✈️ Airport area (Yeongjong-do)

### 1) 🚌 Incheon Airport · Free Shuttle & Airline Programs — 🔁 daily

- **Hours:** 24/7 daily
- **Price:** AREX Express = **₩9,500** to Seoul Station (~$7); AREX All-Stop = **₩4,150** (~$3); regular buses from airport to major Seoul hotels from **₩15,000–18,000**

- **Beginner notes:** Take **AREX Express** from Seoul Station for the fastest deal; kiosk at airport for SIM/eSIM pickup; T-money cards available at convenience stores

## 🏛️ Open Port historical district (Incheon city center)

### 8) 🚢 Incheon Open Port Modern History Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** 19th-century red-brick restored warehouses documenting Incheon's opening to foreign trade in 1883 and the role of the Port in the opening of Korea (1866–1899), displayed through English-readable panels
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [opmm.go.kr](https://www.opmm.go.kr) · Incheon Station Exit 2 (2 min walk)
- **Beginner notes:** Start of a half-day cultural walk.

### 9) 🏛️ Incheon Jayu (Freedom) Park — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Hilltop symbol of liberation featuring the statue of General Douglas MacArthur (commemorating the 1945 landing at Incheon, which turned around the Korean War)
- **Hours:** Open 24/7
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [incheon.go.kr](https://www.incheon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Fantastic views over Incheon Port, harbor, and the Free Economic Zone

### 10) 🏯 Incheon Sinpo International Market (신포국제시장) — 🔁 daily (FREE walk-in)
- **What:** Bustling century-old traditional market right next to Chinatown and Jayu Park. Vendors sell tteokbokki, dumplings, handmade noodles, fried chicken.
- **Hours:** Stalls daily **09:00–22:00**
- **Price:** Free walk-in; dishes ~₩2,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [incheon.go.kr](https://www.incheon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Combine with Chinatown.

### 11) 🥟 Incheon Chinatown (인천 차이나타운) — ✅ CONFIRMED — central hub open daily
- **What:** Korea's only official Chinatown (est. 1884); centered on the iconic red **Paeru Pavilion**, painted dragon walls, and dozens of Chinese-Korean restaurants and food stands. Famous for *jjajangmyeon* (black-bean noodles), fried *mandu*, and sweet-and-sour pork
- **Hours:** Daily **10:00–23:00** (food stalls run later)
- **Price:** Free walk-in; dishes ~₩5,000–15,000
- **Official source:** [chinatown.or.kr](http://www.chinatown.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Try the **8-jjajangmyeon (eight-cuisine tasting)** at Gonghwachun.

### 12) 🥟 Incheon Chinatown Paeru Pavilion — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Multi-story red pagoda in the center of Chinatown — symbol of Korea-China cultural history
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 externally; themed photo-friendly interior
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [chinatown.or.kr](http://www.chinatown.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Perfect photo inside.

### 13) 🏛️ Incheon Anglican Church — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Historic 1884 red-brick Anglican church, oldest in Korea, surviving the Korean War — an English-language plaque memory of Korean Christianity's 1880s entry
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–17:00** for interior visits
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [incheon.go.kr](https://www.incheon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** A 2-min walk from Chinatown.

### 14) 🚂 Incheon Station (Former Japanese) Heritage Building — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Restored 1899 red-brick railway station building, now an exhibition hall narrating the Open Port Era
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00–18:00
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [incheon.go.kr](https://www.incheon.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Connected by short walk to Chinatown.

### 15) 🏯 Sinsido / Sinsu Island Cross-Sea Day Trip — 👀 seasonal schedules
- **What:** Small fishing island off Incheon coast — *Sinsu Port* offers a quiet seafood market lunch and a sunset ferry ride
- **Hours:** Variable

## 🧭 Beginner-friendly essentials

- **From Seoul:** Take AREX or Line 1 — both convenient. From **ICN airport**, paid buses to major Seoul hotels from ₩15,000; AREX Express ₩9,500 single trip
- **Day trip itinerary:** Seoul 09:00 → Incheon Chinatown 11:00 (lunch Jjajangmyeon) → Jayu Park 13:00 → Open Port History Museum/Sinpo Market 14:00 → Wolmido 16:00 → Seoul 19:00 back
- **For arrival/return day:** Plan to arrive through ICN → AREX to hotel. On departure day, check Songdo/Central Park for last sights if time permits
