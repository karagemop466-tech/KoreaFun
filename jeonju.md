# 🏘️ Jeonju — Hanok Heritage & Bibimbap Capital (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's slow-food & heritage capital** — a charming city 2 hr southwest of Seoul by KTX (transfer at Iksan). Famous for the **Jeonju Hanok Village**, **UNESCO Royal Cuisine**, **bibimbap**, and hundreds of hip artisan cafes and craft shops tucked inside traditional tiled-roof houses.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Hanok Village, Gyeonggijeon Shrine, and Jeonju's bibimbap culinary alley.

**Official Jeonju sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Jeonju (official tourism): [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- Jeonju Hanok Village: [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- Jeonju International Film Festival (JIFF): [jiff.or.kr](https://www.jiff.or.kr)
- Korea Traditional Food Culture Center: [koreanfood.or.kr](https://www.koreanfood.or.kr)

**Getting there:** KTX Seoul → Iksan (≈ 1h 50m), local train/bus to Jeonju (≈ 20–40 min) · Or take direct direct ITX from Yongsan/Sin-gwang **3 hr** steps. Note: many travelers also drive from Daejeon/Suwon (1 hr 30 min)

## 🏘️ Hanok Village — the heart of the city

### 1) 🏘️ Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's largest concentration of traditional Korean hanok homes — over **800 traditional tiled-roof houses** packed into a 0.32 km² historical district. Cafes, museums, hanok stays, traditional bibimbap restaurants, art studios. UNESCO-recognized cultural heritage area
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 (most museums 09:00–18:00)
- **Price:** **FREE** village; museum entries ~₩2,000–5,000
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Walkable in 4–6 hours — start at Hyanggyo Confucian School, loop via Gyeonggijeon Shrine, eat bibimbap, hit Jaman Mural Village, end at Nambu Market.

### 2) 🏯 Gyeonggijeon Shrine (경기전) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** 1410 CE Joseon-era Jinseng shrine dedicated to King Taejo (founder of Joseon), holding his royal portrait. Beautiful old pine tree at the main gate is one of Korea's most photographed
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2)
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Photo-worthy gate.

### 3) 🎎 Jeonju Hyanggyo Confucian School — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Immaculately preserved Joseon-era Confucian academy campus with hyanggyo hall, lecture hall, and shrine
- **Hours:** Tu–Su **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Photogenic.

### 4) 🎨 Jeonju Traditional Culture Center — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Modern exhibition hall right at the entrance of Hanok Village showcasing traditional Korean arts
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (Nov winter hours)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Free 30-min introduction to the city.

### 5) 🎭 Jeonju Fan Culture Museum (전주 부채 문화관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Housed in a hanok, dedicated to the **Korean fan (buchae)** — paper-and-bamboo craft the city has produced for centuries. 200+ historical fan displays + hands-on workshop
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2); workshop ~₩5,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Get your own painted fan!

### 6) 🌿 Jeonju Traditional Cultural Life Experience Hall — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Indoor folk museum letting you make Korean paper (hanji), use a wooden loom, mix natural dyes; perfect rainy-day stop
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–18:00
- **Price:** Workshop ~₩10,000–20,000; museum free
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bring your finished product home.

### 7) 🎨 Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall & Hanji Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Largest dedicated museum of paper arts, with traditional paper-making workshops
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (Nov)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000**
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pick up hand-made paper souvenirs.

### 8) 🏯 Jeonju Traditional Tea Ceremony House & Tea Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun

## 🍴 Jeonju food — the bibimbap capital of Korea

### 9) 🥣 Jeonju Bibimbap Festival (전주 비빔밥 축제) — 👀 WATCH (Oct/Nov annual; 2026 dates TBA)
- **What:** Annual celebration of Korea's most famous single dish: bibimbap in hundreds of styles. Held in and around Hanok Village. Cooking contests, music performances, traditional bibimbap tastings, foraging masterclasses (hanwoo, légumes wild greens)
- **Dates:** 2026 TBA — recent editions ran late Oct/early Nov (2024 ran Nov 1–3 at Hanbyeokgung Plaza)
- **Where:** Jeonju Hanok Village center & surroundings
- **Price:** Free entry; cooking experiences paid
- **Official source:** [bibimbapfest.com](http://www.bibimbapfest.com)
- **Beginner notes:** The single best opportunity to Korea-eat bibimbap in context.

### 10) 🥩 Jeonju Original Bibimbap Alley (오늘이전주의집) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Historic flagship Hanok Village restaurant, on record for being the original *bibimbap* pioneers since the 1950s
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–21:00
- **Price:** ~₩8,000–12,000
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Drone-cult signature. **Try the dolsot (stone-pot) bibimbap** to make the rice crispy.

### 11) 🍜 Jeonju Hanok Tradition Restaurants — 🔁 daily (FREE walk-in)
- **What:** Cluster of 30+ traditional restaurants inside hanok serving royal court cuisine, *kongnamul gukbap* (bean sprout soup), and Korean vegetables
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–21:00
- **Price:** ₩7,000–25,000
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Try the bean sprout soup for breakfast.

### 12) 🛒 Nambu Traditional Market (남부수산시장) — 🔁 daily (FREE walk-in)
- **What:** 100-year-old traditional market operating since the 1900s. Vendors serve street food tteokbokki, mandu, *ggobgi* sauce, fresh vegetables, plus hanbok fabric
- **Hours:** Stalls daily 09:00–22:00 (closed 2nd & 4th Sunday)
- **Price:** Free walk-in; snacks ~₩2,000–8,000
- **Official source:** [nambusijang.com](http://www.nambusijang.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Bean sprout soup alley for an authentic eat.

### 13) 🍵 Jeonju Hanji Tea & Specialty Cafe Tour — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Hanok Village & adjacent neighborhoods host 100+ themed cafes — paper art, hanji fabric, traditional teas
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00–22:00
- **Price:** Costa ~₩5,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Jeonju is a coffee-lover city — many outlets serve Korean teas, baking, and desserts in modern hanok interiors.

### 14) 🍫 Jeonju Chocopie & Chocolate Specialty Stores — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Jeonju is the home of the famous Korean *Chocopie* (the original brand HQ). Multiple boutique chocolate and bakery stores — including **Paula's Bean to Bar Chocolate**
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00–19:00
- **Price:** ₩8,000–30,000 per box
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Take a few boxes home.

### 15) 🍺 Makgeolli & Korean Liquor Bars (전주 전통주 가게) — 🔁 nightly
- **What:** Traditional Korean liquors — *makgeolli* (rice wine), *cheongju* (clear), and *soju* — brewed locally
- **Hours:** Nightly 18:00–24:00
- **Price:** ₩5,000–15,000
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pair with Jeonju-style *pajeon* (green onion pancakes).

## 🧭 Beginner-friendly essentials

- **Seoul day trip:** Seoul 08:00 KTX → Iksan 09:50 → local train/bus to Jeonju **11:00** → Hanok Village 11:00–22:00 → Overnight → return 22:00 KTX back. Or **2-day/1-night** recommended
- **Bibimbap Festival date question:** Late Oct/early Nov — watch the official site
- **First-time visitor Itinerary:** Pungnammun → Gyeonggijeon → Hanok Village walk → Bibimbap lunch → Omokdae → Drive to Moaksan sunset → Nambu Market dinner → Stay overnight in hanok
