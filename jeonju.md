# 🏘️ Jeonju — verified events and activities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Jeonju is known for its large living hanok district, Joseon royal heritage, pansori, hanji crafts, and food culture. It is a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy, but **Jeonju Hanok Village is not a UNESCO World Heritage Site**.

This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** from official organizer, venue/operator, Jeonju City, national heritage, museum, market, and tourism sources, and re-checked against live official pages on **August 18, 2026** (Pass 37). The former 159-entry version mixed real attractions with fabricated museums and festivals, unnamed businesses, duplicates, out-of-city day trips, and travel advice counted as activities.

> **Status**
> - ✅ **Confirmed event** — a dated organizer or city page overlaps the trip.
> - 🔎 **Verified place/activity** — a real permanent place supported by an official source; hours and prices can change.
> - ⏳ **Re-check** — a real venue or recurring program whose individual November schedule must be confirmed.

- **What:** Street-theater parade incorporating Jeonju Gijeopnori flag performance, traditional outdoor theater, and audience interaction.
- **When:** The official 2026 listing runs **Saturdays, Apr 18–Oct 31, at 15:00 (60 min)** — the final performance of the season falls on **Sat Oct 31, the trip's first day**.

### 2) ✨ **Gyeonggijeon Starlight Walk (경기전 별빛누빔)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Fridays through Nov 2026
- **What:** Friday-night cultural program inside Gyeonggijeon — a themed walk with performance and experience stops after daytime hours.
- **When:** Official 2026 listing: **June–November, Friday nights only**. **September–November entry sessions are 19:30 and 20:30.** Trip-window Fridays: **Nov 6, 13 and 20**. (Jun–Jul ran 20:30/21:30; **Jul 10–Aug 28 was a heat-wave shutdown**.)

- **⚠️ The inner 정전 is closed for restoration (see #6).** The 2026 official page describes a precinct walk/performance/experience course — it does **not** promise a walk through the Taejo portrait hall. Do not plan on entering the closed inner shrine.
- **Getting there:** No dedicated car park — use the Hanok Village public lot or Nambu Market, both a 5–10 min walk.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 18):** [Visit Jeonju — 경기전 별빛누빔](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000102002001000&facSid=RE_0001040) (Jun–Nov Fridays; Sep–Nov 19:30 / 20:30; ₩10,000; TicketLink)

### 4) 🧘 **Return to the Ritual Hall: The Nahan (Arhat) Statues of Jeonju Seogosa** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Sep 16–Nov 29, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Special exhibition at **Jeonju National Museum** built around the **nahan (arhat) statues of Seogosa Temple** in Jeonju — the disciples of the Buddha, carved with the wry, individualized, almost comic faces that make Korean arhat sculpture so distinctive. The museum programmed this to spotlight the statues ahead of their expected designation as Jeollabuk-do tangible cultural heritage, so you're seeing them at the moment they're being formally recognized.
- **When:** **Wed Sep 16 – Sun Nov 29, 2026**, Special Exhibition Hall (기획전시실) — covers your entire trip.
- **Price:** National museums in Korea are free for permanent galleries and most special exhibitions.
- **Note:** The museum lists the title as provisional (가제), so the final English title may be worded differently.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 18):** [Jeonju National Museum exhibition page](https://jeonju.museum.go.kr/special.es?mid=a10201010000&seq=1711&act=view) (2026.09.16~2026.11.29, 기획전시실, 가제) · [NMK branch listing](https://www.museum.go.kr/MUSEUM/contents/M0207000000.do?museum=JEONJU)

## Hanok Village and royal heritage

### 5) 🏘️ **Jeonju Hanok Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Living neighborhood containing hundreds of hanok, cultural facilities, guesthouses, workshops, restaurants, and historic sites.
- **Hours/price:** Public streets are free and continuously accessible; every museum, house, workshop, and business sets its own hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Jeonju Hanok Village](https://hanok.jeonju.go.kr/)

### 6) 👑 **Gyeonggijeon Shrine and Royal Portrait Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Shrine established to enshrine a portrait of King Taejo, founder of Joseon; the precinct also interprets royal portrait and historical-record traditions.
- **Hours:** **Winter (Nov–Feb) 09:00–18:00 — last entry 17:00, one hour before closing.** Shoulder season (Mar–May, Sep–Oct) 09:00–19:00; Jun–Aug 09:00–20:00. Closed only **Jan 1**.
- **Price:** **₩3,000 adult / ₩2,000 youth and military / ₩1,000 child.** Groups of 20+: ₩2,500 / ₩1,500 / ₩500. **The ticket includes the Royal Portrait Museum (어진박물관)** — it is not a separate charge. Allow **1–1.5 hrs** for both.
- **⚠️ 정전 (main shrine hall) is closed for dancheong restoration through the whole trip.** Operator notice: **Thu Mar 26 – Thu Dec 17, 2026** (end date may be extended). **You cannot enter the inner 정전 zone past the Naesinmun gate.** The rest of the precinct and the Royal Portrait Museum stay open; there is **no hoarding**, so the restoration work on the national-heritage hall is visible. Do not plan on walking into the portrait hall itself.
- **✅ Free on "Culture Day" — which since April 2026 is EVERY Wednesday, not just the last one.** That puts **Wed Nov 4, 11 and 18** in play. *(The operator visitor page still prints the stale “매월 마지막주 수요일” rule.)* Confirm at the gate before relying on it. Fourth Saturday of the month is Jeonju Hanbok Day (50% off if wearing hanbok).
- **Open year-round**, including Mondays — unlike most of the Hanok Village's museums.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 18):** [어진박물관 관람안내 — hours, last-entry rule, fare table](https://jeonju.go.kr/eojinmuseum/index.9is?contentUid=9be517a78020a96201827be8a13d2860) · [정전 단청공사 관람 안내 (2026.3.26.~공사 종료 시까지)](https://www.jeonju.go.kr/eojinmuseum/planweb/board/view.9is?boardUid=9be517a78020a9620182a44463067f48&contentUid=9be517a78020a96201827bebe0d52920&dataUid=9be517a89a2f1137019d1d8c2019416f)

### 7) ⛪ **Jeondong Catholic Cathedral** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Early-20th-century Romanesque cathedral at the Hanok Village entrance, built at a site associated with early Korean Catholic martyrs.
- **Access:** Active church; sightseeing is restricted during Mass and services.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — cathedral](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000016&menuCd=DOM_000000201005001000&paging=ok&startPage=1&dataSid=10023)

### 8) 🎓 **Jeonju Hyanggyo** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Confucian school complex known for historic buildings and mature ginkgo trees.
- **Hours/price (corrected Aug 18):** **Free.** Hours are seasonal and **your trip is in the winter season: Nov–Feb 10:00–17:00** — an hour later opening and an hour earlier close than the 09:00–18:00 summer (Mar–Oct) schedule the entry previously gave. **Closed Mondays**; if a Monday is a public holiday it opens and closes the Tuesday instead (no holidays fall in your window, so plain Monday closure applies). ☎ 063-288-4544.
- **🍁 The reason to come in November:** the courtyard ginkgos are **over 400 years old** and turn gold in **early-to-mid November** — this is one of Jeonju's signature autumn sights and your trip lands squarely on it. Founded under King Gongmin (Goryeo), it was the leading hyanggyo in the Honam region.
- **Tips:** free entry via a side gate 5–10 min walk from the main Hanok Village streets. **Best light ~10:00–11:00 or around 16:00.** Weekdays are far quieter — weekends fill with hanbok photo shoots. It's still an active heritage/educational site, so keep noise down.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju autumn guide](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000025&menuCd=DOM_000000209001000000&paging=ok&startPage=2&dataSid=12750)

### 36) ⚽ **Jeonbuk Hyundai Motors — Jeonju World Cup Stadium** — ⏳ FIXTURES NOT PUBLISHED UNTIL LATE OCTOBER · UPDATED
- **What:** Korea's most successful club of the modern era, playing **K League 1** at **Jeonju World Cup Stadium (전주월드컵경기장, "전주성")** — a 2002 World Cup venue in the north of the city, well away from the Hanok Village.
- **⚠️ Same situation as every K League 1 club — the November fixtures don't exist yet.** The league runs a **33-round regular phase to Oct 25, 2026**, then splits into a **Final Round (34–38) played Oct 31–Dec 6**. Those five fixtures are only drawn once the regular season ends, so at review there was **no published Jeonbuk home date** inside your window. Treat any site claiming otherwise as unreliable.
- **What you can rely on:** the final-round window **spans your whole trip**, and each club gets **2–3 home games** in it, so a Jeonju home match during your stay is likely — just not yet schedulable.
