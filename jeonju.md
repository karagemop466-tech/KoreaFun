# 🏘️ Jeonju — verified events and activities (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

Jeonju is known for its large living hanok district, Joseon royal heritage, pansori, hanji crafts, and food culture. It is a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy, but **Jeonju Hanok Village is not a UNESCO World Heritage Site**.

This file was rebuilt on **August 17, 2026** from official organizer, venue/operator, Jeonju City, national heritage, museum, market, and tourism sources. The former 159-entry version mixed real attractions with fabricated museums and festivals, unnamed businesses, duplicates, out-of-city day trips, and travel advice counted as activities.

> **Status**
> - ✅ **Confirmed event** — a dated organizer or city page overlaps the trip.
> - 🔎 **Verified place/activity** — a real permanent place supported by an official source; hours and prices can change.
> - ⏳ **Re-check** — a real venue or recurring program whose individual November schedule must be confirmed.
>
> Many Hanok Village cultural facilities close Monday. Verify each venue before making a special trip.

## Confirmed during the trip

### 1) 🥁 **Hanok Village Traditional Yeonhui Parade** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Oct 31, 2026
- **What:** Street-theater parade incorporating Jeonju Gijeopnori flag performance, traditional outdoor theater, and audience interaction.
- **When:** The official 2026 listing runs **Saturdays, Apr 18–Oct 31, at 15:00 (60 min)** — the final performance of the season falls on **Sat Oct 31, the trip's first day**.
- **Where/price:** Parade route runs **Namcheongyo Bridge → Gyeonggijeon** through the Hanok Village; free street viewing. Confirm weather cancellation on the day.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/index.jeonju)

### 2) ✨ **Gyeonggijeon Starlight Walk (경기전 별빛누빔)** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Fridays through Nov 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Timed evening cultural program inside Gyeonggijeon, presenting the shrine after normal daytime hours.
- **When:** Official listing confirms **June–November, Friday nights only**. For **September–November the two entry sessions are 19:30 and 20:30** (earlier in the year they ran an hour later). Trip-window Fridays are **Nov 6, 13 and 20**. Capacity is limited per session, so book rather than turn up.
- **Price/booking:** Sessions are capped at roughly **50 people each**. In past editions **half the tickets sold via TicketLink in advance and half were sold on-site at the Gyeonggijeon main gate from about an hour before** — so a walk-up is possible but not safe. Content is a lit-up course through the shrine grounds (Taejo portrait hall, pine path, the Jeondong Cathedral wall view) ending with a fusion-gugak performance.
- **Getting there:** No dedicated car park — use the Hanok Village public lot or Nambu Market, both a 5–10 min walk.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju 2026 listing](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/index.jeonju)

### 3) 🎶 **Jeonju Daesaseupcheong Saturday Regular Performance** — ⏳ SERIES LISTED, NOVEMBER DATES UNPOSTED — May–Nov 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Regular traditional-music performance series at Jeonju Daesaseupcheong.
- **When:** The Hanok Village calendar lists the series as **May–November, Saturdays**. **⚠️ Re-checked Aug 17: the tourism site's related Saturday listing (at the Jeonju Sago pavilion inside Gyeonggijeon, 14:00 and 15:00) is documented only through the earlier part of the season, and no November date list has been posted yet.** Treat this as likely-but-unconfirmed and check on arrival.
- **Price:** Program-specific. The comparable Gyeonggijeon Saturday performance is free, though Gyeonggijeon admission is charged separately.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [Hanok Village program calendar](https://hanok.jeonju.go.kr/) · [Visit Jeonju event calendar](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000202002000000)

### 4) 🧘 **Return to the Ritual Hall: The Nahan (Arhat) Statues of Jeonju Seogosa** — ✅ CONFIRMED — Sep 16–Nov 29, 2026 · NEW
- **What:** Special exhibition at **Jeonju National Museum** built around the **nahan (arhat) statues of Seogosa Temple** in Jeonju — the disciples of the Buddha, carved with the wry, individualized, almost comic faces that make Korean arhat sculpture so distinctive. The museum programmed this to spotlight the statues ahead of their expected designation as Jeollabuk-do tangible cultural heritage, so you're seeing them at the moment they're being formally recognized.
- **When:** **Wed Sep 16 – Sun Nov 29, 2026**, Special Exhibition Hall (기획전시실) — covers your entire trip.
- **Price:** National museums in Korea are free for permanent galleries and most special exhibitions.
- **Note:** The museum lists the title as provisional (가제), so the final English title may be worded differently.
- **Official sources (reviewed Aug 17):** [National Museum of Korea — Jeonju exhibitions](https://www.museum.go.kr/MUSEUM/contents/M0207000000.do?museum=JEONJU) · [Jeonju National Museum](https://jeonju.museum.go.kr/special.es?mid=a10201010000&seq=1711&act=view)

## Hanok Village and royal heritage

### 5) 🏘️ **Jeonju Hanok Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Living neighborhood containing hundreds of hanok, cultural facilities, guesthouses, workshops, restaurants, and historic sites.
- **Hours/price:** Public streets are free and continuously accessible; every museum, house, workshop, and business sets its own hours.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Jeonju Hanok Village](https://hanok.jeonju.go.kr/)

### 6) 👑 **Gyeonggijeon Shrine and Royal Portrait Museum** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Shrine established to enshrine a portrait of King Taejo, founder of Joseon; the precinct also interprets royal portrait and historical-record traditions.
- **Hours:** Visit Jeonju lists Nov–Feb 09:00–17:00. Admission applies; verify last entry and closures.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju guide](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000025&menuCd=DOM_000000209001000000&paging=ok&startPage=2&dataSid=12484)

### 7) ⛪ **Jeondong Catholic Cathedral** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Early-20th-century Romanesque cathedral at the Hanok Village entrance, built at a site associated with early Korean Catholic martyrs.
- **Access:** Active church; sightseeing is restricted during Mass and services.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — cathedral](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000016&menuCd=DOM_000000201005001000&paging=ok&startPage=1&dataSid=10023)

### 8) 🎓 **Jeonju Hyanggyo** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Confucian school complex known for historic buildings and mature ginkgo trees.
- **Hours/price:** Official tourism material lists 09:00–18:00 and Monday closure; admission is free. Verify seasonal access.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju autumn guide](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000025&menuCd=DOM_000000209001000000&paging=ok&startPage=2&dataSid=12750)

### 9) 🌄 **Omokdae and Imokdae** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE WALK
- **What:** Hilltop pavilions connected with Yi Seong-gye and his ancestors, offering views across the hanok roofs.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor access is free; steps can be slippery in rain and fallen leaves.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — Omokdae and Imokdae](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000016&menuCd=DOM_000000209001000000&paging=ok&startPage=1&dataSid=10020)

### 10) 🚪 **Pungnammun Gate** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Surviving south gate of old Jeonju Fortress, immediately beside Nambu Market.
- **Hours/price:** Exterior viewing is free. Interior access is not assumed.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — Pungnammun route](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000201005001000&boardId=BBS_0000016&contentsSid=327&paging=ok&startPage=1&dataSid=10021)

### 11) 🎨 **Jaman Mural Village** — 🔎 VERIFIED AREA
- **What:** Hillside residential area with murals and small businesses east of the Hanok Village.
- **Hours/price:** Public lanes are free. Respect residents, keep noise down, and do not treat homes as exhibits.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — Jaman](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000024&menuCd=DOM_000000201011000000&paging=ok&startPage=4&dataSid=10491)

### 12) 🏛️ **Jeolla Gamyeong** — 🔎 VERIFIED HERITAGE PLACE
- **What:** Restored provincial-government complex representing Jeolla administration during the Joseon period.
- **Hours:** Official listing gives winter hours 09:00–18:00 and free Korean/English tours at 11:00 and 14:00, subject to guide availability.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — Jeolla Gamyeong](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000016&menuCd=DOM_000000201009000000&paging=ok&startPage=4&dataSid=10017)

### 13) 🌉 **Hanbyeokdang and Jeonjucheon riverside** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK
- **What:** Historic pavilion and riverside landscape east of the Hanok Village, near the National Intangible Heritage Center.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor access is free; flood or maintenance restrictions may apply.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju attractions](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000016&menuCd=DOM_000000201005003000&paging=ok&startPage=1&dataSid=16140)

## Parks and local neighborhoods

### 26) 🌳 **Deokjin Park** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** City park centered on a large lotus pond, with walking routes and pavilions.
- **Hours/price:** Public park access is free. Lotus flowering is a summer feature, not a November event.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju year-round attractions](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000025&menuCd=DOM_000000209001000000&paging=ok&startPage=1&dataSid=12948)

### 27) 🌲 **Jeonju Arboretum** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Korea Expressway Corporation arboretum with themed plant collections and seasonal gardens.
- **Hours/price:** Check the operator’s current visitor page; November hours may be shorter.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Jeonju Arboretum](https://www.ex.co.kr/arboretum/)

### 28) 🦁 **Jeonju Zoo** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Municipal ecological zoo with redesigned animal habitats.
- **Hours/price:** Check the city listing for seasonal hours and admission.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — zoo](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000016&menuCd=DOM_000000201005002000&paging=ok&startPage=1&dataSid=10031)

### 29) 🌺 **Wansan Park and Wansan Flower Garden** — 🔎 VERIFIED PLACE
- **What:** Hillside urban park south of the old center with walking paths and viewpoints. Its famous flowering displays are seasonal and should not be promised in November.
- **Hours/price:** Outdoor access is free.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju attractions](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000201005002000)

### 30) 🚶 **Jeonjucheon and Samcheon riverside paths** — 🔎 VERIFIED WALK

## Food and markets

### 31) 🛒 **Jeonju Nambu Market and Youth Mall** — 🔎 VERIFIED MARKET
- **What:** Historic traditional market beside Pungnammun with produce, prepared food, shops, and an upstairs youth-mall area.
- **Hours/price:** Vendor-specific; market sections have different closure days.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — Nambu Market](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000203011000000)

### 32) 🌃 **Nambu Market Hanok Village Night Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED PROGRAM
- **What:** Friday and Saturday evening market with mobile food and retail stalls.
- **Status:** Official tourism describes it as year-round, but weather and operational notices can cancel dates. Check locally before attending.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju — night market](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/board/view.jeonju?boardId=BBS_0000016&menuCd=DOM_000000209001000000&paging=ok&startPage=1&dataSid=16137)

### 33) 🍚 **Jeonju bibimbap and the city’s official food directory** — 🔎 VERIFIED LOCAL FOOD
- **What:** Bibimbap is a recognized Jeonju specialty, but there is no need to invent a single “original alley.” Choose a named restaurant from the city directory.
- **Hours/price:** Restaurant-specific.
- **Official source (reviewed Aug 17):** [Visit Jeonju food and shopping](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr/eng/index.jeonju?menuCd=DOM_000000203000000000)

### 34) 🥣 **Kongnamul-gukbap and pisundae at Nambu Market** — 🔎 VERIFIED LOCAL FOOD
