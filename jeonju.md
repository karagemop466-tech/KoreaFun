# 🏘️ Jeonju — Hanok Heritage & Bibimbap Capital (Oct 31 – Nov 22, 2026)

**Korea's slow-food & heritage capital** — a charming city 2 hr southwest of Seoul by KTX (transfer at Iksan). Famous for the **Jeonju Hanok Village**, **UNESCO Royal Cuisine**, **bibimbap**, and hundreds of hip artisan cafes and craft shops tucked inside traditional tiled-roof houses.

> 🗺️ **Looking for self-guided day routes?** Check [`walking-maps.md`](walking-maps.md) for 1-day walking & food courses covering Hanok Village, Gyeonggijeon Shrine, and Jeonju's bibimbap culinary alley.

**Official Jeonju sources to re-check as your trip nears:**
- Visit Jeonju (official tourism): [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- Jeonju Hanok Village: [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- Jeonju International Film Festival (JIFF): [jiff.or.kr](https://www.jiff.or.kr)
- Korea Traditional Food Culture Center: [koreanfood.or.kr](https://www.koreanfood.or.kr)

**Getting there:** KTX Seoul → Iksan (≈ 1h 50m), local train/bus to Jeonju (≈ 20–40 min) · Or take direct direct ITX from Yongsan/Sin-gwang **3 hr** steps. Note: many travelers also drive from Daejeon/Suwon (1 hr 30 min)

## 🏘️ Hanok Village — the heart of the city

### 1) 🏘️ Jeonju Hanok Village (전주 한옥마을) — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Korea's largest concentration of traditional Korean hanok homes — over **800 traditional tiled-roof houses** packed into a 0.32 km² historical district. Cafes, museums, hanok stays, traditional bibimbap restaurants, art studios. UNESCO-recognized cultural heritage area
- **Hours:** Open 24/7 (most museums 09:00–18:00)
- **Price:** **FREE** village; museum entries ~₩2,000–5,000
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Walkable in 4–6 hours — start at Hyanggyo Confucian School, loop via Gyeonggijeon Shrine, eat bibimbap, hit Jaman Mural Village, end at Nambu Market.

### 2) 🏯 Gyeonggijeon Shrine (경기전) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** 1410 CE Joseon-era Jinseng shrine dedicated to King Taejo (founder of Joseon), holding his royal portrait. Beautiful old pine tree at the main gate is one of Korea's most photographed
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **09:00–18:00** (closed Mondays)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2)
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Photo-worthy gate.

### 3) 🎎 Jeonju Hyanggyo Confucian School — 🔁 always on (FREE)
- **What:** Immaculately preserved Joseon-era Confucian academy campus with hyanggyo hall, lecture hall, and shrine
- **Hours:** Tu–Su **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Photogenic.

### 4) 🎨 Jeonju Traditional Culture Center — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** Modern exhibition hall right at the entrance of Hanok Village showcasing traditional Korean arts
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (Nov winter hours)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Free 30-min introduction to the city.

### 5) 🎭 Jeonju Fan Culture Museum (전주 부채 문화관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Housed in a hanok, dedicated to the **Korean fan (buchae)** — paper-and-bamboo craft the city has produced for centuries. 200+ historical fan displays + hands-on workshop
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000** (~$2); workshop ~₩5,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Get your own painted fan!

### 6) 🌿 Jeonju Traditional Cultural Life Experience Hall — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Indoor folk museum letting you make Korean paper (hanji), use a wooden loom, mix natural dyes; perfect rainy-day stop
- **Hours:** Daily 09:00–18:00
- **Price:** Workshop ~₩10,000–20,000; museum free
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bring your finished product home.

### 7) 🎨 Jeonju Crafts Exhibition Hall & Hanji Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** Largest dedicated museum of paper arts, with traditional paper-making workshops
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (Nov)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000**
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Pick up hand-made paper souvenirs.

### 8) 🏯 Jeonju Traditional Tea Ceremony House & Tea Museum — 🔁 Tue–Sun

## 🍴 Jeonju food — the bibimbap capital of Korea

### 9) 🥣 Jeonju Bibimbap Festival (전주 비빔밥 축제) — 👀 WATCH (Oct/Nov annual; 2026 dates TBA)
- **What:** Annual celebration of Korea's most famous single dish: bibimbap in hundreds of styles. Held in and around Hanok Village. Cooking contests, music performances, traditional bibimbap tastings, foraging masterclasses (hanwoo, légumes wild greens)
- **Dates:** 2026 TBA — recent editions ran late Oct/early Nov (2024 ran Nov 1–3 at Hanbyeokgung Plaza)
- **Where:** Jeonju Hanok Village center & surroundings
- **Price:** Free entry; cooking experiences paid
- **Official source:** [bibimbapfest.com](http://www.bibimbapfest.com)
- **Beginner notes:** The single best opportunity to Korea-eat bibimbap in context.

### 10) 🥩 Jeonju Original Bibimbap Alley (오늘이전주의집) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Historic flagship Hanok Village restaurant, on record for being the original *bibimbap* pioneers since the 1950s
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–21:00
- **Price:** ~₩8,000–12,000
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Drone-cult signature. **Try the dolsot (stone-pot) bibimbap** to make the rice crispy.

### 11) 🍜 Jeonju Hanok Tradition Restaurants — 🔁 daily (FREE walk-in)
- **What:** Cluster of 30+ traditional restaurants inside hanok serving royal court cuisine, *kongnamul gukbap* (bean sprout soup), and Korean vegetables
- **Hours:** Daily 11:00–21:00
- **Price:** ₩7,000–25,000
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Try the bean sprout soup for breakfast.

### 12) 🛒 Nambu Traditional Market (남부수산시장) — 🔁 daily (FREE walk-in)
- **What:** 100-year-old traditional market operating since the 1900s. Vendors serve street food tteokbokki, mandu, *ggobgi* sauce, fresh vegetables, plus hanbok fabric
- **Hours:** Stalls daily 09:00–22:00 (closed 2nd & 4th Sunday)
- **Price:** Free walk-in; snacks ~₩2,000–8,000
- **Official source:** [nambusijang.com](http://www.nambusijang.com)
- **Beginner notes:** Bean sprout soup alley for an authentic eat.

### 13) 🍵 Jeonju Hanji Tea & Specialty Cafe Tour — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Hanok Village & adjacent neighborhoods host 100+ themed cafes — paper art, hanji fabric, traditional teas
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00–22:00
- **Price:** Costa ~₩5,000–10,000
- **Official source:** [hanok.jeonju.go.kr](http://www.hanok.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Jeonju is a coffee-lover city — many outlets serve Korean teas, baking, and desserts in modern hanok interiors.

### 14) 🍫 Jeonju Chocopie & Chocolate Specialty Stores — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Jeonju is the home of the famous Korean *Chocopie* (the original brand HQ). Multiple boutique chocolate and bakery stores — including **Paula's Bean to Bar Chocolate**
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00–19:00
- **Price:** ₩8,000–30,000 per box
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Take a few boxes home.

### 15) 🍺 Makgeolli & Korean Liquor Bars (전주 전통주 가게) — 🔁 nightly
- **What:** Traditional Korean liquors — *makgeolli* (rice wine), *cheongju* (clear), and *soju* — brewed locally

- **Price:** ₩5,000–15,000
- **Official source:** [jjbi.go.kr](http://www.jjbi.go.kr)

## 🆕 More verified Jeonju events & activities (expansion set)

**Verified expansion set** — the full hanok-village inventory, UNESCO gastronomy and pansori heritage, craft museums, the night market, transit, and day trips to Maisan, Naejangsan and the Gochang dolmens.

### 137) 🏘️ Jeonju Hanok Village — the full 800-house district (전주한옥마을) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** **Korea's largest surviving hanok district** — over 800 traditional houses in Pungnam-dong and Gyo-dong, built from the 1930s as a Korean quarter in a Japanese-administered city
- **Hours:** Streets always open; individual houses roughly **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** Streets **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Bus 79/119 from Jeonju Station (20 min). **Go early — by 11:00 in autumn it is genuinely crowded.**

### 138) 👑 Gyeonggijeon Shrine & Royal Portrait Museum (경기전) — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** The 1410 shrine holding the **portrait of King Taejo**, founder of the Joseon dynasty, whose Yi clan originated in Jeonju — plus the adjoining royal portrait museum
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–19:00** (Nov: to 18:00)
- **Price:** Adult **₩3,000**; youth ₩2,000; child ₩1,000
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The bamboo grove behind the shrine is the quietest corner of the whole village.

### 139) ⛪ Jeondong Catholic Cathedral (전동성당) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A 1908–1914 Romanesque-Byzantine church built on the site where **Korea's first Catholic martyrs were executed in 1791** — National Treasure-grade architecture in red brick
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–17:00** (no entry during Mass)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr) · [Catholic Diocese of Jeonju](https://www.jcatholic.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Directly opposite Gyeonggijeon. **Quiet please — it is an active parish church.**

### 140) 🎓 Jeonjuhyanggyo Confucian School & Ginkgo Trees (전주향교) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The 1354 Confucian school, with **six ginkgo trees over 400 years old** whose late-autumn yellow is one of Jeonju's signature sights
- **Hours:** Daily **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Peak ginkgo colour is typically the first ten days of November** — perfect timing for this trip.

### 141) 🏯 Pungnammun Gate (풍남문) — Treasure No. 308 — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The last surviving gate of Jeonju's city wall, rebuilt in 1768 — a two-storey wooden gatehouse now marooned on a roundabout beside the market
- **Hours:** Always visible; interior closed
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr) · [Korea Heritage Service](https://www.khs.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Lit at night and photogenic. Nambu Market is immediately south of it.

### 142) 🌄 Omokdae & Imokdae Pavilions (오목대·이목대) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The hilltop pavilion where **General Yi Seong-gye celebrated his 1380 victory** before founding the Joseon dynasty — and the best free overlook of the hanok roofs
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Five minutes uphill from the main street. **The classic Jeonju photo of tiled roofs is taken here.**

### 143) 🎨 Jaman Mural Village (자만벽화마을) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** A hillside village of small painted houses above the hanok district, reached by a footbridge over the road from Omokdae
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Residential — keep noise down. Several small cafes with hanok-roof views.

### 144) 🍜 Nambu Market & Youth Mall (남부시장) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** A 200-year-old market beside Pungnammun, with a **Youth Mall of young-run shops on the second floor** and a famous kongnamul-gukbap alley
- **Hours:** Daily **07:00–20:00**; closed 1st & 3rd Sunday
- **Price:** **FREE** to browse; gukbap **₩8,000**
- **Official source:** [namsijang.co.kr](http://www.namsijang.co.kr) · [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)

- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Carry ₩50,000 in cash for market nights.** Global-card ATMs are in the CU and GS25 near Pungnammun.

### 210) 🧳 Jeonju as a base vs a day trip — 🔁 planning
- **What:** Jeonju rewards an overnight — the village empties after 18:00 and the makgeolli alleys and night market only happen in the evening
- **Hours:** N/A
- **Price:** N/A
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Recommendation: one night minimum.** Arrive early afternoon, do the village at dusk, eat at the night market.

### 211) 🕌 Jeonju Halal, Vegetarian & Temple Food Options — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Bibimbap is easily made vegetarian on request, several hanok-village restaurants serve Buddhist temple cuisine, and halal options exist near the universities
- **Hours:** Most **11:00–21:00**
- **Price:** Temple set from **₩15,000**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr) · [visitkorea.or.kr](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Ask for bibimbap **without gochujang-with-beef and without the raw beef** — most places manage it easily.

### 212) 🎎 Jeonju Traditional Culture Center & Nori Madang (전주전통문화관) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** A city-run centre in the hanok village running free traditional performances, tea ceremony and craft classes on a published monthly schedule
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** Many programmes **FREE**
- **Official source:** [jjcc.or.kr](http://www.jjcc.or.kr) · [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Check the monthly programme board on arrival** — the free weekend performances are genuinely good.

### 213) 🏵️ Jeonju Hyanggyo Autumn Rite & Cultural Events — 👀 WATCH
- **What:** The Confucian school and Gyeonggijeon hold seasonal rites and re-enactments; the hyanggyo also runs free hanbok and etiquette programmes
- **Hours:** By event, usually weekends
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Dates are announced locally with little notice — ask at the village information centre.

### 214) 📷 Jeonju Photo Spots — the definitive list — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** Omokdae rooftop panorama, the Jeonjucheon stepping stones, Jeonjuhyanggyo ginkgos, Jeondong Cathedral's facade and the Eunhaeng-ro alleys
- **Hours:** Best **07:30–09:00** and after dark
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Before 09:00 you can photograph the main street essentially empty.** After 11:00 you cannot.

### 215) 🧴 Jeonju Hanji Products & Souvenir Shopping — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Hanji lamps, notebooks, fans and dolls made locally, sold at the Crafts Exhibition Hall, the Hanji Museum shop and village stores
- **Hours:** Roughly **10:00–19:00**
- **Price:** **₩5,000–₩50,000**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Buy at the Crafts Exhibition Hall** — same makers, better prices and no tourist markup.

### 216) 🥘 Jeonju Hanjeongsik Full-Table Banquet (한정식) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** The Jeolla full-course meal — twenty to thirty side dishes arriving at once — the regional cuisine at its most complete
- **Hours:** Lunch **12:00–14:30**; dinner **17:30–21:00**
- **Price:** **₩25,000–₩60,000** per person
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr) · [UNESCO Creative Cities](https://www.unesco.org/en/creative-cities)
- **Beginner notes:** **Reserve a day ahead** and go hungry. Minimum two people at nearly every restaurant.

### 217) 🍢 Jeonju Piyanggi & Grilled Offal Alley (피순대) — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Jeonju's blood sausage — thicker and offal-rich compared to the national version — sold at Nambu Market and a dedicated alley nearby
- **Hours:** Roughly **10:00–22:00**
- **Price:** **₩10,000–₩14,000**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** *Sundae-guk* soup is the gentler entry point if the sausage plate looks daunting.

### 218) 🍵 Jeonju Tea Houses & Traditional Sweets — 🔁 daily
- **What:** Hanok tea houses in the village serving *omija*, *ssanghwa* medicinal tea and *hangwa* sweets — the warm-up stop on a cold November afternoon
- **Hours:** Roughly **10:00–21:00**
- **Price:** Tea **₩6,000–₩9,000**
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Ssanghwa-cha with pine nuts and jujube is the November drink** — it's a herbal tonic served hot.

### 219) 🐦 Jeonjucheon Stream Birdwatching & Ecology (전주천) — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The restored city stream now supports otters, herons and **overwintering ducks that arrive in November** — a signature Korean urban-restoration success
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [jeonju.go.kr](https://www.jeonju.go.kr) · [me.go.kr](https://www.me.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** The stretch below Hanbyeokdang is the richest. Early morning is best.

### 220) 🏃 Jeonju Running & Walking Routes — 🔁 daily (FREE)
- **What:** The Jeonjucheon and Samcheon stream paths give a flat, continuous, traffic-free loop of about 12 km through the city
- **Hours:** Always open
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [jeonju.go.kr](https://www.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Lit at night. **Cool, dry November mornings are ideal running conditions here.**

### 221) 🏛️ Jeonju City Hall Observatory & Free Viewpoints — 🔁 weekdays (FREE)
- **What:** Free public viewing floors at Jeonju City Hall and the Jeollabuk-do provincial building give city-wide views without paying for a tower
- **Hours:** Weekdays roughly **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [jeonju.go.kr](https://www.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Ask at the lobby desk. A quirky free alternative to Omokdae if the weather is clear.

### 222) 🎨 Jeonju Modern Art & Palbok Art Factory (팔복예술공장) — 🔁 Tue–Sun (FREE)
- **What:** A disused cassette-tape factory converted into a contemporary art centre with studios, exhibitions and a cafe
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00** (closed Mon)
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [palbokart.kr](http://www.palbokart.kr) · [jeonju.go.kr](https://www.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** In the industrial north of the city. The best contemporary counterweight to the hanok village.

### 223) 🎠 Jeonju Ohn Culture Center & Family Programmes — 🔁 Tue–Sun
- **What:** City-run family and children's culture facilities with indoor play, craft workshops and a children's library — useful on a cold or wet day
- **Hours:** Tue–Sun **10:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE** to ₩3,000
- **Official source:** [jeonju.go.kr](https://www.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** Book online for weekend slots; weekdays are usually walk-in.

### 224) 🌏 Jeonju International Community & Language Exchange — 🔁 weekly (FREE)
- **What:** The Jeonju Global Center runs free Korean classes, language exchanges and a foreigner help line in English
- **Hours:** Weekdays **09:00–18:00**
- **Price:** **FREE**
- **Official source:** [jeonju.go.kr](https://www.jeonju.go.kr) · [1330 Tourist Hotline](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Dial 1330 for 24-hour English tourist help anywhere in Korea** — free from any phone.

### 225) 🚑 Jeonju Emergency, Pharmacy & Medical Info — 🔁 24 h
- **What:** Jeonju has two large university hospitals with 24-hour emergency departments, and pharmacies signed 약국 across the centre
- **Hours:** Emergency **24 h**; pharmacies roughly **09:00–20:00**
- **Price:** Consultation from **₩20,000** for visitors
- **Official source:** [Jeonbuk National University Hospital](https://www.jbuh.co.kr) · [1339 Medical Hotline](https://www.kdca.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **119 for ambulance, 1339 for medical advice in English.** Travel insurance strongly recommended.

### 226) 📋 Jeonju One-Day and Two-Day Model Routes — 🔁 planning
- **What:** Day 1: Gyeonggijeon → Cathedral → Taejo-ro → Omokdae → Jaman → dusk lighting → makgeolli alley. Day 2: Nambu Market → Hanji Museum → Deokjin Park → Palbok Art Factory
- **Hours:** N/A
- **Price:** N/A
- **Official source:** [tour.jeonju.go.kr](https://tour.jeonju.go.kr)
- **Beginner notes:** **Add a third day for Naejangsan or Maisan** if your window allows — both are outstanding in November.
